## Purpose and Scope

This Procurement Policy ("Policy") establishes standards and procedures for the acquisition of goods, services, and software subscriptions by NovaTech Solutions. The Policy applies to all employees authorized to initiate or approve purchases on behalf of the Company. Its purpose is to ensure fiscal responsibility, maintain compliance with legal and contractual obligations, achieve cost efficiency through strategic vendor selection, and protect NovaTech's interests through appropriate contract terms.

All employees who engage vendors, sign contracts, or approve purchases must adhere to this Policy. Failure to comply may result in personal liability for unauthorized expenditures, disciplinary action, and/or denial of reimbursement.

## Section 1: Vendor Selection Process

### 1.1 General Principles

NovaTech is committed to selecting vendors and suppliers who offer the best combination of quality, price, service, and reliability. Vendor selection must be free from conflicts of interest, favoritism, or any factor unrelated to the legitimate business interests of the Company.

### 1.2 Vendor Qualification Requirements

All new vendors providing goods or services with an annual value exceeding $10,000 must be qualified before engagement. Qualification includes:

1. **Business Legitimacy:** Verified business registration and legal standing
2. **Financial Stability:** For contracts > $50,000, vendors must provide evidence of financial stability (e.g., recent financials or credit reference)
3. **Security Assessment:** Vendors who will access NovaTech systems, data, or customer data must complete the NovaTech Vendor Security Questionnaire (VSQ) and achieve a satisfactory rating from the Security team
4. **Insurance Verification:** Certificate of Insurance confirming compliance with NovaTech's insurance requirements (Section 7)
5. **Data Processing Agreement:** Required for any vendor who processes personal data (see Section 8)
6. **Conflict of Interest Disclosure:** Vendor must certify no prohibited relationship with NovaTech employees (see Section 9)

### 1.3 Preferred Vendor List

NovaTech maintains a Preferred Vendor List (PVL) managed by the Finance/Operations team. Purchasing from PVL vendors is strongly encouraged, as these vendors have been pre-qualified, have negotiated pricing agreements, and have appropriate contracts in place. The PVL is updated quarterly.

## Section 2: RFP Requirements

### 2.1 When RFPs Are Required

A formal Request for Proposal (RFP) process is required for all new vendor engagements where the anticipated annual or total contract value exceeds **$50,000**. RFPs are also required when renewing existing contracts where market alternatives should be evaluated.

### 2.2 RFP Process Steps

1. **Define Requirements:** The requesting department documents the business requirements, desired outcomes, evaluation criteria, and timeline.
2. **Identify Candidates:** A minimum of three (3) qualified vendors must be invited to respond to the RFP. The PVL should be consulted as the first source.
3. **Issue RFP:** The RFP document, prepared with Finance support, is sent to all candidate vendors simultaneously. RFPs include: scope of work, technical requirements, pricing model required, contract terms expected, evaluation criteria and weighting, submission deadline.
4. **Receive and Review Proposals:** Proposals are reviewed by a cross-functional evaluation team including the requesting department, Finance, IT (for technology purchases), and Legal (for contracts > $100K).
5. **Score and Select:** Vendors are scored against the pre-defined evaluation criteria. The evaluation scorecard is documented and retained.
6. **Negotiate:** Finance and Legal lead contract negotiation with the selected vendor.
7. **Award and Notify:** Award decision is communicated to all participating vendors.

### 2.3 RFP Exceptions (Sole-Source)

The RFP requirement may be waived if any of the following sole-source justification criteria are met:

- **Technical Compatibility:** Only one vendor's product is technically compatible with existing NovaTech systems
- **Sole Provider:** Only one qualified vendor exists in the market for the required goods/services
- **Emergency:** A genuine emergency exists requiring immediate procurement (must be documented and approved by CFO)
- **Continuation:** The purchase is a continuation or expansion of an existing contract where switching costs would outweigh competitive benefits (must be documented)
- **Proprietary Requirement:** The goods/services are proprietary to a specific vendor and no substitutes exist

All sole-source justifications must be documented in writing and approved by the VP Finance (for contracts < $100K) or CFO (for contracts > $100K). Sole-source approvals are logged in the Procurement register.

## Section 4: Contract Terms Standards

### 4.1 Standard Payment Terms

NovaTech's standard payment terms are **Net-30** from the date of invoice. All vendor agreements should include Net-30 payment terms. Terms of Net-15 or shorter may only be accepted for small vendors (< $10K annual) with Finance approval. Early payment discounts (e.g., 2/10 Net-30) require Finance evaluation before acceptance.

### 4.2 Liability Caps

All vendor contracts must include a limitation of liability provision. NovaTech's standard position:
- **Vendor's liability cap:** Greater of $500,000 or 12 months of fees paid to vendor
- **NovaTech's liability cap:** Greater of $500,000 or 12 months of fees paid to vendor
- **Exceptions to caps:** Gross negligence, willful misconduct, indemnification for IP infringement or data breach are typically excluded from caps

### 4.3 Termination Rights

All vendor agreements must include:
- Termination for convenience with 30–90 days written notice (depending on contract duration)
- Termination for cause with 15-day cure period
- Automatic termination upon insolvency or bankruptcy

### 4.4 Intellectual Property

NovaTech must ensure that any work product created by vendors on NovaTech's behalf is assigned to NovaTech as "work made for hire" or through an explicit IP assignment clause. This is particularly important for software development, design work, and content creation. Legal must review all contracts involving IP creation.

### 4.5 Data Processing Requirements

Any vendor who will have access to NovaTech's customer data, employee data, or other personal data must execute a Data Processing Addendum (DPA) that complies with GDPR (for EU data subjects) and CCPA (for California residents). The standard NovaTech DPA is maintained by the Legal team and must be used without material modification unless Legal approves.

### 4.6 Confidentiality

All vendor agreements must include mutual confidentiality obligations. Vendors must protect NovaTech's confidential information with at least the same degree of care they use for their own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care.

## Section 5: Purchase Order Process

### 5.1 When Purchase Orders Are Required

Purchase Orders (POs) are required for all purchases exceeding $2,500. POs are issued through the Finance team using the Company's accounting system (QuickBooks Enterprise).

### 5.2 PO Lifecycle

1. **PO Request:** Submitted by department via the PO request form in Notion
2. **Budget Verification:** Finance verifies budget availability
3. **Approval:** Routed for appropriate approval per Section 3.1
4. **PO Issuance:** Finance issues PO number to vendor and department
5. **Goods/Services Receipt:** Department confirms receipt or completion
6. **Invoice Matching:** AP team matches vendor invoice to PO (3-way match for goods: PO + receipt + invoice)
7. **Payment Processing:** Payment processed per agreed terms

### 5.3 Blanket Purchase Orders

Recurring purchases from approved vendors may be covered by a Blanket PO valid for one fiscal year. Blanket POs are reviewed and renewed annually during the budget process.

## Section 6: Software and SaaS Procurement

### 6.1 Shadow IT Prevention

All SaaS tools and software subscriptions — regardless of cost — must be reviewed and approved by the IT team before procurement. This prevents shadow IT, ensures security compliance, and avoids duplicate spend on overlapping tools.

### 6.2 SaaS Approval Checklist

Before procuring any SaaS product, the following must be confirmed:
- [ ] IT security review completed (for tools accessing NovaTech data)
- [ ] No duplicate functionality exists in currently licensed tools
- [ ] Data residency requirements are met
- [ ] SOC 2 report (or equivalent) reviewed and accepted by Security team
- [ ] Single Sign-On (SSO) support confirmed (Okta integration)
- [ ] Renewal date and owner documented in Vendor & SaaS Spend register

### 6.3 Negotiation of Multi-Year and Volume Discounts

For SaaS vendors with expected annual spend > $25,000, Finance should negotiate multi-year or volume discount terms. Target: 15–20% discount for 2-year commits; 25%+ for 3-year commits.

## Section 7: Insurance Requirements

Vendors providing services to NovaTech (as opposed to software-only) must maintain the following minimum insurance coverage:

| Insurance Type | Minimum Coverage |
|---|---|
| Commercial General Liability | $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate |
| Professional Liability (E&O) | $2,000,000 per occurrence |
| Cyber Liability | $2,000,000 per occurrence (required for data-handling vendors) |
| Workers' Compensation | As required by applicable law |
| Umbrella/Excess Liability | $5,000,000 (for contracts > $500K) |

NovaTech must be named as an Additional Insured on the vendor's General Liability policy.

## Section 8: Conflict of Interest Policy

### 8.1 Employee Disclosure Requirements

Employees must disclose to their manager and the Finance team any personal relationship (family, romantic, financial interest) with a vendor, potential vendor, or vendor employee that could influence their objectivity in the procurement process.

### 8.2 Gifts and Hospitality

NovaTech employees may not accept gifts from vendors with a value exceeding $50. Business meals paid by vendors in the context of a legitimate business meeting are permissible up to $150 per person. Tickets to events, travel, or other entertainment paid by vendors exceeding $100 in value must be reported to the Finance team.

### 8.3 Vendor Relationships with Former Employees

Procurement decisions involving a vendor founded by or employing a former NovaTech employee (within the past 2 years) require CFO approval, regardless of contract value.
