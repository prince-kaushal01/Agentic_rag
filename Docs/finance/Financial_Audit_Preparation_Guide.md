## Section 3: Revenue Recognition Policies (ASC 606 Summary)

### 3.1 Five-Step Revenue Model Application

NovaTech applies the five-step revenue recognition model under ASC 606 as follows:

**Step 1 — Identify the contract:** NovaTech enters into contracts with customers through executed Order Forms, Enterprise Subscription Agreements, or self-service online sign-up (for Starter and Professional tiers). A contract exists when both parties have approved it and both have rights and obligations.

**Step 2 — Identify performance obligations:** NovaTech identifies the following distinct performance obligations in its customer contracts:
- Software as a Service subscription (primary obligation)
- Implementation and onboarding services (distinct in most cases; bundled in some enterprise contracts)
- Professional services / custom development (separate SOW)
- Training services (separate SOW or delivered concurrently with implementation)

**Step 3 — Determine the transaction price:** Transaction price is the fixed subscription fee as stated in the Order Form, plus any variable consideration (usage-based charges, overage fees). Variable consideration is estimated using the most likely amount method.

**Step 4 — Allocate the transaction price:** In contracts with multiple performance obligations, the transaction price is allocated based on Standalone Selling Prices (SSP). SSP for the SaaS subscription is determined by reference to observable prices in standalone contracts. SSP for professional services is the list rate card ($250/hour standard; $350/hour enterprise).

**Step 5 — Recognize revenue:** Subscription revenue is recognized ratably over the subscription term (daily straight-line). Implementation fees are recognized over the expected customer life (currently estimated at 36 months). Professional services are recognized as services are rendered (percentage of completion based on hours).

### 3.2 Deferred Revenue

Subscription fees billed in advance of the service period are recorded as deferred revenue (contract liability) on the balance sheet and recognized ratably. As of December 31, 2025, total deferred revenue was $3,840,000, all of which is expected to be recognized within the next 12 months.

### 3.3 Contract Assets

Contract assets (unbilled receivables) arise when revenue is recognized before the customer is invoiced. This occurs primarily in professional services engagements billed monthly in arrears. As of December 31, 2025, contract assets were $185,000.

## Section 4: Key Judgments and Estimates

The following areas require significant judgment and are subject to auditor scrutiny:

| Judgment Area | Basis | Key Risk |
|---|---|---|
| SSP determination | Observable pricing + residual approach | Changes in pricing strategy may affect allocation |
| Variable consideration estimate | Most likely amount; constrained | Customer usage variability |
| Capitalized software costs | Developer time-tracking; stage determination | Misclassification between R&D expense and capitalized cost |
| Bad debt allowance | Historical collection rates + specific reserves | New enterprise customers with limited history |
| SBC expense (Black-Scholes) | Volatility, risk-free rate, expected term | Estimation uncertainty inherent in private company valuation |
| 409A valuation | Third-party valuation (Carta); most recent January 2026 | Timing between grants and valuations |
| Income tax valuation allowance | Full valuation allowance on all DTAs | Judgment on likelihood of future taxable income |

## Section 5: Stock-Based Compensation Accounting

NovaTech grants Incentive Stock Options (ISOs) to employees and Non-Qualified Stock Options (NSOs) to contractors and board members under the 2020 Equity Incentive Plan.

**Accounting Treatment:**
- Fair value measured at grant date using Black-Scholes model
- Recognized over the requisite service period (4-year vesting with 1-year cliff, quarterly vesting thereafter)
- Forfeitures recognized as they occur (not estimated in advance per ASC 718)

**Key Assumptions (2025 Grants):**
- Risk-free interest rate: 4.25% – 4.75% (based on U.S. Treasury rates at grant dates)
- Expected term: 6.25 years (simplified method — midpoint of vesting and contractual term)
- Expected volatility: 65% – 72% (based on comparable public company basket)
- Dividend yield: 0% (Company does not pay dividends)
- FY2025 SBC expense: $1,420,000

## Section 8: Internal Controls Key Areas

NovaTech maintains the following key internal controls, which are subject to walkthrough and testing during the interim audit:

| Control Area | Control Description | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue recognition | Revenue schedule reviewed and approved monthly | Monthly | Controller |
| Cash reconciliation | Bank reconciliations prepared and reviewed | Monthly | Finance Manager |
| AP invoice approval | 3-way match for vendor invoices > $5,000 | Per transaction | Finance Analyst |
| Payroll review | Payroll register reviewed by VP Finance prior to processing | Bi-weekly | VP Finance |
| Expense report approval | Expense reports reviewed by manager and Finance | Per submission | Finance Manager |
| Access controls | User access review for Salesforce, AWS, banking portals | Quarterly | IT/Security |
| Journal entry review | All manual JEs require second-approver signature | Per transaction | Controller |
| Segregation of duties | No single employee can initiate and approve payment | Continuous | CFO |

## Section 9: Post-Audit Deliverables and Management Letter

Following completion of the audit, Deloitte will issue:

1. **Auditor's Report** — Unqualified opinion on financial statements (target outcome)
2. **Management Letter** — Findings related to internal control deficiencies (if any)
3. **Required Communications to Audit Committee** — Significant accounting policies, adjustments, disagreements (none expected)

NovaTech management is responsible for preparing a written response to any management letter findings within 30 days of receipt. Responses include the remediation action, responsible owner, and target completion date. The Audit Committee reviews all management letter findings at the next Board meeting.
