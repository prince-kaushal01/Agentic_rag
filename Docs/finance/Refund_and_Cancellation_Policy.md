## Policy Overview

This Refund and Cancellation Policy ("Policy") governs all refund, credit, and cancellation requests submitted by customers of NovaTech Solutions, Inc. ("NovaTech," "Company," "we," "us"). This Policy applies to all subscription tiers — Starter, Professional, Business, and Enterprise — and is incorporated by reference into all customer agreements, order forms, and enterprise contracts.

NovaTech Solutions is committed to delivering high-quality enterprise software that meets contracted performance standards. We recognize that circumstances may arise in which a customer requires a refund or wishes to cancel service. This Policy sets forth the conditions, procedures, timelines, and limitations applicable to all such requests.

All monetary values referenced herein are denominated in United States Dollars (USD) unless otherwise specified in the applicable customer agreement.

---

## Section 1: Definitions

For purposes of this Policy, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

**1.1 "Invoice Date"** means the date appearing on the NovaTech invoice as issued to the customer, which serves as the baseline for calculating refund eligibility windows.

**1.2 "Subscription Term"** means the period of service purchased by the customer as set forth in the applicable Order Form or Enterprise Contract, whether monthly or annual.

**1.3 "Annual Contract"** means any subscription agreement with a term of twelve (12) months or greater, typically invoiced annually in advance.

**1.4 "Enterprise Customer"** means any customer operating under a fully executed Enterprise Subscription Agreement with NovaTech, typically with an annual contract value (ACV) exceeding $50,000.

**1.5 "Critical Feature"** means any core platform feature explicitly identified in the customer's contracted Scope of Services or Order Form, the unavailability of which would materially impair the customer's intended use of the NovaTech platform.

**1.6 "SLA Breach"** means a failure by NovaTech to meet the uptime commitments set forth in the applicable Service Level Agreement, as documented and verified through NovaTech's monitoring infrastructure.

**1.7 "Prorated Refund"** means a partial refund calculated on a daily or monthly basis for the unused portion of a prepaid subscription period.

**1.8 "Implementation Fees"** means one-time charges for professional services associated with onboarding, configuration, data migration, or system integration, as itemized on the Order Form.

**1.9 "Business Day"** means Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays and NovaTech company holidays as published on the internal company calendar.

**1.10 "Finance Team"** means NovaTech's Finance Department, currently led by Margaret Liu, VP Finance, reachable at finance@novatech.io.

## Section 2: Standard Refund Window — All Subscription Tiers

**2.1 General Refund Eligibility**

NovaTech offers a standard refund window of **thirty (30) calendar days from the invoice date** for all subscription tiers (Starter, Professional, Business, and Enterprise). Customers who are dissatisfied with the NovaTech platform for any reason may submit a refund request within this window.

**2.2 Starter and Professional Tiers**

Customers on Starter (up to 10 users) and Professional (up to 50 users) tiers are eligible for a full refund of subscription fees paid, provided the refund request is submitted within 30 days of the invoice date. These tiers are monthly billing by default; annual billing customers on these tiers are eligible for a full refund within the 30-day window, and a prorated refund for months 2-12 may be considered on a case-by-case basis at the discretion of the VP Finance.

**2.3 Business Tier**

Business tier customers (51–200 users) are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of the invoice date. For annual Business contracts, refund requests submitted after the 30-day window but before the 6-month mark of the contract term may be eligible for a prorated refund equal to the value of unused complete months, less a 15% early cancellation fee. Requests after the 6-month mark are not eligible for monetary refunds but may be converted to account credits.

**2.4 Monthly Subscriptions**

For monthly subscription customers, refunds are limited to the most recent billing cycle. Prior months are non-refundable. Monthly subscribers may cancel at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle, and no partial-month refunds are issued.

**2.5 Activation Requirement**

The 30-day refund window begins on the invoice date, not the activation date. Customers are encouraged to activate and evaluate the platform promptly after invoicing to make use of the full refund window.

## Section 3: Non-Refundable Items and Fees

The following fees and charges are explicitly **non-refundable** under all circumstances, regardless of the reason for cancellation or the subscription tier:

**3.1 Implementation and Onboarding Fees**
All professional services fees for implementation, system configuration, onboarding sessions, and data migration are non-refundable once work has commenced. These fees represent services rendered and costs incurred by NovaTech's Professional Services team.

**3.2 Custom Development Fees**
Any fees for custom feature development, custom integrations, API extensions, or bespoke reporting modules are non-refundable once a Statement of Work (SOW) has been signed and work has begun, regardless of project completion status.

**3.3 Training Services**
Fees for live instructor-led training sessions, custom training curriculum development, and training materials are non-refundable once sessions have been scheduled and/or delivered.

**3.4 Third-Party Pass-Through Costs**
Any costs passed through to the customer for third-party services (e.g., SMS/telephony credits, data enrichment API calls, third-party marketplace integrations) are non-refundable.

**3.5 Add-On Modules Already Consumed**
Consumption-based add-ons (e.g., AI credits, advanced analytics queries, API call overage charges) that have already been utilized are non-refundable.

**3.6 Expedited Support Retainers**
Premium support retainer fees, once activated, are non-refundable for the current quarter.

## Section 4: Enterprise Refund Policy

**4.1 Overview of Enterprise Refund Eligibility**

Enterprise customers operate under individualized Enterprise Subscription Agreements that may include specific SLA commitments, custom feature sets, and dedicated support structures. Accordingly, the refund conditions for Enterprise customers include both the standard 30-day window and additional grounds for refund tied to contractual performance failures.

**Enterprise customers may request a refund within thirty (30) days of the applicable invoice date if any of the following conditions are met:**

**(a) Critical Feature Unavailability Exceeding 72 Hours**

If a Critical Feature — as defined in Section 1.5 and as specifically enumerated in the customer's Order Form or Scope of Services document — is unavailable for a continuous period exceeding seventy-two (72) hours due to factors within NovaTech's control, the Enterprise customer may submit a refund request. The 72-hour period begins from the time NovaTech acknowledges the incident via the customer's support ticket. Documentation of the outage must be included in the refund request. The refund amount for qualifying Critical Feature unavailability shall be calculated as follows:

- Unavailability 72–120 hours: 10% of the monthly invoice value
- Unavailability 120–168 hours (5–7 days): 25% of the monthly invoice value
- Unavailability exceeding 168 hours (7 days): 50% of the monthly invoice value, or full monthly invoice, at VP Finance discretion

**(b) SLA Breach of the 99.9% Uptime Guarantee**

Enterprise customers who are subject to a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA (as specified in their Enterprise Contract) are entitled to a refund credit if NovaTech fails to meet this commitment in a given calendar month. The uptime is measured by NovaTech's monitoring systems and corroborated by third-party status monitoring (currently StatusPage.io). The SLA credit structure is:

| Monthly Uptime Achieved | Service Credit (of Monthly Fee) |
|-------------------------|----------------------------------|
| 99.0% – 99.8%           | 10%                              |
| 95.0% – 98.9%           | 25%                              |
| 90.0% – 94.9%           | 50%                              |
| Below 90.0%             | 100%                             |

SLA credits are issued as account credits by default. Customers may request cash refunds for SLA credits exceeding $5,000 in a calendar quarter; such requests are subject to VP Finance approval.

**(c) Product Materially Fails to Meet Contracted Specifications**

If the NovaTech platform materially fails to perform in accordance with the documented product specifications as set forth in the Enterprise Contract's Exhibit A (Product Specifications), the Enterprise customer may submit a refund request. "Material failure" means a documented, reproducible failure of core platform functionality — not a configuration issue, user error, or limitation of a non-contracted feature. Refund amounts for material specification failures are determined by the Finance team in consultation with the VP of Engineering and Account Management, based on the severity and scope of the failure.

**4.2 Annual Contract Prorated Refunds**

For Enterprise customers on annual contracts, refunds approved after the 30-day window (but based on qualifying conditions under Section 4.1) are calculated on a prorated monthly basis. The prorated refund equals the number of unused complete months remaining in the contract term multiplied by the monthly equivalent contract value, minus any Implementation Fees paid (which remain non-refundable per Section 3).

**Formula:** Prorated Refund = (Remaining Full Months / 12) × Annual Contract Value — Implementation Fees

**Example:** An Enterprise customer with an annual contract of $240,000, 8 months remaining, and $15,000 in implementation fees would receive: (8/12 × $240,000) − $15,000 = $145,000.

**4.3 Upgrade and Downgrade Credits**

Enterprise customers who upgrade or downgrade their subscription tier mid-term are not entitled to refunds for the prior tier's fees. Upgrade credits may be issued at NovaTech's discretion. Tier changes take effect at the beginning of the next billing cycle.

## Section 5: Cancellation Policy

**5.1 Month-to-Month Cancellations**

Customers on month-to-month subscriptions may cancel at any time by submitting a cancellation request through the customer portal or by contacting their Customer Success Manager. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. No further charges will be assessed after the cancellation effective date.

**5.2 Annual Contract Cancellations**

Customers with annual contracts who wish to cancel prior to the end of their contracted term are subject to an early termination fee (ETF) equal to fifty percent (50%) of the remaining contract value, unless the cancellation is due to a qualifying SLA breach or material specification failure (see Section 4). Notice must be submitted in writing to both the assigned Account Executive and the Finance team.

**5.3 Enterprise Contract Cancellations**

Enterprise customers must provide ninety (90) days written notice prior to contract expiration if they do not intend to renew. Failure to provide timely notice may result in automatic renewal at the then-current pricing as set forth in the Enterprise Contract. Early termination of Enterprise contracts is governed by the termination provisions of the applicable Enterprise Subscription Agreement.

**5.4 Data Retention After Cancellation**

Upon cancellation, customer data is retained in a read-only, downloadable state for 60 days. After 60 days, data is permanently deleted from NovaTech systems unless a data export has been completed. Enterprise customers may negotiate extended data retention periods in their contracts.

## Section 6: Refund Request Process

**6.1 How to Submit a Refund Request**

All refund requests must be submitted through one of the following channels:

- **Customer Support Portal:** support.novatech.io — Submit a ticket under category "Billing & Refunds"
- **Email:** billing@novatech.io — Include subject line: "Refund Request — [Company Name] — Invoice #[XXXXX]"
- **Enterprise Customers:** Contact your assigned Customer Success Manager directly, who will coordinate with the Finance team

**6.2 Required Information**

Every refund request must include the following information to be processed:

1. Company name and account ID
2. Invoice number and invoice date
3. Amount requested for refund
4. Reason for refund request (with supporting documentation if applicable)
5. For SLA breach claims: incident ticket numbers, downtime logs, and monitoring screenshots
6. For material specification failure claims: detailed description, reproducible steps, and error documentation
7. Preferred refund method (original payment method or account credit)
8. Authorized signatory name and title

**6.3 Review and Approval Timeline**

| Request Type | Review Period | Approval Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Standard refund ≤ $1,000 | 3 Business Days | Finance Analyst |
| Standard refund $1,001–$10,000 | 5 Business Days | Finance Manager |
| Standard refund > $10,000 | 5 Business Days | VP Finance (Margaret Liu) |
| Enterprise SLA credit | 7 Business Days | Finance Manager + VP Customer Success |
| Enterprise prorated refund | 10 Business Days | VP Finance + CEO approval if > $50K |
| Disputed refund | 15 Business Days | VP Finance + Legal review |

**6.4 Acknowledgment and Communication**

NovaTech will acknowledge all refund requests within one (1) Business Day of receipt. The Finance team will communicate the status of the review within the applicable review period. If additional information is required, the Finance team will reach out to the customer within 3 Business Days of the initial request.

**6.5 Refund Processing Time**

Once a refund is approved:
- Credit card refunds: 5–10 business days to appear on statement
- ACH/wire transfer refunds: 3–5 business days
- Account credit: Applied within 1 Business Day of approval
- Check issuance: 10–15 business days

## Section 7: Enterprise-Specific Conditions and Cross-Reference

**7.1 Applicability of Enterprise Contract Terms**

For Enterprise customers, refund eligibility is always assessed in conjunction with the specific terms of the applicable Enterprise Subscription Agreement. In the event of a conflict between this Policy and the Enterprise Contract, the Enterprise Contract shall govern. This Policy is intended to supplement, not supersede, the contractual refund provisions agreed upon in Enterprise contracts.

**7.2 Current Payment Requirement**

Refunds will not be issued to Enterprise customers who are not current on their payment obligations. Any outstanding invoices beyond net-30 payment terms must be resolved before a refund request will be processed. NovaTech reserves the right to offset any approved refund amount against outstanding balances owed by the customer.

**7.3 Refunds Not Available During Active Legal Disputes**

If a customer has initiated, or is subject to, an active legal dispute, arbitration proceeding, or formal complaint with NovaTech, refund requests related to the subject matter of that dispute will be held in abeyance until the dispute is resolved. This does not preclude SLA service credits that accrue automatically under the terms of an Enterprise Contract.

**7.4 Refund Restrictions for Customers in Collections**

Accounts that have been referred to a collections agency or that have had payment disputes initiated through their financial institution (e.g., chargebacks) are not eligible for standard refund processing. Such matters are handled exclusively through the Finance and Legal teams in coordination with the collections process.

**7.5 Custom Refund Provisions**

Enterprise customers may negotiate custom refund terms as part of their Enterprise Subscription Agreement. Such custom terms, once executed, supersede the corresponding provisions of this Policy and are recorded in the customer's contract file maintained by the Legal team.

## Section 8: Credit vs. Cash Refund Options

**8.1 Default Refund Method**

Unless otherwise specified by the customer, approved refunds are issued via the same payment method used for the original transaction:
- Credit card transactions → refunded to the same credit card
- ACH/bank transfer transactions → refunded to the originating bank account
- Wire transfer transactions → refunded via wire to the originating account

**8.2 Account Credit Option**

Customers may elect to receive an approved refund as an account credit (balance credit applied to the NovaTech account) rather than a cash refund. Account credits do not expire for the duration of the customer's active subscription and may be applied to any future invoice. Account credits are non-transferable and have no cash value if the customer terminates service.

**8.3 Cash Refund vs. Credit — Enterprise Consideration**

For Enterprise customers, the choice between cash refund and account credit must be documented in writing and submitted with the refund request. Approved SLA service credits are always issued as account credits unless the customer formally requests a cash refund per Section 4.1(b).

**8.4 Partial Credits for Mid-Cycle Downgrade**

When a customer downgrades their subscription tier mid-cycle, the difference in plan price may be issued as a prorated account credit for the remainder of the billing cycle, at NovaTech's discretion.

## Section 9: Dispute Resolution

**9.1 Internal Escalation**

Customers who disagree with a refund decision may escalate the matter internally at NovaTech by following this escalation path:

1. **Level 1:** Finance Analyst (initial reviewer) → Response within 3 Business Days
2. **Level 2:** Finance Manager (Sarah Okonkwo, finance-manager@novatech.io) → Response within 5 Business Days
3. **Level 3:** VP Finance (Margaret Liu, margaret.liu@novatech.io) → Final internal review, response within 7 Business Days

**9.2 Formal Dispute Submission**

If internal escalation does not resolve the matter to the customer's satisfaction, the customer may submit a formal written dispute to legal@novatech.io. The NovaTech Legal team will acknowledge the dispute within 2 Business Days and schedule a resolution call within 10 Business Days.

**9.3 Mediation**

If a dispute remains unresolved after internal escalation, either party may request non-binding mediation administered by JAMS (Judicial Arbitration and Mediation Services) in San Francisco, California. Mediation costs are shared equally by both parties.

**9.4 Arbitration**

Disputes that cannot be resolved through mediation shall be submitted to binding arbitration in accordance with the JAMS Comprehensive Arbitration Rules and Procedures. The arbitration shall be conducted in San Francisco, California, and the arbitrator's decision shall be final and binding. This provision does not limit either party's right to seek injunctive relief in a court of competent jurisdiction.

**9.5 Chargeback Policy**

NovaTech strongly encourages customers to resolve billing disputes directly with our Finance team before initiating a chargeback with their financial institution. Chargebacks initiated without prior communication with NovaTech may result in account suspension, and NovaTech reserves the right to dispute chargebacks with documentary evidence. Customers who initiate chargebacks are not eligible for any other refund or credit during the pendency of the chargeback.
