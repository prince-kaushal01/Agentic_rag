## Purpose and Scope

This Expense and Travel Policy ("Policy") establishes the standards and procedures for NovaTech Solutions employees incurring business-related expenses on behalf of the Company. The Policy applies to all full-time employees, part-time employees, and contractors who are authorized to incur expenses on NovaTech's behalf.

NovaTech Solutions is committed to providing employees with the resources necessary to conduct business effectively while maintaining fiscal responsibility. This Policy is designed to ensure that Company funds are spent wisely, that employees are reimbursed fairly and promptly, and that NovaTech remains compliant with applicable tax and accounting regulations.

Violations of this Policy may result in delayed reimbursement, denial of reimbursement, disciplinary action, or, in cases of intentional misuse, termination and/or legal action.

## Section 1: Pre-Approval Requirements

### 1.1 Pre-Approval Thresholds

Pre-approval is required for expenses meeting the following thresholds, **before** the expense is incurred:

| Expense Amount | Approval Required | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Up to $499.99 | No pre-approval required | Manager reviews at submission |
| $500.00 – $1,999.99 | Direct Manager approval | Immediate supervisor |
| $2,000.00 – $4,999.99 | Department Director approval | Director-level or above |
| $5,000.00 – $24,999.99 | VP-level approval | VP of relevant department |
| $25,000.00 and above | CFO approval | CFO (currently Priya Nair) |

### 1.2 Pre-Approval Process

Pre-approval must be obtained via email or the NovaTech expense management system (Expensify). Verbal approvals are not accepted. The employee must retain the written approval and attach it to the expense report submission. Pre-approval does not guarantee reimbursement if the expense does not comply with other provisions of this Policy.

### 1.3 Travel Pre-Approval — Specific Requirements

All domestic travel requiring airfare must be pre-approved by the employee's direct manager regardless of cost. International travel requires VP-level approval for any destination and duration. Travel to countries on the U.S. State Department Travel Advisory Level 3 or higher requires CFO approval.

### 1.4 Emergency Expenses

In cases where a legitimate business expense must be incurred urgently without time for pre-approval (e.g., a critical client situation requiring an unplanned flight), the employee should notify their manager by phone or text as soon as practicable and submit a written after-the-fact approval request within 24 hours. Retroactive approval is at manager discretion.

## Section 2: Reimbursable Expenses

### 2.1 Transportation

**Air Travel**
- **Flights under 4 hours:** Economy class only. No exceptions, regardless of seniority level, for flights with a scheduled flight time under 4 hours.
- **Flights over 4 hours (scheduled flight time):** Business class is permitted for employees at Director level and above. All other employees are reimbursed for economy class; any upgrade cost is the employee's personal expense.
- **Advance Booking:** Airfare should be booked at least 14 days in advance where possible to secure favorable rates. Flights booked within 7 days of travel require manager justification in the expense report.
- **Preferred Booking Channel:** Employees are encouraged to book through the Company's approved travel portal (TripActions). Bookings outside TripActions require the employee to certify that the rate was the lowest available.
- **Baggage:** One checked bag is reimbursable for trips of 3+ nights. Excess or oversized baggage fees are not reimbursable.

**Ground Transportation**
- Taxis, rideshare (Uber, Lyft): Reimbursable for business travel. Receipts required for fares over $15.
- Rental cars: Permitted when necessary for client visits or locations not served by public transit. Book economy or compact class; full-size and above require manager justification. NovaTech's corporate CDW insurance covers rental vehicles — employees should decline the rental company's optional collision coverage.
- Personal vehicle mileage: Reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate (currently $0.70/mile for 2026). Mileage to and from an employee's home office is not reimbursable for employees with a designated office location.
- Train, subway, bus: Reimbursable with receipt.
- Airport parking: Reimbursable at economy/off-site parking rates. Valet parking at airports is not reimbursable unless no other option exists.

### 2.2 Lodging

| Location | Nightly Rate Cap | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| U.S. – Standard Markets | $200/night | Excluding taxes |
| U.S. – High-Cost Markets (NYC, Boston, DC, Chicago, LA, Seattle) | $275/night | Excluding taxes |
| International – Standard | $250/night | Excluding taxes |
| International – High-Cost (London, Tokyo, Zurich, Singapore) | $350/night | Excluding taxes |

- Hotel room rates above the caps require manager approval and documentation that lower-cost alternatives were unavailable.
- Hotels should be booked through TripActions where possible.
- Incidental charges (minibar, movies, personal calls, room service beyond meals cap) are not reimbursable.
- Loyalty points earned during business travel belong to the employee.
- Extended stays (7+ nights): Employees are encouraged to use corporate apartment services or extended-stay hotels, which often provide lower effective nightly rates.

### 2.3 Meals and Entertainment

**Daily Meal Per Diem**

| Meal | Domestic (U.S.) | International |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | $20 | $25 |
| Lunch | $25 | $30 |
| Dinner | $30 | $40 |
| **Daily Total** | **$75** | **$95** |

- Per diems are the maximum reimbursable amounts, not guaranteed allowances. Employees are expected to exercise reasonable judgment.
- Receipts are required for all individual meal charges over $25.
- Alcohol: Limited reimbursement for client entertainment (one or two drinks per meal). Excessive alcohol charges are not reimbursable. Bar-only charges with no accompanying meal are not reimbursable.
- Team meals and client entertainment: Business purpose and names of all attendees must be documented. Meals exceeding $75 per person for client entertainment require manager approval.

**Client Entertainment**
- Client entertainment (dinners, events) is reimbursable with manager pre-approval.
- Documentation required: names of all attendees, company affiliations, and business purpose.
- Sporting events, concerts, or other entertainment tickets: Limit of $150 per person per event without VP approval.
- Golf: Reimbursable as client entertainment with manager approval; green fees up to $100 per person.

### 2.4 Technology and Equipment

- Home office equipment purchased for business use requires manager and IT approval before purchase. Approved equipment is tracked as Company asset.
- Software subscriptions: Must be approved by IT department. Employees should not purchase individual SaaS subscriptions that may be available through Company-wide licensing.
- Mobile phone: NovaTech provides a $75/month mobile phone stipend for employees in sales, customer success, and engineering roles. Usage above personal plan cost is reimbursable with receipts.
- Internet: A $50/month home internet stipend is provided to remote-designated employees.

### 2.5 Professional Development

- Conferences and seminars: Pre-approval required; registration fees, travel, and lodging reimbursable per this Policy.
- Books and online courses: Up to $500/year reimbursable with manager approval. Coursera, LinkedIn Learning, Pluralsight subscriptions may be approved by HR.
- Professional certifications: Exam fees and prep materials reimbursable up to $1,500/year with manager and HR approval.

## Section 3: Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following expenses are explicitly **not reimbursable** under any circumstances:

- Personal entertainment, leisure activities, or tourism
- Clothing or personal grooming (including haircuts, dry cleaning for personal wardrobe)
- Gym memberships or personal fitness (separate wellness benefit exists via HR)
- Fines, parking tickets, traffic violations, or tolls for personal detours
- Childcare or pet care during business travel
- Lost or stolen personal property
- Personal travel or leisure extensions of business trips (the business portion is reimbursable; personal days are not)
- First-class airfare (except when upgraded using personal loyalty points at no incremental cost to NovaTech)
- Luxury vehicle rentals (premium, full-size SUV, or above without justification)
- Political donations or contributions
- Gifts to employees exceeding $25 per person (HR manages employee gift programs)
- Personal subscriptions or memberships
- Alcohol consumed outside of client entertainment context
- Tips exceeding 20% of the pre-tax bill
- Expenses for family members or non-NovaTech guests (unless explicitly approved as client entertainment)

## Section 4: Receipt Requirements

### 4.1 Receipt Thresholds

| Transaction Amount | Receipt Required? |
|---|---|
| Under $10 | No (except for per diem categories; all lodging) |
| $10 – $24.99 | Recommended |
| $25 and above | Required |

### 4.2 Acceptable Receipts

- Original itemized receipts (digital or physical scans)
- Email receipts from vendors
- Credit card statements are **not** acceptable as the sole receipt (they may supplement but not replace itemized receipts)
- For meals, the itemized receipt showing food/drink breakdown is required (not just the credit card slip showing total)

### 4.3 Lost Receipts

If a receipt is genuinely lost, the employee must complete a "Missing Receipt Declaration" form in Expensify, signed by their manager. Missing receipt declarations are limited to 3 per quarter per employee; exceeding this limit may result in denial of the claim.

## Section 5: Expense Report Submission

### 5.1 Submission Timeline

- **Standard submission:** All expense reports must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the expense being incurred.
- **Quarter-end:** All expenses from a fiscal quarter must be submitted by the 10th calendar day of the following month to be captured in the correct accounting period.
- **Year-end:** All 2026 expenses must be submitted by January 10, 2027.
- **Late submissions:** Expense reports submitted more than 60 days after the expense date may be denied at the Finance team's discretion. Reports over 90 days old will not be processed.

### 5.2 Expense Report Requirements

Each expense report must include:
1. Employee name, department, and manager
2. Business purpose for each expense line item
3. Receipts for all qualifying transactions
4. Pre-approval documentation (where applicable)
5. Names of attendees (for meals/entertainment)
6. Project or cost center code

### 5.3 Expense Management System

NovaTech uses **Expensify** as the Company's expense management platform. All expense reports must be submitted via Expensify. Paper expense reports are only accepted in exceptional circumstances and must be pre-approved by Finance. Employees can access Expensify at expensify.com using their NovaTech Google Workspace SSO.

### 5.4 Reimbursement Timeline

- Properly submitted and approved expense reports are reimbursed within **7 business days** via direct deposit to the bank account on file in Workday.
- Reports requiring additional documentation or approval are placed on hold and the Finance team will notify the employee within 3 business days.

## Section 6: Corporate Credit Card Usage

### 6.1 Eligibility

Corporate credit cards (issued through Chase Ink Business) are available to:
- All employees at Director level and above
- Sales team members (AEs and above)
- Employees who travel more than 4 times per quarter (upon manager request)
- Other employees with demonstrated need, approved by Finance

### 6.2 Corporate Card Requirements

- Corporate cards are for business expenses only. Personal use of corporate cards is a policy violation and may result in immediate card suspension and disciplinary action.
- All corporate card transactions must be reconciled in Expensify monthly; statements are due by the 5th of the following month.
- Receipts must be uploaded to Expensify for each transaction.
- Employees are personally liable for transactions that are found to be non-business expenses or policy violations.
- Credit limits: Analyst/Associate: $2,000/month; Manager: $5,000/month; Director: $10,000/month; VP+: $25,000/month.

### 6.3 Corporate Card Termination

Upon separation from the Company, the corporate card must be returned or destroyed within 1 business day. The Finance team will notify Chase of the termination and deactivate the card. Outstanding balances must be reconciled before final paycheck is issued.

## Section 7: International Travel

### 7.1 Pre-Approval Requirements

All international travel requires VP-level written approval at least 5 business days before departure. The approval request should include:
- Destination(s) and dates
- Business purpose and client/partner name
- Estimated total cost
- Visa and documentation requirements

### 7.2 Currency and Foreign Exchange

- Employees should use their corporate card for international transactions wherever possible to benefit from favorable exchange rates and eliminate individual currency conversion.
- Foreign currency conversion fees on corporate cards are reimbursable.
- If local cash is necessary, employees may request a travel advance from Finance (minimum 5 business days notice). Unused advance must be returned within 5 business days of return.
- Receipts in foreign currency must include the exchange rate and USD equivalent.

### 7.3 Travel Insurance and Safety

- NovaTech provides company-paid travel insurance for all employees on approved business travel (through Chubb International Travel Insurance).
- Employees traveling to countries with State Department Level 2 or higher advisories must register their travel with the HR/Operations team.
- Emergency 24-hour travel assistance: +1-800-555-0178 (Chubb Global Assistance)

### 7.4 International Per Diem Adjustments

Standard international per diem applies per Section 2.3. For specific high-cost cities, the following adjusted per diems apply:

## Section 8: Policy Exceptions and Appeals

### 8.1 Requesting Exceptions

Employees who believe a necessary business expense does not comply with this Policy may request an exception by submitting a written justification to their manager and the Finance team. Exception requests must be submitted in advance of incurring the expense (or within 5 business days for unplanned exceptions).

### 8.2 Appeal Process

If an expense reimbursement is denied and the employee believes the denial is incorrect, they may appeal by submitting a written appeal to the Finance Manager within 10 business days of the denial. The Finance Manager's decision is final for amounts under $2,000. For amounts over $2,000, the VP Finance provides final review.
