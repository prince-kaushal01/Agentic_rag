## Executive Summary

NovaTech Solutions delivered solid Q1 2026 results, achieving revenue of **$4.2 million** against a budget of $4.05 million — a 3.7% favorable variance. Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) grew to **$18.2 million**, up from $16.8 million at the end of Q4 2025, representing 8.3% quarter-over-quarter growth and 36% year-over-year growth. Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) stands at **$1.517 million**.

The Company continues to invest aggressively in go-to-market and product development, resulting in an operating loss (EBITDA) of **$(800,000)** for the quarter — within the board-approved investment plan of a maximum $(900,000) quarterly EBITDA loss. Cash position remains healthy at **$12.0 million**, providing **approximately 11 months of runway** at the current burn rate of $1.1 million per month.

Key highlights:
- Net new ARR of $1.4M, driven by three new enterprise wins and strong expansion from existing accounts
- Gross margin improved to 74% from 71% in Q4 2025
- Customer count reached 87 (up from 81 in Q4 2025)
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR) of 112%
- Logo churn of 2 customers in Q1 (MRR churn of $28,000, or 1.8% of beginning MRR)

## Section 6: Churn Analysis

### 6.1 Logo Churn

In Q1 2026, NovaTech lost 2 customers representing $28,000 in MRR:

| Customer | Tier | MRR | Reason | AE Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brightwood Agency | Professional | $8,500 | Competitor (price) | Tom Reyes |
| Kellner Manufacturing | Business | $19,500 | Budget freeze / internal mandate | Lisa Park |

**Brightwood Agency** churned after a competitive evaluation in which a lower-cost tool (Competitor pricing approx. 40% lower) won on price. The customer did not cite product dissatisfaction. A win-back play is planned for Q3 2026 after they complete their current evaluation period.

**Kellner Manufacturing** was affected by a company-wide spending freeze imposed by their new CFO. The churn was unexpected and unrelated to product performance. The account team has maintained relationship contacts for potential re-engagement in 2027.

### 6.2 ARR Churn Rate

- Beginning ARR: $16,800,000
- ARR Churn: $220,000 (including $28K MRR × 12 months annualized, plus annualized churn from prior quarter that flowed through)
- **ARR Churn Rate: 1.3%** (within 2.0% annual target)

### 6.3 Expansion Revenue

Expansion ARR of $640,000 in Q1 was driven by:
- ACME Corp seat expansion (+50 users, +$24,000 ACV): $24,000

## Section 9: Variance vs. Budget Commentary (CFO Notes)

*Commentary by Priya Nair, CFO (Interim)*

Q1 2026 represents a strong start to the fiscal year. Revenue outperformance of $150K vs. budget was driven by two factors: stronger-than-expected expansion from existing enterprise customers (particularly GlobalTech's AI Analytics upgrade, which closed two weeks ahead of plan), and professional services revenue from a new ACME Corp customization project initiated in January.

On the expense side, total OpEx came in $53,760 below budget. Engineering slightly overran on contractor costs due to the mobile app acceleration initiative — this is an approved strategic investment. Sales & Marketing underspent, reflecting the delayed start of two enterprise AE hires and favorable timing on marketing programs.

Gross margin improvement to 74% is a positive trend and we are on track for our 76% FY2026 target. The AWS EDP renegotiation completed in December is yielding expected savings and we expect an additional optimization when our Snowflake contract renews in August 2026.

The fundraising process is progressing on schedule. We expect to announce a Series B close by late Q3 2026, which will extend our runway and fund the planned product platform expansion and international go-to-market investment.
