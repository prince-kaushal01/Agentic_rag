## 1. Overview & Learning Philosophy

NovaTech Solutions views continuous learning as a strategic advantage and a core component of employee experience. We believe that well-trained, curious employees build better products, serve customers more effectively, and grow into future leaders of the company.

Our learning philosophy is built on three pillars:
- **Self-Directed Learning:** We trust employees to identify their development needs and pursue relevant learning with minimal bureaucracy
- **On-the-Job Application:** The best learning happens through stretch assignments, real projects, and mentorship — not just courses
- **Community Learning:** We invest heavily in internal knowledge-sharing through lunch-and-learns, internal documentation, and mentorship programs

All employees receive:
- **$1,500/year Learning & Development budget** (see Benefits Guide for eligible expenses)
- **LinkedIn Learning** enterprise license (unlimited access)
- Access to NovaTech's internal Learning Hub on Notion
- Eligibility for the Mentorship Program (after 90 days of employment)
- Invitation to all internal Lunch-and-Learn sessions

### 5.2 Request Process for Non-Listed Certifications
1. Submit a request to learning@novatechsolutions.com with certification name, cost, and business justification
2. Manager approves the request
3. People Ops reviews and approves within 5 business days
4. Employee pays upfront and submits receipt in Rippling
5. Reimbursed in next payroll cycle

### 5.3 Certification Retention Agreement
For certifications costing over $1,000 in total (prep materials + exam), employees must remain employed at NovaTech for 12 months after reimbursement or repay a prorated portion:
- Departure within 6 months: repay 100%
- Departure 6–12 months: repay 50%
- Departure after 12 months: no repayment required

## 7. Mentorship Program

NovaTech's Mentorship Program connects employees across levels and departments for 6-month, structured developmental relationships.

### 7.1 Program Overview
- **Duration:** 6 months per cohort (two cohorts per year: January–June and July–December)
- **Time commitment:** 1 hour per month (minimum; most pairs meet 2 hours/month)
- **Format:** Primarily 1:1 video or in-person meetings; optional group mentor circles of 4–6 mentees

### 7.2 Eligibility
- **Mentees:** All employees with 90+ days of tenure; no level cap
- **Mentors:** Employees at IC4 or above, or M1 and above; must have been at NovaTech 12+ months; must volunteer

### 7.3 Application & Matching Process
Applications open 6 weeks before each cohort start. Employees fill out a matching survey in Lattice covering:
- Professional goals and development areas
- Preferred mentor background and function
- Preferred meeting cadence

People Ops matches mentors and mentees using the survey data. Both parties confirm the match before the cohort begins. If a match doesn't work, People Ops re-matches within 2 weeks.

### 7.4 Program Structure

| Month | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| 1 | Introductions; goal-setting; establish relationship norms |
| 2 | Skill development conversation #1; career mapping |
| 3 | Midpoint check-in (facilitated by People Ops) |
| 4 | Skill development conversation #2; project or challenge coaching |
| 5 | Networking introductions (mentor introduces mentee to 1–2 contacts) |
| 6 | Wrap-up; reflection; next steps |

### 7.5 Mentor Benefits
- Recognition at company All-Hands
- Lattice leadership development credit
- $75 team dinner credit (to take mentee to lunch or dinner)

## 9. Manager Development Program

NovaTech invests specifically in the development of people managers through the **NovaTech Manager Excellence Program (NMEP)**.

### 9.1 New Manager Bootcamp (For First-Time Managers)
A 3-day intensive program run quarterly. Topics covered:
- Transitioning from IC to Manager: mindset shifts
- How to conduct effective 1:1s
- Setting goals and OKRs with your team
- Performance management basics and how to give feedback
- Hiring and onboarding your first team member
- Managing underperformance

Scheduled: March 16–18, June 15–17, September 14–16, December 7–9, 2026.

### 9.2 Manager Peer Learning Groups
Monthly 90-minute cohort discussions (groups of 6–8 managers, facilitated by People Ops HRBP). Topics rotate based on manager-submitted challenges.

### 9.3 Executive Coaching
Directors (M3) and VPs (M4) are eligible for one-on-one executive coaching through **BetterUp** (up to 12 sessions/year, fully company-funded). Coaches are matched based on development focus areas.
