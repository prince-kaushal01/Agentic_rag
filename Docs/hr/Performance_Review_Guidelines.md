## 1. Overview & Philosophy

NovaTech Solutions conducts formal performance reviews on a **twice-yearly cycle** — in **June (Mid-Year Review)** and **December (Year-End Review)**. These reviews are the cornerstone of our performance management system and serve multiple connected purposes:
- Providing structured, documented feedback to support employee growth
- Creating accountability for goal achievement and behavioral expectations
- Informing compensation decisions (Year-End only)
- Identifying promotion candidates and high-potential employees
- Flagging underperformance early and creating support structures

We believe performance management is a continuous process, not a once-a-year event. Formal reviews supplement — not replace — ongoing 1:1 conversations, real-time feedback, and quarterly OKR check-ins. Managers are expected to ensure that review ratings never come as a surprise to their direct reports.

The performance review process is administered through **Lattice**, our People Success Platform. All forms, submissions, and calibration data are managed within Lattice.

## 3. Rating Scale & Descriptors

All performance assessments use a **1–5 rating scale**. Ratings apply both to individual goal achievement and to behavioral competencies, and the combination produces an overall rating.

### Rating Definitions

| Rating | Label | Description |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **5** | Exceptional | Far exceeds all expectations. Demonstrates extraordinary impact beyond the role's defined scope. Consistently delivers outcomes that significantly accelerate team or company results. A model for others. Top ~5% of performers. |
| **4** | Exceeds Expectations | Consistently exceeds role expectations and delivers above what is required. Takes initiative, anticipates needs, and generates results above target. Operates effectively at the next level in some areas. Top ~20% of performers. |
| **3** | Meets Expectations | Fully meets the requirements of the role. Delivers on commitments, demonstrates core competencies, and is a reliable, effective contributor. This is a positive, respected rating — the majority of NovaTech employees should fall here. |
| **2** | Partially Meets Expectations | Inconsistently meets expectations. Has strengths but demonstrates notable gaps in performance, skill, or behavior that require active development and coaching. May be appropriate for newer employees still ramping. |
| **1** | Does Not Meet Expectations | Consistently falls below role expectations in one or more significant areas. Typically triggers a structured Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). Significant risk to business outcomes. |

### Rating Distribution Guidance
Calibration targets (not hard caps) for Year-End ratings:
- Rating 5 (Exceptional): ~5% of employees
- Rating 4 (Exceeds Expectations): ~20% of employees
- Rating 3 (Meets Expectations): ~55–60% of employees
- Rating 2 (Partially Meets): ~10–15% of employees
- Rating 1 (Does Not Meet): ~2–5% of employees

These are calibration targets. Teams may deviate based on genuine performance distribution with justification.

## 4. Self-Assessment Template

Employees complete a self-assessment in Lattice covering the following sections. Recommended length: 400–800 words total.

### Section 1: Goal Achievement
For each OKR set at the beginning of the review period:
- What was the goal and its business context?
- What was achieved? (Include quantitative metrics where possible)
- What obstacles were encountered and how were they addressed?
- What would you do differently?

**Example prompt:** *"Describe your most significant accomplishment this period. What was the business impact, and how did you achieve it?"*

### Section 2: Behavioral Competencies (Self-Rating)
Rate yourself on the following competencies (using the 1–5 scale) and provide 1–2 sentences of evidence:
- Customer Focus
- Collaboration & Teamwork
- Communication (written and verbal)
- Innovation & Problem Solving
- Accountability & Execution
- (Role-specific competency — e.g., Technical Depth for Engineering, Pipeline Discipline for Sales)

### Section 3: Development & Growth
- What new skills or knowledge did you develop this period?
- What areas do you most want to develop in the next 6 months?
- What resources, training, or experiences would best support your growth?

### Section 4: Looking Forward
- What are your career aspirations for the next 12–24 months?
- Is there anything about your current role, team, or environment that you'd like to share?

## 5. Manager Review Form

Managers complete their assessment in Lattice before reviewing the employee's self-assessment to ensure independence of perspective. Manager reviews cover:

### Section 1: Goal Achievement Rating
Rate each employee OKR on a 1–5 scale with specific evidence. Then provide an overall goal achievement rating.

**Narrative prompt:** *"Summarize this employee's achievement against their goals this period. What were their standout accomplishments and where did they fall short?"*

### Section 2: Behavioral Competency Ratings
For each core competency, provide:
- A 1–5 rating
- 2–3 sentences of specific behavioral evidence (STAR format preferred)
- At least one specific example per rating of 4 or above, and at least one area for development per rating of 2 or below

### Section 3: Overall Rating
The overall rating is determined by the manager holistically weighing goal achievement and behavioral competencies. It is not a simple mathematical average.

### Section 4: Development Recommendations
- 2–3 specific development goals for the next 6 months
- Suggested learning resources, stretch assignments, or mentors
- Honest assessment of readiness for promotion (Yes / No / Not Yet / In 12+ months)

### Section 5: Retention Risk Assessment
**This section is visible to People Ops and the manager's manager only — not the employee.**
- Retention risk: High / Medium / Low
- Flight risk factors (if any)
- Recommended retention actions

## 6. Peer Feedback Collection

Each employee selects **3–5 peer feedback providers** from Lattice's peer suggestion tool. Managers can adjust the list.

Peer reviewers are asked to complete a 15-minute survey covering:
- Collaboration: "Describe a time this person made a meaningful contribution to shared work. What impact did they have?"
- Communication: "How effectively does this person communicate ideas, expectations, or feedback?"
- Overall impression: One key strength and one area for development.

Peer feedback is anonymous and shared with managers and employees (in aggregate) as developmental input. It does not directly set ratings but informs the manager's assessment.

## 7. Calibration Process

### 7.1 Purpose of Calibration
Calibration ensures rating consistency across managers and teams, reduces unconscious bias, and prevents "grade inflation" by any single team.

### 7.2 Calibration Session Structure
Calibration occurs at the VP/Director level. Each calibration session includes:
- All managers within the VP's organization
- A People Ops HRBP facilitator
- An optional Finance observer (Year-End only)

**Session format (typically 2–3 hours):**
1. HRBP presents aggregate rating distribution for the team
2. Managers share ratings for any employee rated 1, 4, or 5 (outliers) and provide evidence
3. Group discussion to test consistency and surface bias
4. Manager may adjust ratings with justification (all adjustments tracked)
5. Final ratings locked in Lattice after session

### 7.3 Calibration Principles
- Ratings must be evidence-based, not tenure-based
- Recency bias (over-weighting the most recent 4 weeks) is actively challenged
- Collaboration, communication, and behavioral ratings should be calibrated across cross-functional perspectives
- Managers who consistently cluster ratings at 3 or above without evidence are coached

## 8. Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

A Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is initiated when an employee receives an overall rating of 1 (Does Not Meet Expectations) or upon manager determination of serious performance deficiencies at any point in the year.

### 8.1 PIP Initiation
1. Manager discusses performance concerns with their People Ops HRBP before any formal steps
2. People Ops drafts the PIP document in collaboration with the manager
3. PIP is reviewed by VP and Legal before delivery
4. Manager delivers the PIP in a 1:1 meeting with an HR witness

### 8.2 PIP Components
A PIP must include:
- Specific performance concerns with documented examples
- Clear, measurable improvement targets (SMART goals)
- Timeline for improvement (typically 30, 60, or 90 days depending on severity)
- Support resources (additional coaching, training, closer check-ins)
- Consequences of not meeting improvement targets
- Employee acknowledgment signature

### 8.3 PIP Monitoring
Managers conduct bi-weekly check-in meetings with the employee during the PIP period, documenting progress in Lattice. At the PIP conclusion:
- **Improvement demonstrated:** PIP is formally closed; employee remains employed with continued monitoring
- **Insufficient improvement:** Manager initiates termination process with People Ops

### 8.4 PIP Protections
PIP processes must not be used as a pretext for discrimination. Any PIP initiated within 90 days of an employee's leave return, disability disclosure, or protected activity must be reviewed by Legal.

## 9. Promotion Criteria

Promotions at NovaTech are based on demonstrated performance at the next level, not tenure. The guiding question is: *"Is this person already performing at the next level, or are we promoting them in hopes they will?"*

### 9.1 Promotion Requirements
- Minimum 12 months in current level (exceptions for extraordinary performers require VP + CEO approval)
- Two consecutive performance periods at rating 4 (Exceeds) or one at rating 5 (Exceptional)
- Specific evidence of operating at or above the target level
- Manager and Department VP sponsorship
- Calibration group endorsement

### 9.2 Promotion Timeline
Promotions are reviewed twice per year aligned with performance reviews. Promotion recommendations are submitted in the calibration session and require Finance sign-off on the associated compensation adjustment.

### 9.3 Promotion Raise Guidelines
Promotions include a salary adjustment of **8–15%** above the employee's current base, landing within the target band for the new level. The specific amount is determined by:
- Where the employee sits in the current band (employees at the low end of the band receive closer to 15%)
- Market data for the target level
- Internal equity considerations

## 10. Compensation Review Linkage (Year-End Only)

Year-End review ratings directly inform the following compensation actions effective February 1 of the following year:

| Overall Rating | Merit Increase Range | Bonus Payout | Equity Refresh Eligibility |
|---------------|---------------------|--------------|---------------------------|
| 5 (Exceptional) | 6–10% | 120–150% of target | Yes — above-standard grant |
| 4 (Exceeds) | 4–6% | 100–120% of target | Yes — standard grant |
| 3 (Meets) | 2–4% | 80–100% of target | Yes — standard grant |
| 2 (Partially Meets) | 0–2% | 50–80% of target | No |
| 1 (Does Not Meet) | 0% | 0% | No |

Compensation decisions are finalized by the VP of each department and Finance, with People Ops providing equity and market benchmarking data.

## 12. Manager Training Requirements

All people managers must complete the following before conducting performance reviews:

### 12.1 Required Training
- **"Conducting Effective Performance Conversations" (Lattice Learning):** 2-hour online module — must be completed annually in May and November
- **"Recognizing and Reducing Bias in Performance Evaluations" (LinkedIn Learning):** 1-hour module — annual completion required
- **"How to Write a Performance Improvement Plan" (People Ops workshop):** Required before issuing first PIP, and refreshed annually

### 12.2 Manager Readiness Checklist (Before Review Season)
- [ ] Completed required training modules
- [ ] Reviewed direct reports' OKRs and progress data
- [ ] Held at least bi-weekly 1:1s throughout the period
- [ ] Gathered peer feedback inputs
- [ ] Scheduled review conversation meetings with direct reports
- [ ] Attended calibration session with People Ops HRBP

Managers who have not completed training requirements by the review kickoff date will be flagged to their department VP.
