## 2. Mission, Vision & Core Values

### Our Mission
To empower enterprise organizations with intelligent software that transforms how teams share knowledge, resolve issues, and operate with confidence.

### Our Vision
A world where every employee, regardless of their role or location, has instant access to the knowledge and tools they need to do their best work.

### Core Values

**1. Customer Obsession**
Every decision we make starts with the customer. We ask "how does this serve the person using our product?" before we ask "is this convenient for us?" We invest in deep customer relationships and measure our success by their success.

**2. Bias Toward Action**
We ship, we learn, we iterate. We prefer a 70% solution delivered today over a perfect solution delivered never. We encourage experimentation and accept that not every initiative will succeed. The worst outcome is standing still.

**3. Radical Transparency**
We share information openly — financial results, strategic decisions, and organizational challenges. We give candid feedback and expect to receive it. Transparency requires courage; we practice it anyway.

**4. Craft Excellence**
We take pride in the quality of our work. Whether you're writing code, crafting a proposal, or designing a support process, excellence in craft matters. We invest in improving our skills and hold each other to high standards.

**5. Inclusive by Default**
We build diverse teams and create environments where every voice is heard. We recognize that our product serves a global customer base, and our workforce must reflect that diversity to serve them well.

**6. Long-Term Thinking**
We make decisions that are right for NovaTech over a 5–10 year horizon, not just the next quarter. This means investing in talent development, infrastructure, and relationships before they become urgent.

## 3. Code of Conduct

### 3.1 Professional Standards
All NovaTech employees are expected to conduct themselves with integrity, professionalism, and respect in all work-related interactions — whether with colleagues, customers, vendors, or the general public.

Specific expectations include:
- Representing NovaTech accurately and professionally in all external communications
- Maintaining confidentiality of proprietary company information, customer data, and personnel matters
- Exercising sound judgment when representing the company at conferences, events, or on social media
- Disclosing any conflicts of interest to your manager and People Operations promptly

### 3.2 Conflicts of Interest
A conflict of interest arises when personal interests could improperly influence your professional decisions. Examples include:
- Holding a financial stake in a competitor or potential customer without disclosure
- Conducting outside employment that competes directly with NovaTech's business
- Using NovaTech resources (time, equipment, relationships) for personal business
- Participating in hiring decisions involving immediate family members without disclosure

Employees must disclose any actual or perceived conflicts of interest to their manager and People Operations within 10 business days of becoming aware of the conflict.

### 3.3 Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure
All employees sign a Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) as a condition of employment. This agreement remains in effect during employment and for 24 months following separation, regardless of the reason for separation.

Confidential information includes, but is not limited to:
- Source code and technical architecture
- Customer lists, pricing, and contract terms
- Financial projections and board materials
- Product roadmap and unreleased features
- Personnel records and salary information
- M&A discussions and partnership negotiations

Employees who inadvertently disclose confidential information must report the disclosure to Legal & Compliance at legal@novatechsolutions.com within 24 hours.

### 3.4 Use of Company Assets
Company-owned assets — including laptops, software licenses, communication platforms, and cloud infrastructure — are provided for business use. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it does not:
- Consume significant company resources
- Create legal liability or reputational risk
- Interfere with your job responsibilities
- Violate any law or NovaTech policy

Employees must return all company assets within 5 business days of separation. Failure to do so may result in the company pursuing recovery through appropriate legal channels.

## 4. Workplace Policies

### 4.1 Working Hours
NovaTech operates on a flexible work model. While there are no mandatory fixed start and end times for most roles, all employees are expected to be available during core collaboration hours of **10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone**, Monday through Friday. Attendance at scheduled team meetings is required unless prior arrangements are made with your manager.

Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime pay. Non-exempt employees must accurately record all hours worked and obtain manager approval before working overtime.

### 4.2 Attendance & Punctuality
Regular attendance is essential. If you are unable to work on a scheduled day, notify your manager via direct message (Slack) and email as early as possible, and no later than 30 minutes after your expected start time. Chronic absenteeism or tardiness without adequate justification may result in disciplinary action.

### 4.3 Travel Policy
Business travel must be pre-approved by your manager and submitted through Concur. Booking guidelines:
- **Flights:** Book economy class for domestic and flights under 6 hours; business class permitted for international flights over 8 hours with VP approval
- **Hotels:** Maximum $250/night in Tier 1 cities (San Francisco, New York, London); $175/night elsewhere
- **Meals:** Up to $75/day per diem; receipts required for individual expenses over $25
- **Ground Transport:** Rideshare or public transit preferred; rental cars require manager approval

All travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the trip via Concur.

### 4.4 Expense Reimbursement
Non-travel work expenses (software subscriptions, office supplies, etc.) must be pre-approved if over $100 and submitted within 30 days of purchase. Reimbursements are processed on a bi-weekly basis.

### 4.5 Data Privacy & Security
All employees must complete the annual Security Awareness Training (administered through KnowBe4) and adhere to NovaTech's Information Security Policy. Key requirements:
- Use strong, unique passwords and enable MFA on all company systems
- Never share login credentials with colleagues
- Lock your workstation when stepping away
- Report phishing emails to security@novatechsolutions.com immediately
- Store all sensitive documents in approved company systems (Google Drive, Notion) — not personal storage

## 5. Communication Guidelines

### 5.1 Internal Communication Tools
NovaTech uses the following primary communication platforms:
- **Slack:** Day-to-day messaging, quick questions, team channels. Response expected within 4 business hours during core hours.
- **Email (Google Workspace):** Formal communications, external-facing messages, documentation. Response expected within 1 business day.
- **Notion:** Documentation, wikis, project plans, meeting notes.
- **Google Meet / Zoom:** Video meetings. Default to Google Meet for internal; Zoom for large external calls.
- **Asana:** Project and task tracking.

### 5.2 Meeting Culture
We value focused, effective meetings. Guidelines:
- Every meeting must have a written agenda shared at least 24 hours in advance
- Meetings should have a designated note-taker; notes must be posted to Notion within 24 hours
- Default meeting length is 25 minutes or 50 minutes (leave buffer time)
- Decline meetings where your attendance is not critical; flag this to the organizer
- Camera-on is encouraged but not mandatory for video meetings

### 5.3 Slack Etiquette
- Use threads to keep channel conversations organized
- Set your status when unavailable (e.g., "In a meeting until 2pm")
- Avoid @here or @channel tags unless the message is genuinely urgent for all channel members
- Keep #general channels positive and inclusive; avoid controversial non-work topics

## 6. Anti-Harassment & Non-Discrimination Policy

NovaTech Solutions is committed to maintaining a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns, customers, and vendors in any work-related context — including company offices, remote work environments, business travel, and company-sponsored events.

### 6.1 Protected Characteristics
It is unlawful and contrary to NovaTech policy to discriminate based on:
Race, color, national origin, ancestry, religion, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, age (40+), disability, pregnancy or childbirth, military or veteran status, genetic information, marital status, or any other characteristic protected by applicable federal, state, or local law.

### 6.2 What Constitutes Harassment
Harassment includes any unwelcome conduct — verbal, physical, visual, or digital — that creates a hostile, intimidating, or offensive work environment. Examples include:
- Offensive jokes, slurs, or epithets
- Unwanted physical contact
- Display of offensive images or material
- Persistent unwanted romantic advances
- Threats or intimidation

Sexual harassment specifically includes quid pro quo harassment (conditioning employment benefits on sexual favors) and hostile environment harassment.

### 6.3 Reporting Procedure
Employees who experience or witness harassment should report it through one of the following channels:
- **Direct report to manager** (if the manager is not the subject of the complaint)
- **HR Business Partner:** hrbp@novatechsolutions.com
- **Anonymous Hotline:** 1-800-555-0177 (operated by a third-party provider)
- **EthicsPoint Online Portal:** novatech.ethicspoint.com

All reports will be taken seriously, investigated promptly and confidentially, and resolved within 30 business days where possible. Retaliation against anyone who in good faith reports harassment or participates in an investigation is strictly prohibited and will itself be subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 7. Dress Code

NovaTech operates a **business casual** dress code in office environments and no dress code for remote work. When meeting clients, attending conferences, or participating in formal company events, employees are expected to dress at a level that reflects professional respect for the occasion.

**Business casual includes:**
- Collared shirts, blouses, sweaters, or professional tops
- Slacks, chinos, skirts, or professional dresses
- Clean, closed-toe shoes or smart casual footwear

**Not permitted in office or client settings:**
- Ripped or distressed clothing
- Graphic tees with offensive or political content
- Athletic wear (yoga pants, gym shorts) unless a specific casual day is designated
- Beach or pool attire

Employees with religious or cultural dress requirements that differ from these guidelines should contact People Operations to discuss reasonable accommodations.

## 9. Performance Expectations

### 9.1 Goal Setting
All employees set quarterly OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) in collaboration with their manager. Individual OKRs should ladder up to team and company OKRs. OKRs are documented in Lattice by the end of Week 1 of each quarter.

### 9.2 Feedback Culture
NovaTech operates a continuous feedback culture. Managers are expected to hold 1:1 meetings with direct reports at minimum bi-weekly. Employees are encouraged to request feedback proactively and to give peer feedback through Lattice.

### 9.3 Formal Performance Reviews
Formal performance reviews occur twice per year — in **June** (mid-year) and **December** (year-end). Reviews include a self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer input. Ratings use a 1–5 scale. Full details are in the **Performance Review Guidelines** document.

### 9.4 Performance Improvement Plans
Employees whose performance falls below expectations may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). A PIP outlines specific, measurable improvement targets and a defined timeline (typically 30–90 days). Completion of a PIP successfully may result in removal of the plan; failure to meet PIP targets may result in termination.

## 10. Disciplinary Process

NovaTech follows a progressive disciplinary process for most policy violations, though the company reserves the right to skip steps for serious or gross misconduct.

**Step 1: Verbal Warning**
A documented conversation between the manager and employee outlining the specific concern, expected change, and timeline. A summary is placed in the employee's HR file.

**Step 2: Written Warning**
A formal written notice detailing the issue, prior warnings, required corrective actions, and consequences of continued non-compliance. Signed by the employee and manager.

**Step 3: Final Written Warning**
A final formal notice, often accompanied by a PIP or suspension with pay pending investigation. Clearly states that termination will follow if improvement is not demonstrated.

**Step 4: Termination**
Employment separation, which may be with or without severance depending on circumstances.

Gross misconduct (e.g., fraud, physical violence, theft, serious data breach) may result in immediate termination without prior warnings. Full details are in the **Disciplinary and Grievance Policy** document.

## 11. Offboarding

When employment ends — whether through resignation, termination, or retirement — NovaTech follows a structured offboarding process.

**Employee Responsibilities:**
- Submit resignation in writing to your manager with a minimum of 2 weeks' notice (4 weeks recommended for senior roles)
- Return all company equipment within 5 business days of last day
- Transfer work documentation and active project notes to your manager or designated team member
- Complete exit interview with People Operations

**Company Responsibilities:**
- Process final paycheck including accrued unused PTO per California law
- Provide COBRA election materials within 14 days of benefits termination
- Offboard system access on the final day of employment
- Provide employment verification as requested through Truework

**Notice Period During Offboarding:**
Employees are expected to remain professionally engaged during their notice period. NovaTech may choose to place an employee on garden leave (paid leave during notice period) at its discretion, particularly for roles with access to sensitive information.
