## 1. Policy Overview

NovaTech Solutions is committed to a flexible-first work model that enables high performance regardless of location. We believe exceptional work happens when employees have the autonomy to design a work environment that suits them — provided they meet clear standards for availability, collaboration, and output.

This policy applies to all full-time employees and establishes the requirements, expectations, and entitlements associated with remote and hybrid work at NovaTech Solutions.

As of January 2026, approximately **67% of NovaTech's workforce works fully remotely**, 18% works in a hybrid arrangement (2–3 days/week in office), and 15% works fully in-office at our San Francisco HQ or Austin satellite office.

Remote work is a **privilege and a practical arrangement**, not an unconditional right. NovaTech reserves the right to modify remote work arrangements with reasonable notice where business needs require it (e.g., a project requiring extended in-person collaboration, a role reclassified to require office presence).

## 2. Remote Work Eligibility

### 2.1 Fully Remote Eligibility
The following criteria must be met to be eligible for a fully remote work arrangement:
- Role is not designated as "office-required" in the job description or offer letter
- Employee has reliable internet connectivity (minimum 50 Mbps download, 20 Mbps upload)
- Employee has a dedicated workspace meeting the requirements in §4
- Employee is located in a country where NovaTech is legally authorized to employ staff
- Employee is not on an active Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) — employees on a PIP may be required to work in-office more frequently at manager discretion

### 2.2 Roles Designated as Office-Required (as of 2026)
The following role categories currently require in-office presence in San Francisco at minimum 3 days/week:
- Executive Leadership (C-suite and their Executive Assistants)
- Facilities and IT Infrastructure roles
- Roles designated as "onsite" in the job posting

All other roles are eligible for remote or hybrid arrangements. When in doubt, refer to the role designation in the offer letter or ask People Operations.

### 2.3 New Employee Remote Policy
New employees in engineering, sales, and customer success are encouraged (but not required) to be in-office or participate in in-person onboarding for their first 30 days, where feasible. This is strongly recommended to accelerate relationship-building and knowledge transfer.

## 4. Home Office Requirements

Employees working remotely are responsible for establishing and maintaining a workspace that supports professional, productive work.

### 4.1 Required Setup

**Internet Connectivity:**
- Minimum **50 Mbps download / 20 Mbps upload** speed
- Wired (Ethernet) connection strongly preferred for video calls
- A backup connectivity option (mobile hotspot) is recommended for outages
- NovaTech reimburses $50/month for internet service (see §7)

**Workspace:**
- A dedicated workspace separate from living/sleeping areas (preferred but not strictly required)
- Workspace must be private enough to protect the confidentiality of customer information during calls
- Suitable lighting for professional video calls (natural light or a desk lamp minimum; ring light recommended)
- Ergonomic setup: chair that supports extended sitting, monitor at eye level, keyboard at comfortable height

**Technology (company-provided — see §8):**
- NovaTech-issued laptop (MacBook Pro 14" M3 Pro, standard issue)
- Access to all required company software (provisioned on Day 1)

**Optional Setup:**
- External monitor (supported by $800 home office stipend — see §9)
- External webcam, microphone, or headset (covered by home office stipend)

### 4.2 What NovaTech Cannot Require
NovaTech may not require employees to:
- Maintain a specific room layout or background
- Use personal funds to purchase required equipment (all required equipment is company-provided)
- Work from a space that is not reasonably private or professional

Employees are encouraged to use the Slack #home-office-setups channel to share ideas and inspiration for workspace optimization.

## 5. Core Working Hours & Availability

### 5.1 Core Collaboration Hours
All remote employees, regardless of time zone, must be **available and responsive during core collaboration hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone**, Monday through Friday.

During core hours, employees are expected to:
- Respond to Slack messages within 60 minutes
- Be available for unscheduled calls with reasonable (15-minute) notice
- Have Slack status set accurately (e.g., "In a meeting," "Focused work until 2pm," "At lunch")

### 5.2 Flexible Hours Outside Core Window
Outside of core hours (8am–10am and 3pm–6pm local), employees have flexibility to structure their workday as they see fit, provided:
- Total working hours average approximately 8 hours/day
- Scheduled meetings are attended (see §6)
- Deliverables are completed on time

NovaTech does not use surveillance software to monitor employee activity or screen time.

### 5.3 Time Zone Guidelines
NovaTech operates primarily in US time zones (PT, MT, CT, ET) with a growing EMEA team on GMT/CET. When scheduling cross-time-zone meetings:
- Default to overlap windows: 10:00 AM–3:00 PM PT (which is 6:00 PM–11:00 PM GMT)
- Rotate meeting times for recurring cross-timezone meetings to share the burden
- Never schedule required meetings outside an employee's core work hours (10am–6pm local) without advance agreement
- EMEA employees have flexible core hours of 9:00 AM–2:00 PM CET

## 6. Meeting Attendance Expectations

### 6.1 Required Meetings
All remote employees are expected to attend:
- **Team standups or weekly syncs** (as scheduled by team)
- **Company All-Hands** (held monthly via Zoom, 11:00 AM PT, first Friday of the month)
- **1:1 meetings with manager** (at minimum bi-weekly; attendance is mandatory unless emergency)
- **Project or client meetings** directly relevant to the employee's role
- **Quarterly team offsites** (in-person travel; see §10)

### 6.2 Camera-On Expectations
NovaTech encourages but does not require cameras to be on during meetings. However:
- Cameras are expected to be on for **external customer meetings**
- Cameras are encouraged for **team standups and important internal discussions**
- It is always acceptable to turn off your camera for background noise, bandwidth, or personal reasons — just flag it in the meeting chat

### 6.3 Meeting-Free Blocks
NovaTech protects two **meeting-free blocks** per week:
- **Wednesday afternoons:** No internal meetings scheduled after 12:00 PM local time (for deep work)
- **Friday afternoons:** No internal meetings scheduled after 2:00 PM local time

These blocks are defaults and may occasionally be overridden with advance notice for critical business needs.

## 7. Expense Reimbursement for Remote Work

### 7.1 Internet Reimbursement
All remote employees receive a **$50/month internet reimbursement**, processed automatically through Rippling as a monthly stipend (taxable income). No receipt submission required.

### 7.2 Phone Stipend
Employees who use their personal phone for work receive a **$50/month phone stipend**, also auto-processed through Rippling (taxable income).

### 7.3 Home Office Setup
- **One-time $800 home office stipend** for eligible equipment purchases (see §9 and Benefits Guide)
- Additional equipment requests exceeding the stipend require manager and Finance approval

### 7.4 Other Remote Work Expenses
Recurring work expenses (software subscriptions not covered by company accounts, office supplies, etc.) require manager approval over $50 and must be submitted through Concur within 30 days.

Engineering employees additionally receive:

- Ethernet adapter (USB-C to Gigabit Ethernet)

### 8.2 Equipment Maintenance
- NovaTech equipment must be kept in good working order
- Accidental damage should be reported to IT within 48 hours (it@novatechsolutions.com)
- Intentional damage or theft are the employee's financial responsibility
- Lost equipment must be reported immediately to IT and People Ops for remote wipe

### 8.3 Equipment Return
All company-owned equipment must be returned within **5 business days** of separation. NovaTech will provide a prepaid shipping label for remote employees. Unreturned equipment may be deducted from final paycheck in accordance with applicable law, or pursued through legal channels.

### 8.4 Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)
Personal devices (phones, tablets) may be used to access company email and Slack. BYOD access requires enrollment in NovaTech's Mobile Device Management (MDM) solution (Jamf for Apple devices). Employees who prefer not to enroll their personal device may request a company mobile device.

## 9. Coworking Space Stipend

NovaTech recognizes that not all remote employees have ideal home working conditions. Employees may expense up to **$150/month for a coworking space membership**.

### 9.1 Eligible Expenses
- WeWork, Industrious, Regus, or similar coworking space membership
- Day passes at coworking spaces (up to $30/day)
- Private office rentals at shared coworking spaces

### 9.2 Not Eligible
- Coffee shops or restaurants (even if used for work)
- Home office furniture or supplies (covered by home office stipend)
- Hotel business centers during personal travel

### 9.3 How to Submit
Submit monthly receipts in Rippling under the "Coworking" expense category. Receipts required for all coworking expenses.

## 10. Travel Requirements & Quarterly Offsites

### 10.1 Quarterly Team Offsites
Every NovaTech team holds a **quarterly in-person offsite** (4 per year). Attendance is **mandatory** for all team members, barring medical or family emergency.

- NovaTech covers **all travel costs**: flights (economy domestic, business for flights >8 hours), hotel, meals, and event activities
- Employees must book travel through Concur at least 3 weeks in advance
- Offsites typically run 2–3 days (Mon-Wed or Tue-Thu) and include both work sessions and social activities

### 10.2 Annual Company All-Hands Summit
Once per year, NovaTech brings the entire company together for a **3-day Company All-Hands Summit**. The 2026 Summit is scheduled for **October 19–21, 2026** in San Francisco, CA. Attendance is expected for all full-time employees worldwide. NovaTech covers travel, hotel, and all event costs.

### 10.3 Customer Travel
Sales, Customer Success, and Executive roles may be required to travel to customer locations as needed. Customer travel is approved through Concur and follows the standard travel policy (Employee Handbook §4.3).

### 10.4 Advance Notice for Travel
Employees should receive at least **4 weeks advance notice** for required travel (offsites, summits). Emergency business travel may require shorter notice; People Ops and managers should minimize this where possible, especially for employees with caregiving responsibilities.

## 11. International Remote Work

### 11.1 Approved Work Locations
Employees may only work from countries where NovaTech has established legal entity or employer of record (EOR) infrastructure:

| Country | Legal Entity Type |
|---------|------------------|
| United States | NovaTech Solutions, Inc. (HQ) |
| United Kingdom | NovaTech Solutions Ltd. |
| Germany | NovaTech Solutions GmbH |
| Canada | EOR via Deel |
| Australia | EOR via Deel |
| Netherlands | EOR via Deel |
| Ireland | EOR via Deel |

Working from any country not on this list requires advance legal review and is generally not permitted for periods exceeding 30 days.

### 11.2 Temporary Remote Work from Another Country
Employees who wish to work temporarily from another country (e.g., visiting family, "workcation") must:
- Request approval from their manager AND People Ops at least **30 days in advance**
- Provide the dates and location of planned work
- Receive written approval before departure

Temporary international remote work is generally approved for up to **30 consecutive days** in countries with no NovaTech presence. Longer periods require Legal review.

### 11.3 Tax and Visa Compliance
Employees are responsible for understanding their personal tax and visa obligations when working abroad. NovaTech does not provide individual tax advice for international work situations. Employees should consult with a tax advisor for stays over 30 days.

## 12. Data Security & Confidentiality Requirements

Remote employees bear the same information security obligations as in-office employees, with additional considerations for home environments.

**Remote Security Requirements:**
- Always use NovaTech's company-issued VPN (Tailscale) when accessing internal systems from networks other than your home network
- Do not conduct confidential customer calls in public spaces where conversations can be overheard
- Lock your laptop screen when stepping away, even at home
- Never connect company devices to untrusted public Wi-Fi without VPN active
- Report any suspected data breach, loss of equipment, or security incident to security@novatechsolutions.com within 1 hour
- Do not use personal cloud storage (Google Drive personal account, Dropbox personal) for company data
- Do not print company documents at home unless using a secure, private printer in your home office

All remote employees must complete the annual Security Awareness Training and phishing simulations (mandatory — see Training Catalog §2).

## 13. Performance Standards for Remote Employees

Remote employees are held to the **same performance standards** as in-office employees. Location does not affect performance evaluation criteria, promotion eligibility, or compensation decisions.

Managers of remote employees must:
- Hold bi-weekly 1:1s (non-negotiable for remote direct reports)
- Set clear, written expectations for deliverables and communication
- Not penalize employees for working different hours within the approved flexible window
- Ensure remote team members have equal access to development opportunities, high-visibility projects, and recognition

If performance issues emerge, managers must follow the same performance management process as for in-office employees.

---

## 14. Manager Responsibilities

Managers of remote or hybrid teams are expected to:

| Responsibility | Frequency |
|---------------|-----------|
| Hold 1:1 with each remote direct report | At minimum bi-weekly |
| Actively include remote employees in team discussions | Every meeting |
| Ensure offsite planning accounts for remote employee logistics | Every quarter |
| Check in on remote employee well-being (not just work output) | Monthly |
| Provide equal visibility and recognition to remote employees | Ongoing |
| Report concerns about remote employee performance proactively | As needed |

Managers who struggle with managing remote teams are encouraged to take LinkedIn Learning's "Managing Remote and Hybrid Teams" course (3 hours) and to discuss challenges with their People Ops HRBP.
