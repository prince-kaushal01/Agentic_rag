## 1. Onboarding Philosophy

At NovaTech Solutions, we believe the onboarding experience sets the tone for an employee's entire journey with us. Our goal is for every new hire to feel:
- **Informed:** You understand our business, our product, our customers, and your role before you finish your first month
- **Connected:** You have built real relationships with your manager, your team, and cross-functional partners
- **Impactful:** You have made a meaningful contribution to the team's work within your first 90 days

Our 30-60-90 day framework provides structure, but it is a guide — not a rigid checklist. Work with your manager to adapt goals to your specific role and circumstances.

People Operations coordinates onboarding centrally. Your hiring manager is responsible for the day-to-day experience. Your assigned Onboarding Buddy provides peer support and a safe space for questions you might not want to ask your manager.

## 2. Pre-Boarding Tasks (Before Day 1)

### People Operations Will:
- [ ] Send welcome email with Day 1 logistics within 24 hours of offer acceptance
- [ ] Initiate background check via Checkr (must be cleared before start date)
- [ ] Send I-9 employment verification (via Workbright) — must be completed 3 days before start
- [ ] Create accounts in Rippling (HRIS), Google Workspace, Slack, Greenhouse
- [ ] Ship company laptop (MacBook Pro 14" M3, standard) to home address (remote employees) or have it ready at desk (office employees) — shipped 5 business days before start
- [ ] Assign Onboarding Buddy (People Ops matches based on role, team, and location)
- [ ] Send pre-boarding Notion welcome guide link
- [ ] Schedule Day 1 orientation (video call at 10:00 AM PT)

### New Employee Will:
- [ ] Complete I-9 documentation via Workbright
- [ ] Complete direct deposit setup in Rippling
- [ ] Enroll in benefits (30-day window opens on start date but review options in advance)
- [ ] Sign and return any outstanding agreements (NDA, equity grant if applicable)
- [ ] Review the pre-boarding Notion welcome guide (company overview, product walkthrough video, team org chart)
- [ ] Connect with your Onboarding Buddy via a brief intro Slack message or email before Day 1 (Buddy's name will be in the welcome email)
- [ ] Set up your laptop when it arrives (unbox guide included in shipment)

### Hiring Manager Will:
- [ ] Confirm start date and remote/in-office setup with People Ops
- [ ] Prepare a written 30-day onboarding plan specific to the role (template in Notion)
- [ ] Schedule Day 1 manager 1:1 at 1:00 PM PT
- [ ] Notify team of new hire's start date and arrange informal welcome activities
- [ ] Add new hire to relevant Slack channels, Notion spaces, Asana projects
- [ ] Schedule standing bi-weekly 1:1 meetings
- [ ] Prepare list of key internal stakeholders for new hire to meet in Week 1–2

## 3. Day 1 Schedule

**9:30 AM PT:** Laptop setup assistance (IT helpdesk available via Slack #it-help or onsite)

**10:00 AM PT:** Welcome Orientation (Video Call — led by People Ops)
- Introductions from People Ops team
- NovaTech company history, mission, and values
- Product overview (30-minute demo walkthrough)
- Org structure and key leaders introduction
- Logistics: payroll, benefits enrollment window, expense policy

**12:00 PM PT:** Virtual Welcome Lunch or In-Person Team Lunch (Hiring Manager coordinates)

**1:00 PM PT:** 1:1 with Hiring Manager
- Your role and 30-day plan
- Team context and current priorities
- Communication preferences and working style
- Q&A

**2:30 PM PT:** IT System Setup Block (self-paced)
- Activate Google Workspace (email, Drive, Calendar)
- Set up Slack (channels, notifications, profile)
- Log in to Notion, Asana, Rippling, Lattice
- Set up 1Password (company password manager)

**4:00 PM PT:** Meet your Onboarding Buddy (video call)
- Get to know each other
- Buddy shares their experience and tips
- Agree on cadence for check-ins (typically weekly for first 30 days)

**4:45 PM PT:** Day 1 Wrap
- Reflect on what you learned
- Write down any outstanding questions
- Optional: join #new-hires Slack channel and introduce yourself

## 4. First Week Activities (Days 2–5)

### Day 2: Team & Product Deep Dive
- [ ] Attend team standup or weekly meeting
- [ ] 1:1 with each direct teammate (15–30 min each — manager schedules)
- [ ] Complete NovaTech Product Tour (self-guided in Notion) — ~2 hours
- [ ] Watch recorded customer demo (link in Notion Onboarding Space)
- [ ] Read 3 customer case studies (ACME Corp, GlobalTech Inc, Meridian Enterprises)

### Day 3: Cross-Functional Orientation
- [ ] Meeting with Sales (30 min) — understand go-to-market and ICP
- [ ] Meeting with Customer Success (30 min) — understand customer journey post-sale
- [ ] Meeting with Engineering lead (30 min) — understand technical architecture at high level
- [ ] Review company OKRs for current quarter in Notion

### Day 4: Tools & Processes
- [ ] Complete mandatory Security Awareness Training (KnowBe4) — ~1 hour
- [ ] Complete Harassment Prevention Training (Traliant) — ~1 hour
- [ ] Review relevant team runbooks and processes in Notion
- [ ] Set up Asana task list for your 30-day plan
- [ ] Review expense policy and set up Concur account

### Day 5: Reflection & Goals
- [ ] Complete Week 1 feedback survey (sent via Lattice)
- [ ] Draft your first 30-day goals with your manager's input
- [ ] Write 3 things you want to learn in your first 30 days
- [ ] Finalize benefits enrollment (if not already done)
- [ ] Join optional Friday All-Hands (held remotely, 11am PT — calendar invite auto-sent)

## 6. Buddy Program

The Buddy Program pairs every new hire with an experienced NovaTech employee (typically 1–2 years of tenure, same general function but different team).

### Buddy Responsibilities
- Reach out to new hire before Day 1 with a friendly welcome message
- Meet weekly for the first 30 days (30-minute casual video or coffee chat)
- Be a no-judgment resource for "how do we do things here" questions
- Share context on team culture, unwritten norms, and communication styles
- Introduce the new hire to 3–5 additional colleagues in the broader organization
- Complete a 30-day check-in survey (sent by People Ops via Lattice)

### Buddy Selection Criteria
- Not the direct manager or skip-level of the new hire
- Has been at NovaTech at least 12 months
- Voluntarily joined the Buddy Program (no mandatory assignment)
- Has capacity in their current workload (confirmed by their manager)
- Ideally shares some functional background with the new hire

### Buddy Benefits
- Recognition in the monthly People Ops newsletter
- $50 Buddy lunch credit (redeemable via Compt, once per quarter)
- Leadership development credit for mentorship activity in Lattice

## 8. Team Introductions & Networking

Building a strong internal network is critical to success at NovaTech. During your first 30 days, aim to meet:
- All direct teammates (1:1 or group)
- Your manager's peer managers (skip-level introductions)
- Key cross-functional partners (identified by your manager)
- 1–2 leaders in departments you'll work with closely

**Tips:**
- Use the #new-hires Slack channel to meet other recent joiners
- Attend virtual or in-person team social events (check #novatech-social in Slack)
- Reach out to people you want to learn from — cold Slack messages are welcomed in our culture
- Use the Notion org chart to explore the company and identify people you're curious about

## 9. 30-Day Goals by Role Type

### Engineering (IC1–IC4)
- [ ] Set up complete development environment and make first commit (even a minor fix or docs update)
- [ ] Complete code review on 3 pull requests submitted by teammates
- [ ] Understand the architecture of your team's core service(s) (review architecture docs, ask questions)
- [ ] Shadow at least one customer call or support escalation
- [ ] Complete Product Certification Level 1

### Engineering (IC5–IC6)
- [ ] Technical architecture deep dive: read all design docs for team's domain
- [ ] Identify one area for technical improvement; write a proposal doc
- [ ] Lead or participate in at least one sprint planning and retrospective
- [ ] Meet with Engineering leads in adjacent teams (platform, security, infra)

### Sales (SDR/BDR)
- [ ] Complete NovaTech Sales Foundations training (Notion + Gong library)
- [ ] Shadow 10 customer calls with senior reps (Chorus)
- [ ] Master the product demo with a practice run with your manager
- [ ] Book first 5 discovery calls independently by Day 30

### Sales (AE)
- [ ] Complete Sales Playbook and ICP review
- [ ] Shadow 5 customer calls across stages (discovery, demo, negotiation)
- [ ] Take ownership of at least 2 active opportunities from the pipeline
- [ ] Submit your first weekly pipeline forecast in Salesforce

### Customer Success
- [ ] Shadow 5 customer calls with senior CSMs
- [ ] Complete Gainsight platform training (Gainsight University — Admin + CSM tracks)
- [ ] Read all customer health score records for assigned accounts
- [ ] Hold introductory calls with your top 5 assigned accounts

### Marketing
- [ ] Review all active campaigns and content calendar
- [ ] Meet with Sales to understand marketing-sales alignment and ICP
- [ ] Complete first draft of an assigned project (content piece, campaign brief, etc.)
- [ ] Set up tracking in HubSpot and Google Analytics

## 10. 60-Day Goals by Role Type

### Engineering
- [ ] Own at least one feature or bug-fix end-to-end through the full development cycle
- [ ] Present a technical deep-dive or findings to the team in a lunch-and-learn format
- [ ] Be a primary reviewer for your team's code review process
- [ ] Have a clear understanding of team's quarterly OKRs and how your work maps to them

### Sales (AE)
- [ ] Take sole ownership of at least 5 active opportunities
- [ ] Complete first customer demo independently (non-shadowed)
- [ ] Understand and articulate competitive positioning vs. 3 key competitors
- [ ] Hit 80% of your first partial-quarter pipeline-building quota

### Customer Success
- [ ] Complete first QBR (Quarterly Business Review) with a key account (co-facilitated with senior CSM)
- [ ] Identify at least 1 at-risk account and document an intervention plan
- [ ] Submit first health score updates in Gainsight for all assigned accounts

### Marketing
- [ ] Launch or contribute to an active campaign end-to-end
- [ ] Develop proficiency in HubSpot and key analytics tools
- [ ] Contribute to the content calendar for the next quarter

---

## 11. 90-Day Goals by Role Type

### Engineering
- [ ] Independently own a full project or significant component from design to deployment
- [ ] Actively participate in team planning and technical decision-making
- [ ] Deliver a measurable quality or efficiency improvement
- [ ] Have a clear 6-month personal development plan documented in Lattice

### Sales (AE)
- [ ] Close at least 1 deal or advance an opportunity to a final stage
- [ ] Be running a clean, accurate pipeline in Salesforce independently
- [ ] Have a personal territory plan for the next 2 quarters
- [ ] Receive a "Meet Expectations" or above feedback from manager

### Customer Success
- [ ] Independently manage full book of assigned accounts
- [ ] Demonstrate measurable improvement in at least one account's health score
- [ ] Contribute to a renewal or expansion conversation independently

### All Employees
- [ ] 90-day check-in conversation with manager (formal, documented in Lattice)
- [ ] Self-assessment of onboarding experience submitted via Lattice survey
- [ ] Clear OKRs set for the following quarter
- [ ] Feedback shared with Onboarding Buddy and People Ops on program improvement
