## 1. Overview & Guiding Principles

NovaTech Solutions is committed to hiring exceptional talent efficiently, equitably, and with an outstanding candidate experience. Our recruitment process is designed to:
- Identify candidates who demonstrate both technical skill and cultural alignment
- Move quickly (median time-to-offer of 21 days for IC roles, 35 days for manager and above)
- Provide every candidate with a consistent, respectful interview experience
- Build a diverse workforce that reflects our global customer base

This SOP applies to all hiring at NovaTech Solutions, including full-time employee hires across all levels and departments. Contractor and consultant engagements follow a separate procurement process.

## 2. Job Requisition Approval

### 2.1 Initiating a Req
All new hires must be backed by an approved headcount plan. Hiring managers may only open a recruitment process after receiving written approval of an open requisition (req) through the Lever/Greenhouse workflow.

**Requisition submission requires:**
- Business justification (growth hire, backfill, or new function)
- Start date target
- Level designation (IC1–IC6 or M1–M4)
- Salary band confirmation from People Ops
- Budget confirmation from Finance (req number from Adaptive Insights)
- Location designation (SF HQ, Austin, Remote-US, Remote-EMEA)

### 2.2 Approval Tiers

| Hire Level | Approvers Required |
|------------|-------------------|
| IC1 – IC3 | Hiring Manager + Department VP |
| IC4 – IC5 | Hiring Manager + Department VP + People Ops |
| IC6 (Staff/Principal) | Hiring Manager + Department VP + CPO/CTO + People Ops |
| M1 – M2 (Manager) | Department VP + People Ops + CFO |
| M3 – M4 (Director/VP) | CEO + People Ops + CFO |

Approval must be documented in Greenhouse before the role is posted. People Ops will not initiate sourcing for unapproved reqs.

### 2.3 Headcount Planning
Headcount is planned annually in October/November and refreshed quarterly. Unplanned hires outside the approved headcount plan require CFO + CEO approval and must be documented with a specific business case. Emergency backfills (e.g., sudden departure in a critical role) may be fast-tracked with VP approval.

## 3. Job Description Writing Guidelines

### 3.1 Standard JD Structure
All job descriptions must follow this template:
1. **Role Summary** (2–3 sentences: what the role does and its primary impact)
2. **Key Responsibilities** (5–8 bullet points, action-oriented)
3. **Required Qualifications** (must-haves only — keep this list short, 4–6 items)
4. **Preferred Qualifications** (nice-to-haves — clearly labeled as such)
5. **About NovaTech Solutions** (standard boilerplate — provided by People Ops)
6. **Benefits & Compensation** (salary band, equity range, key benefits)

### 3.2 Language Guidelines
- Use gender-neutral language throughout (e.g., "they" pronoun references, avoid "rockstar" or "ninja")
- Limit required qualifications to what is genuinely necessary. Studies show requirements lists over 8 items significantly reduce applications from underrepresented candidates.
- Do not include degree requirements unless the role genuinely requires specialized academic credentials (e.g., certain Legal or Finance roles). Use "equivalent experience" language.
- Avoid internal jargon unfamiliar to external candidates
- All JDs must pass the Textio gender bias score before posting (target: Textio Score 65+)

### 3.3 Compensation Transparency
Per California SB 1162 (effective January 1, 2023), all posted positions must include a salary range. Use the full approved band from the Compensation structure; do not narrow the range artificially.

## 4. Sourcing Channels & Strategy

### 4.1 Primary Sourcing Channels

| Channel | Use Case | Owner |
|---------|----------|-------|
| LinkedIn Recruiter | Active and passive candidate sourcing | Recruiter |
| Greenhouse Job Board | Inbound applications | Recruiter |
| NovaTech Careers Page | SEO inbound | Marketing + Recruiting |
| Employee Referrals | Warm pipeline | All Employees |
| Gem (sourcing CRM) | Outbound sequences, nurture campaigns | Recruiter |
| Wellfound (AngelList) | Startup-aware tech candidates | Recruiter |
| Underrepresented Communities | Specific diversity initiatives (see §10) | Recruiter + DEI |

### 4.2 Employee Referral Program
Employee referrals are a priority sourcing channel. Referral bonuses:
- **IC1–IC3 hire:** $2,000 paid 90 days after new hire's start date
- **IC4–IC6 hire:** $4,000 paid 90 days after new hire's start date
- **Manager (M1–M2) hire:** $5,000 paid 90 days after start
- **Director+ hire:** $7,500 paid 90 days after start

Referrals from employees in the same department as the open role are eligible but require additional approval to ensure objectivity in the process.

### 4.3 Agency Usage
Recruitment agencies (contingency or retained) are only engaged when:
- Internal recruiting is unable to source sufficient pipeline within 4 weeks
- The role is highly specialized (e.g., Principal Security Engineer)
- The role is at M3+ level

Approved agency partners include Spencer Stuart (executive search), Kforce (technical roles), and Motion Recruitment (engineering). New agency relationships require Legal and Finance approval. Agency fees are capped at 18% of first-year base salary.

## 5. Screening Criteria & Application Review

### 5.1 Resume Review
Recruiters review all applications within 5 business days of submission. Minimum review criteria:
- Relevant experience (years and type, role-specific)
- Evidence of scope and impact (not just responsibilities)
- Trajectory and growth
- Company/environment fit (enterprise SaaS experience preferred for most roles)

Applications from underrepresented groups should receive equivalent review time. Automated rejection for resume keywords alone is prohibited.

### 5.2 Recruiter Screen
A 30-minute phone or video screen with the recruiter covering:
- Role interest and motivation
- Current situation and timeline
- Compensation expectations and equity comfort
- Basic qualification confirmation
- NovaTech overview and Q&A

Recruiter screen disposition must be documented in Greenhouse within 24 hours. Candidates advancing receive a hiring manager screen invitation within 3 business days.

## 6. Interview Stages

### 6.1 Standard Interview Process (IC1–IC4)

| Stage | Format | Duration | Conducted By |
|-------|--------|----------|-------------|
| 1. Recruiter Screen | Video/Phone | 30 min | Recruiter |
| 2. Hiring Manager Screen | Video | 45 min | Hiring Manager |
| 3. Technical/Skills Assessment | Take-home or live coding/case | Varies | Designated Assessor |
| 4. Virtual Panel Loop | 4 x 45-min video interviews | 3 hours | Panel of 4 |
| 5. Executive Interview | Video | 30 min | VP or C-suite |
| 6. Reference Checks | Phone | 20 min each x3 | Recruiter |

### 6.2 Panel Interview Composition
The 4-person interview panel should include:
- Hiring Manager (behavioral/role-fit focus)
- Peer from same team (technical/domain depth)
- Cross-functional peer (collaboration/communication)
- People Ops or team member from underrepresented group (culture/DEI lens)

Panel composition must be approved by the recruiter in advance. No two interviewers should cover the same competency area.

### 6.3 Senior & Executive Roles (IC5–IC6, M3–M4)
Additional stages may include:
- Board member or investor conversation (C-level hires)
- Extended business case presentation (Director+ hires)
- In-person visit to SF HQ (strongly recommended for all M3+ offers)

### 6.4 Interview Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Ask consistent, structured questions across all candidates for the same role
- Take notes in Greenhouse Scorecards in real time
- Focus on past behaviors and specific examples (STAR framework)
- Ask about impact and outcomes, not just responsibilities

**Don't:**
- Ask about age, family status, children, pregnancy, disability, religion, national origin, or any protected characteristic
- Discuss compensation unless you are the recruiter or People Ops
- Share feedback informally with other interviewers before they've completed their scorecard

## 7. Scoring Rubrics & Evaluation Criteria

### 7.1 Greenhouse Scorecard
Every interviewer must submit a completed Greenhouse Scorecard within **24 hours** of their interview. Scorecards include:
- A rating on each assigned competency (1–4 scale)
- An overall hire recommendation (Strong Hire / Hire / No Hire / Strong No Hire)
- Required written comments for every competency rated

### 7.2 Universal Competencies (All Roles)

| Competency | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Communication | Clarity, conciseness, listening, adapts to audience |
| Problem Solving | Structured approach, breaks down complexity, creative solutions |
| Collaboration | Works across functions, builds trust, manages conflict constructively |
| Customer Focus | Demonstrates customer empathy and outcome orientation |
| Growth Mindset | Takes feedback well, shows intellectual curiosity, learns from failure |

### 7.3 Role-Specific Competencies

**Engineering Roles:**
- Technical depth (algorithms, system design, code quality)
- Debugging and troubleshooting
- Technical communication

**Sales Roles:**
- Discovery and qualification (MEDDIC framework)
- Objection handling
- Pipeline management discipline

**Product Roles:**
- Prioritization frameworks
- Data literacy and hypothesis-driven thinking
- Stakeholder alignment

### 7.4 Debrief Process
A structured debrief call (30–45 minutes) is held within 2 business days of the final interview. The recruiter facilitates. Each interviewer shares their scorecard rating and key data points. The hiring manager makes the final recommendation, with the recruiter acting as a DEI and process accountability check.

## 8. Offer Process

### 8.1 Offer Approval
Once the hiring manager recommends extending an offer, the following approvals are required:

| Comp Level | Approvers |
|------------|-----------|
| Within band, standard | Hiring Manager + People Ops |
| Above midpoint of band | Department VP + People Ops + Finance |
| Above band maximum | CEO + CFO + People Ops (rare exception) |

Approvals are tracked in Greenhouse offer approval workflow. Target time from recommendation to verbal offer: **2 business days**.

### 8.2 Verbal Offer
The recruiter (not the hiring manager) delivers all verbal offers. The verbal offer includes:
- Base salary
- Equity grant (shares/value, vesting schedule)
- Bonus eligibility and target
- Start date flexibility
- Benefits summary

### 8.3 Written Offer Letter
Written offer letters are generated in Greenhouse and sent via DocuSign within 24 hours of verbal offer acceptance. Offer letters expire in **5 business days** if not signed.

### 8.4 Compensation Philosophy
NovaTech targets compensation at the **65th percentile** of the San Francisco Bay Area market for all roles, using Radford/Aon survey data updated annually in January. Equity grants are intended to place total comp at the **75th percentile** over a 4-year vest period.

## 9. Background Checks

All offers are conditional pending a satisfactory background check conducted through **Checkr**. Background checks are initiated immediately upon offer letter signing.

**Standard background check includes:**
- Social Security Number (SSN) verification
- Criminal history (7-year lookback, federal and county)
- Employment history verification (last 3 employers)
- Education verification (highest degree claimed)
- Sex offender registry check

**Additional checks for certain roles:**
- Credit check: Finance roles with fiduciary responsibility
- Driving record: Roles requiring company vehicle operation
- Professional license verification: Legal, accounting, medical roles

Background check results are reviewed by People Ops. Adverse findings trigger an individualized assessment before any adverse action is taken, in compliance with FCRA. Candidates are notified and given the opportunity to dispute inaccuracies.

## 10. Diversity, Equity & Inclusion in Hiring

### 10.1 DEI Targets (2026)
NovaTech has set the following representation targets for 2026 hires:
- 45%+ women and gender non-conforming individuals in new hires
- 35%+ underrepresented racial/ethnic groups in new hires (Black, Latinx, Native American, Pacific Islander)
- 50%+ diverse candidates in final-round interview slates for all roles
- 100% diverse panel (at least one interviewer from an underrepresented group) for all final rounds

### 10.2 Diverse Sourcing Requirements
For every requisition, recruiters must document active outreach to at least 2 of the following diversity-focused sourcing channels:
- Lesbians Who Tech
- National Society of Black Engineers (NSBE)
- Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers (SHPE)
- Out in Tech
- Latinx in Tech
- Black Girls Code talent network
- PowerToFly
- Incluzion

### 10.3 Blind Resume Review
Engineering and Product roles use blind resume screening for the initial pass (names, institutions, and photos removed via Greenhouse blind review mode).

## 11. ATS Usage (Greenhouse)

Greenhouse is NovaTech's Applicant Tracking System of record. All candidate interactions must be documented in Greenhouse.

**Required in Greenhouse:**
- All candidate communications (via Greenhouse email integration)
- Interview schedules (synced with Google Calendar)
- Scorecards and debrief notes
- Offer details and approval chain
- Rejection reasons (standardized dropdown list)
- Disposition of every applicant (no orphan records)

**Greenhouse Access Levels:**
- Recruiter: Full admin for assigned reqs
- Hiring Manager: Can view applications, scorecards, and submit scorecards; cannot change offer terms
- Interviewer: Can access assigned interviews and submit scorecards only

Greenhouse data is audited quarterly by People Ops for completeness and bias indicators (e.g., pass-through rate disparities by demographic).

| IC1 – IC2 (Junior) | 15 days |

| IC5 (Senior) | 28 days |

Timelines are measured from the date the req is opened in Greenhouse to the date the written offer is sent. Timelines that exceed these targets require a written explanation from the recruiter to the Head of Talent.

## 13. Recruiter & Hiring Manager Responsibilities

### Recruiter Responsibilities
- Own the overall process and candidate experience
- Source, screen, and pipeline candidates within SLA
- Schedule all interviews and coordinate panel logistics
- Conduct all verbal and written offer conversations
- Ensure DEI commitments are met for every req
- Document all activity in Greenhouse within 24 hours

### Hiring Manager Responsibilities
- Write or review the job description before posting
- Participate in panel debrief and provide a clear recommendation
- Be available for interview scheduling within 5 business days of advancing a candidate
- Submit scorecards within 24 hours of interviews
- Avoid discussing candidates with panel members before they've completed their scorecard
- Provide new hire with a strong Day 1 onboarding experience
