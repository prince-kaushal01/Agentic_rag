## 1. Policy Overview & Scope

This policy governs all forms of leave available to full-time employees of NovaTech Solutions headquartered or employed in the United States. Part-time employees (defined as working fewer than 30 hours per week) receive prorated leave entitlements. Contractors and consultants engaged through third-party staffing firms are not covered by this policy.

NovaTech Solutions complies with all applicable federal, state, and local leave laws, including but not limited to the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA), California Family Rights Act (CFRA), California Paid Sick Leave law, California Pregnancy Disability Leave (PDL), New York Paid Family Leave (NY PFL), and applicable local ordinances. Where applicable law provides greater leave entitlements than this policy, the law governs.

All leave entitlements are calculated on a **calendar year basis** (January 1 – December 31), unless otherwise specified below. Employees who begin employment mid-year receive prorated entitlements based on their start date.

For leave-related questions, contact your HR Business Partner directly or email leaves@novatechsolutions.com.

## 2. Annual Leave (Paid Time Off – PTO)

### 2.1 Entitlement
All full-time employees receive **20 days (160 hours) of PTO per calendar year**. PTO accrues at a rate of **1.67 days (13.33 hours) per month**, beginning from the first day of employment.

Employees in their first 90 days of employment may use accrued PTO but may not take PTO in advance of accrual during this period without manager approval.

### 2.2 PTO Rate by Tenure
To reward long-term commitment, PTO accrual rates increase with tenure:

| Years of Service | Annual PTO Entitlement | Monthly Accrual |
|-----------------|----------------------|-----------------|
| 0 – 2 years | 20 days (160 hours) | 1.67 days |
| 3 – 5 years | 23 days (184 hours) | 1.92 days |
| 6 – 9 years | 26 days (208 hours) | 2.17 days |
| 10+ years | 30 days (240 hours) | 2.50 days |

### 2.3 Requesting PTO
- PTO requests must be submitted through **Rippling** (HRIS) and approved by the employee's direct manager
- For absences of **1–2 days:** minimum 48 hours advance notice is requested (but unplanned single-day absences may be handled per the sick leave procedure)
- For absences of **3–5 days:** minimum 1 week advance notice
- For absences of **6+ days (extended vacation):** minimum 4 weeks advance notice
- No more than two employees from the same team may be on PTO simultaneously without manager approval

### 2.4 Manager Approval Guidelines
Managers should approve PTO requests in a timely fashion (within 3 business days). Denial of PTO is permitted where business needs require coverage; in such cases, managers must provide a specific alternative window or negotiate a revised date. Managers may not deny PTO in a manner that results in forfeiture of legally protected leave.

### 2.5 PTO Payout on Termination
In compliance with California labor law, all accrued, unused PTO is paid out at the employee's current base salary rate upon separation, regardless of the reason for separation. Employees in other states should refer to applicable state law provisions, which may differ.

## 3. Sick Leave

### 3.1 Entitlement
All full-time employees receive **10 days (80 hours) of paid sick leave per calendar year**. Sick leave does not accrue on a per-month basis — it is front-loaded on January 1 each year (or upon hire for new employees). Unused sick leave does not carry forward to the following year.

### 3.2 Permitted Uses of Sick Leave
Sick leave may be used for:
- Employee's own illness, injury, or preventative medical care
- Care of a family member who is ill (spouse, domestic partner, parent, child, sibling, grandparent, or grandchild)
- Mental health days (up to 3 days per year without documentation requirement)
- Appointments related to domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking (per applicable law)

### 3.3 Documentation Requirements
- For sick leave absences of **1–3 days:** no documentation required
- For absences of **4 or more consecutive days:** a doctor's note or similar documentation may be requested by People Operations
- For extended or pattern absences (e.g., recurring same-day absences), People Operations may request documentation to ensure appropriate support and accommodation

### 3.4 Sick Leave Integration with FMLA/CFRA
When an employee's illness qualifies for FMLA or CFRA leave, NovaTech will run sick leave concurrently with protected leave. Employees will be notified when their leave is designated as FMLA/CFRA-protected.

## 4. Parental Leave

NovaTech Solutions offers generous parental leave to support new parents during a critical time in their family's life. This policy applies to all full-time employees who have completed at least **90 days of employment** prior to the leave start date.

### 4.1 Maternity Leave (Birth Parent)
Birth parents are entitled to:
- **Up to 16 weeks (112 days) of fully paid parental leave**
- Leave may begin up to 4 weeks prior to the expected due date for medical preparation
- An additional 6–8 weeks of California Pregnancy Disability Leave (PDL) may apply prior to parental leave for pregnancy-related disability, running concurrently with FMLA where applicable

**Total potential protected leave for birth parents: up to 24 weeks**

### 4.2 Paternity / Non-Birth Parent Leave
Non-birth parents (including fathers, same-sex partners, domestic partners, and adoptive parents) are entitled to:
- **8 weeks (56 days) of fully paid parental leave**
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth, adoption, or foster placement

### 4.3 Adoption & Foster Care Leave
Employees who adopt or become foster parents are entitled to:
- **8 weeks paid leave** for primary caregiver
- **4 weeks paid leave** for secondary caregiver
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of placement

### 4.4 Pay During Parental Leave
NovaTech supplements state disability insurance (SDI) and paid family leave (PFL) benefits to provide employees with **100% of their regular base salary** during parental leave periods, up to the entitlement maximums above.

### 4.5 Benefits Continuation During Parental Leave
All benefits (health insurance, 401k, equity vesting) continue uninterrupted during paid parental leave. If leave extends beyond the paid period into unpaid leave, health insurance continues through the end of the calendar month; 401k contributions and equity vesting are paused.

### 4.6 Return to Work After Parental Leave
Employees returning from parental leave will be reinstated to the same or equivalent position with no reduction in salary, benefits, or seniority. A phased return-to-work schedule (e.g., 50% time for first 2 weeks) may be arranged with manager and People Operations approval.

## 5. Bereavement Leave

Employees may take paid bereavement leave upon the death of a family member:

| Relationship | Paid Leave Entitlement |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Spouse / domestic partner | 5 business days |
| Child (biological, adopted, foster, step) | 5 business days |
| Parent (biological, adopted, step, in-law) | 5 business days |
| Sibling | 3 business days |
| Grandparent / Grandchild | 3 business days |
| Close friend (non-family) | 1 business day (manager discretion) |

Additional unpaid leave of up to 5 business days may be granted with manager approval where travel or complex estate matters require more time.

Documentation (e.g., obituary, death certificate, funeral program) may be requested by People Operations at their discretion, but will be handled sensitively.

## 6. Jury Duty & Civic Leave

### 6.1 Jury Duty
NovaTech provides paid leave for jury duty for up to **15 business days per calendar year**. Employees must:
- Notify their manager and People Operations as soon as they receive a summons
- Submit the jury summons documentation in Rippling
- Be available for work on any day when released from jury duty by 1:00 PM local time

Jury duty pay is at the employee's regular base salary rate. Any jury stipend received from the court may be retained by the employee.

### 6.2 Witness Duty
Employees subpoenaed to appear as witnesses in legal proceedings are granted paid leave for the required duration, up to 10 business days.

### 6.3 Voting Leave
Employees who need time to vote in federal, state, or local elections and are unable to do so outside of work hours are entitled to up to **2 hours of paid leave** per applicable law.

## 7. Unpaid Leave of Absence

Employees may request an unpaid leave of absence for personal reasons not covered by other leave types. Approval is granted at the company's discretion based on business needs.

### 7.1 Eligibility
- Minimum 1 year of service with NovaTech
- No active disciplinary actions or PIPs
- Manager and People Operations approval required

### 7.2 Duration
- Unpaid leaves are typically granted for 30–90 days
- Extensions beyond 90 days require VP-level approval and are rare
- Leaves beyond 6 months will be treated as a voluntary resignation

### 7.3 Benefits During Unpaid Leave
- Health insurance is maintained at employee's expense (via COBRA-equivalent or direct billing) during unpaid leave
- 401k contributions and equity vesting are paused
- PTO does not accrue during unpaid leave periods

## 8. Military Leave

NovaTech complies fully with the Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act (USERRA). Employees called to active military duty are entitled to:
- Reinstatement to their position (or equivalent) upon return, with seniority, benefits, and pay protection
- Up to **30 days of paid military leave per calendar year** for training and active duty
- Health insurance continuation for up to 24 months during military service (at employee cost after the initial 30-day period)

Employees must provide advance notice of military leave where possible. Documentation of military orders must be submitted to People Operations.

## 10. Leave Request Process

### 10.1 Planned Leave (PTO, Extended Leave, Parental Leave)
1. Employee submits leave request in **Rippling** at least the minimum notice period in advance
2. Manager reviews and approves/denies within 3 business days
3. For leaves of 5+ days, People Operations is automatically notified via Rippling
4. For parental, medical, or FMLA-qualifying leave, employee is contacted by People Operations to complete required paperwork within 5 business days of notification
5. Employee receives confirmation of approved leave dates and pay status

### 10.2 Unplanned Sick Leave
1. Employee notifies their direct manager via **Slack DM** and **email** as early as possible (no later than 30 minutes after expected start time)
2. Employee marks the absence in Rippling by end of business day
3. For absences of 4+ consecutive days, People Operations initiates leave paperwork automatically

### 10.3 Emergency Leave
In the event of an emergency (sudden illness, family emergency, unexpected bereavement):
1. Employee or a designated contact notifies manager as soon as possible
2. Full leave documentation can be completed upon return
3. People Operations will reach out to ensure the employee has appropriate support

## 11. Carryover Rules

### Annual PTO
- Employees may carry over up to **5 days (40 hours)** of unused PTO from one calendar year to the next
- Carryover PTO must be used by **March 31** of the following year, after which it is forfeited
- **California employees:** Per California labor law, PTO is treated as earned wages and does not expire or forfeit. California employees may carry over all unused PTO subject to a maximum cap of **30 days (240 hours)**.

### Sick Leave
- Unused sick leave does **not** carry over from one calendar year to the next (except where required by local law, e.g., certain cities in California)
- No payout of unused sick leave upon termination

### Floating Holidays
- Unused floating holidays do **not** carry over. Use both by December 31.

## 12. Unplanned Absence Contact Procedures

If you will be unexpectedly absent from work:

**Step 1:** Send a Slack DM to your direct manager immediately (or as soon as possible)
**Step 2:** Send an email to your manager at their company email address
**Step 3:** If you are unable to reach your manager (e.g., they are also unavailable), contact your skip-level manager or your team's People Operations HRBP

**Contact for Leave Administration:**
- **General Leave Inquiries:** leaves@novatechsolutions.com
- **HR Business Partners:** See Notion People Ops directory
- **Emergency People Ops Line:** +1 (415) 555-0142 (available 8am–6pm PT, Mon–Fri)

If you anticipate an extended absence (3+ days), proactively arrange for a colleague to cover critical responsibilities. Your manager can help coordinate this.

## 13. Return to Work

### 13.1 Standard Return
After any leave period, employees are expected to return to work on the agreed return date. If unable to return as planned, employees must notify People Operations at least 2 business days in advance.

### 13.2 Medical Return to Work
Employees returning from medical leave of 5+ days must provide a physician's clearance to return to work (if requested by People Operations). NovaTech may offer a phased return or temporary modified duties as an accommodation.

### 13.3 Accommodation Requests
Employees requiring workplace accommodations due to disability, pregnancy, or other protected conditions should contact People Operations at accommodations@novatechsolutions.com. NovaTech engages in an interactive process to identify reasonable accommodations.

## 14. Leave Interactions & Stacking Rules

Several leave types may overlap or run concurrently:
- FMLA/CFRA leave runs concurrently with any other qualifying leave (PTO, Sick Leave, Parental Leave)
- Paid parental leave and California Paid Family Leave (PFL) may run concurrently; NovaTech supplements to 100% of base salary
- Employees may not receive more than 100% of their base salary through a combination of company leave pay and state benefits
- PTO may generally not be used to extend parental or medical leaves beyond their defined entitlement periods

Questions about how different leave types interact in specific circumstances should be directed to leaves@novatechsolutions.com.
