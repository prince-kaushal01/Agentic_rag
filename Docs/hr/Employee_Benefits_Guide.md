## 1. Benefits Overview

NovaTech Solutions offers a comprehensive benefits package designed to support employees' physical health, financial security, professional growth, and overall well-being. Benefits are available to all **full-time employees** working 30 or more hours per week. Part-time employees (20–29 hours/week) are eligible for health benefits only, at a reduced company contribution.

Benefits eligibility begins on your **first day of employment** for most programs. Enrollment must be completed within **30 days of your start date**. If you miss the enrollment window, you must wait until the next Open Enrollment period (October 15 – November 15 annually, effective January 1 of the following year) or a qualifying life event.

**Total Compensation Philosophy:** NovaTech targets total compensation at the **65th–75th percentile** of the San Francisco Bay Area market, combining base salary, equity, bonus, and the benefits described in this guide.

## 2. Health Insurance — Medical

NovaTech partners with **Anthem Blue Cross** for medical insurance. Three plan options are available:

### Plan A: Premium PPO (Anthem Blue Cross PPO Plus)
- **Best for:** Employees who want maximum flexibility and low out-of-pocket costs
- **Monthly Premium (2026):**
  - Employee only: $0 (company covers 100%)
  - Employee + Spouse/DP: $180/month employee contribution
  - Employee + Child(ren): $140/month employee contribution
  - Employee + Family: $290/month employee contribution
- **Annual Deductible:** $500 individual / $1,000 family
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $2,500 individual / $5,000 family
- **Primary Care Visit Copay:** $15
- **Specialist Visit Copay:** $30
- **Emergency Room:** $150 (waived if admitted)
- **In-Network Coverage:** 90% after deductible
- **Out-of-Network Coverage:** 70% after deductible
- **Prescription Coverage:** $10 generic / $35 preferred brand / $70 non-preferred brand

### Plan B: Standard HMO (Anthem HMO Select)
- **Best for:** Employees who prefer lower premiums and are comfortable with a network
- **Monthly Premium (2026):**
  - Employee only: $0 (company covers 100%)
  - Employee + Spouse/DP: $95/month employee contribution
  - Employee + Child(ren): $75/month employee contribution
  - Employee + Family: $160/month employee contribution
- **Annual Deductible:** $0 (most services)
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $3,500 individual / $7,000 family
- **Primary Care Visit Copay:** $20
- **Specialist Visit Copay:** $40
- **Emergency Room:** $200 (waived if admitted)
- **In-Network Coverage:** 80% after deductible
- **Out-of-Network Coverage:** Not covered (emergencies excepted)
- **Prescription Coverage:** $5 generic / $25 preferred brand / $50 non-preferred brand

### Plan C: High-Deductible Health Plan with HSA (Anthem HDHP)
- **Best for:** Generally healthy employees who want to maximize HSA tax savings
- **Monthly Premium (2026):**
  - Employee only: $0 (company covers 100%)
  - Employee + Spouse/DP: $45/month employee contribution
  - Employee + Child(ren): $35/month employee contribution
  - Employee + Family: $75/month employee contribution
- **Annual Deductible:** $1,500 individual / $3,000 family
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $5,000 individual / $10,000 family
- **HSA Company Contribution:** NovaTech contributes $750 individual / $1,500 family to your HSA annually
- **In-Network Coverage:** 80% after deductible
- **Prescription Coverage:** Subject to deductible, then 80/20 co-insurance

**HSA Details:** Employees electing Plan C may contribute pre-tax dollars to a Health Savings Account (HSA) administered through Optum Bank. 2026 IRS HSA contribution limits: $4,150 (individual) / $8,300 (family). Funds roll over year to year and are portable.

## 5. 401(k) Retirement Plan

### 5.1 Plan Overview
NovaTech Solutions sponsors a 401(k) plan administered by **Fidelity Investments**. The plan allows employees to save for retirement on a pre-tax (Traditional) or post-tax (Roth) basis.

### 5.2 Eligibility & Enrollment
- **Eligibility:** First day of employment
- **Auto-Enrollment:** All employees are automatically enrolled at a **3% contribution rate** 30 days after hire date. Employees may opt out or change their contribution at any time via Fidelity NetBenefits.

### 5.3 Company Match
NovaTech matches **100% of employee contributions up to 4% of base salary**.

**Example:** If you earn $120,000 and contribute 4% ($4,800/year), NovaTech contributes $4,800, for a combined retirement contribution of $9,600/year.

Employees who contribute less than 4% will receive a match only on what they contribute. There is no "make-up" for periods of lower contribution.

### 5.4 Vesting Schedule
Company matching contributions vest on the following schedule:

| Years of Service | Vested Percentage |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| < 1 year | 0% |
| 1 year | 25% |
| 2 years | 50% |
| 3 years | 75% |
| 4 years | 100% |

Employee contributions (your own money) are always **100% vested immediately**.

### 5.5 Contribution Limits (2026 IRS Limits)
- Under age 50: $23,500/year
- Age 50 or older (catch-up): $31,000/year

### 5.6 Investment Options
The plan offers 25+ investment options including Fidelity index funds, target-date funds (Freedom Funds), and actively managed funds. NovaTech's default investment is the **Fidelity Freedom Index Fund** corresponding to the employee's projected retirement year. Employees may change investment elections at any time via NetBenefits.

## 6. Equity Compensation — Incentive Stock Options (ISOs)

### 6.1 Overview
NovaTech Solutions grants Incentive Stock Options (ISOs) to eligible employees as part of their total compensation package. Equity grants are subject to the terms of the 2021 Equity Incentive Plan and individual grant agreements.

### 6.2 Grant at Hire
New hire equity grants are issued in the form of ISOs with a **4-year vesting schedule and a 1-year cliff**:
- After **12 months of service**: 25% of the grant vests (the "cliff")
- Each subsequent month: an additional 1/48th of the total grant vests
- Full vesting occurs after **48 months**

**Example Grant Sizes (approximate, based on 2025 409A valuation of $12.50/share):**

| Level | Typical Grant (Shares) | Grant Value at 409A |
|-------|----------------------|---------------------|
| IC1–IC2 | 3,000–8,000 | $37,500–$100,000 |
| IC3–IC4 | 10,000–20,000 | $125,000–$250,000 |
| IC5 (Senior) | 22,000–40,000 | $275,000–$500,000 |
| IC6 (Staff/Principal) | 40,000–80,000 | $500,000–$1,000,000 |
| M1–M2 (Manager) | 20,000–40,000 | $250,000–$500,000 |
| M3 (Director) | 50,000–100,000 | $625,000–$1,250,000 |
| M4 (VP) | 100,000–200,000 | $1,250,000–$2,500,000 |

### 6.3 Exercise Price
ISOs are granted at the fair market value (FMV) per share as determined by the most recent 409A valuation at the time of grant. The current exercise price is **$12.50/share** (as of the October 2025 409A).

### 6.4 Exercise Window
- Options must be exercised within **90 days of leaving NovaTech** (for most separations)
- Options expire **10 years from the grant date** if not exercised
- Early exercise (83(b) election) is permitted within the first 30 days of grant

### 6.5 Annual Equity Refresh Grants
Employees who receive a performance rating of 3 or above are eligible for annual equity refresh grants in Q1 of each year, subject to board approval. Refresh grants typically represent 25–50% of the original new hire grant size and vest over 4 years from grant date (no cliff).

## 7. Wellness Stipend

NovaTech provides a **$100/month ($1,200/year) wellness stipend** for all full-time employees. The stipend is processed through **Compt** and reimbursed monthly upon submission of receipts.

**Eligible Wellness Expenses Include:**
- Gym memberships and fitness classes
- Home exercise equipment (e.g., dumbbells, resistance bands, yoga mat)
- Mental health apps (Calm, Headspace, Woebot)
- Meditation or yoga classes
- Nutrition and dietitian services
- Ergonomic items (separate from home office — e.g., standing desk mat)
- Recreational sports leagues or equipment
- Massages (up to $60/session, once per month)

**Not Eligible:** Alcohol, tobacco, recreational drugs, medical prescriptions (covered under health plan).

Stipend funds do not roll over; unused monthly amounts are forfeited at month end.

## 8. Learning & Development Budget

NovaTech invests in your professional growth with a **$1,500 per calendar year learning and development stipend** per employee.

### 8.1 Eligible Expenses
- Professional certifications and exam fees (AWS, Google Cloud, Salesforce, PMP, CPA, etc.)
- Online courses and learning platforms (Coursera, Udemy, Pluralsight, O'Reilly)
- Industry conference registration fees (not including travel — see Travel Policy)
- Professional books and publications (up to $200/year)
- External coaching or mentorship programs (pre-approved)

### 8.2 Not Eligible
- Undergraduate or MBA degree programs (separate tuition assistance program available — contact People Ops)
- Personal interest courses unrelated to professional development
- Hardware (covered by separate stipends)

### 8.3 Process
Submit requests through **Rippling** or email learning@novatechsolutions.com with a description and cost. Requests under $300 are pre-approved for eligible categories. Requests over $300 require manager approval. Reimbursement is processed within 15 business days.

### 8.4 LinkedIn Learning
All NovaTech employees have free access to **LinkedIn Learning** through the company enterprise license. Access is provisioned within your first week. LinkedIn Learning is in addition to, not counted against, the $1,500 L&D budget.

## 9. Home Office Stipend

Remote and hybrid employees receive a **one-time $800 home office setup stipend** to equip their workspace for productive work.

**Eligible Purchases:**
- External monitor(s)
- Keyboard and mouse
- Webcam and microphone
- Headset or headphones
- Desk lamp
- Desk organizers or storage
- Blue-light-blocking glasses
- Ergonomic chair accessories (lumbar support, seat cushion)

## 12. Life & Disability Insurance

### 12.1 Basic Life Insurance
NovaTech provides **company-paid basic life insurance** equal to **2x your annual base salary**, up to a maximum of $500,000, through **Lincoln Financial Group**. Coverage begins on Day 1.

### 12.2 Supplemental Life Insurance
Employees may purchase additional life insurance up to **8x their annual salary** (up to $2,000,000 maximum). Amounts over $300,000 require evidence of insurability. Premiums are employee-paid and vary by age.

Employees may also purchase life insurance for:
- Spouse/Domestic Partner: up to $100,000 (evidence of insurability required over $50,000)
- Children: $10,000 flat (no evidence of insurability required)

### 12.3 Short-Term Disability (STD)
NovaTech provides company-paid **Short-Term Disability insurance** covering:
- **Benefit:** 60% of weekly base salary
- **Maximum weekly benefit:** $2,500
- **Elimination period:** 7 calendar days
- **Benefit duration:** Up to 26 weeks

### 12.4 Long-Term Disability (LTD)
NovaTech provides company-paid **Long-Term Disability insurance** covering:
- **Benefit:** 60% of monthly base salary
- **Maximum monthly benefit:** $12,000
- **Elimination period:** 90 calendar days (after STD exhausted)
- **Benefit duration:** To age 65 (for qualifying disabilities)

## 14. Enrollment & Eligibility

### New Employee Enrollment
- **Enrollment deadline:** 30 days from hire date
- **How to enroll:** Log in to **Rippling** (rippling.com/novatech) and complete benefits elections in the New Hire Benefits portal
- **Effective date:** First day of employment (Day 1 coverage)

### Open Enrollment
- **Annual window:** October 15 – November 15
- **Effective date:** January 1 of the following year

### Qualifying Life Events (QLE)
You may change your benefits outside of Open Enrollment within **30 days** of a qualifying life event:
- Marriage, divorce, or legal separation
- Birth or adoption of a child
- Death of a dependent
- Loss of other health coverage
- Change in dependent's eligibility

Submit QLE documentation in Rippling within 30 days of the event.
