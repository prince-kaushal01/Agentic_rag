## 1. Compensation Philosophy

NovaTech Solutions designs total compensation packages to attract, retain, and motivate exceptional talent at every level of the organization. Our compensation philosophy is grounded in four principles:

**1. Market Competitive:** We target base salary at the **65th percentile** of the San Francisco Bay Area market, benchmarked annually against Radford/Aon, Levels.fyi, Mercer, and Carta compensation surveys. For total compensation (base + equity + bonus), we target the **75th percentile**.

**2. Internally Equitable:** We regularly audit compensation across gender, race/ethnicity, and tenure to identify and correct unjustified gaps. Any employee may request a compensation equity review from People Ops.

**3. Performance Differentiated:** Compensation growth is linked to performance ratings. Employees who consistently exceed expectations earn meaningfully higher compensation trajectories than those at "meets expectations."

**4. Transparent:** We share salary band ranges with all employees and publish them in all job postings. Employees may discuss their compensation with colleagues; such discussions are protected under the National Labor Relations Act.

**Total Compensation Package Components:**
- Base Salary (described in this document)
- Annual Bonus (10–20% of base, described in §8)
- Equity (ISOs, described in Benefits Guide)
- Benefits (~$27,000–$37,000 annual value, described in Benefits Guide)

## 2. Level Framework

NovaTech uses two tracks: Individual Contributor (IC) and Manager (M).

### Individual Contributor Track

## 8. Bonus Structure

NovaTech operates an annual discretionary cash bonus program for all full-time employees. Bonuses are paid in February for the prior calendar year performance, after Year-End review calibration.

### 8.1 Target Bonus as Percentage of Base Salary

| Level | Target Bonus % |
|-------|---------------|
| IC1 – IC2 | 10% of base salary |
| IC3 – IC4 | 12% of base salary |
| IC5 – IC6 | 15% of base salary |
| M1 – M2 | 15% of base salary |
| M3 | 20% of base salary |
| M4 | 25% of base salary |

### 8.2 Bonus Payout Multiplier (Based on Performance Rating)

| Performance Rating | Bonus Multiplier | Example: IC4 with $180K Base, 12% Target |
|-------------------|-----------------|----------------------------------------|
| 5 — Exceptional | 1.5x | $32,400 |
| 4 — Exceeds | 1.1x–1.2x | $23,760–$25,920 |
| 3 — Meets | 0.9x–1.0x | $19,440–$21,600 |
| 2 — Partially Meets | 0.5x–0.7x | $10,800–$15,120 |
| 1 — Does Not Meet | 0x | $0 |

Bonus multipliers also factor in company performance against the annual revenue target. If NovaTech achieves <85% of annual ARR target, the company modifier reduces the bonus pool. If NovaTech achieves >110% of ARR target, the company modifier increases the pool. Specific company modifiers are set by the Board each January.

### 8.3 Bonus Eligibility
- Employees must be actively employed on **December 31** of the performance year to receive a bonus
- Employees hired after July 1 receive a prorated bonus for their months of service
- Employees on a PIP at any point during the review period are ineligible for a bonus unless the PIP was formally closed more than 90 days before year-end
- Voluntary resignation before the February payout date forfeits bonus eligibility (unless agreed otherwise in a separation agreement)

## 9. Equity Refresh Policy

Annual equity refresh grants are awarded to eligible employees in **March** of each year, based on Year-End performance ratings and tenure. Refresh grants vest over 4 years from grant date (no cliff).

### 9.1 Refresh Grant Eligibility

| Performance Rating | Eligible for Refresh? |
|-------------------|-----------------------|
| 5 — Exceptional | Yes — enhanced grant |
| 4 — Exceeds | Yes — standard grant |
| 3 — Meets | Yes — standard grant |
| 2 — Partially Meets | No |
| 1 — Does Not Meet | No |

### 9.2 Typical Refresh Grant Size (% of New Hire Grant)

| Level | Standard Refresh | Enhanced Refresh (Rating 5) |
|-------|-----------------|---------------------------|
| IC1 – IC2 | 10–15% of original | 20–25% of original |
| IC3 – IC4 | 15–20% of original | 25–35% of original |
| IC5 – IC6 | 20–25% of original | 35–50% of original |
| M1 – M2 | 20–25% of original | 35–45% of original |
| M3 – M4 | 25–35% of original | 45–60% of original |

Refresh grants are also considered for employees whose remaining unvested equity has fallen significantly (e.g., below 12 months of value), regardless of performance rating, to maintain retention incentives.

## 10. Promotion Raise Guidelines

Promotions are effective on the first day of the month following calibration approval, typically February 1 or August 1.

Promotion salary adjustments are designed to:
1. Move the employee's salary to the **range of the new level's band** (ideally between minimum and midpoint)
2. Provide a meaningful increase that reflects the added scope and responsibility

### 10.1 Promotion Raise Range

| From Level | To Level | Minimum Increase | Typical Increase | Maximum Increase |
|------------|----------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| IC1 → IC2 | 8% | 10% | 15% |
| IC2 → IC3 | 10% | 12% | 18% |
| IC3 → IC4 | 10% | 13% | 18% |
| IC4 → IC5 | 12% | 15% | 22% |
| IC5 → IC6 | 12% | 15% | 20% |
| IC4 → M1 | 10% | 13% | 18% |
| M1 → M2 | 10% | 13% | 18% |
| M2 → M3 | 12% | 16% | 22% |
| M3 → M4 | 15% | 20% | 30% |

If the minimum increase would not bring the employee to the minimum of the new band, the raise is set to achieve band minimum, overriding the table above.

## 11. Market Adjustment Policy

NovaTech conducts an annual compensation market review every **January** using the most current Radford/Aon and Levels.fyi survey data. If market data indicates that a role's band has shifted significantly (>5% at the midpoint), People Ops will propose a band adjustment for Finance and Executive approval.

### 11.1 Employee Market Adjustments
Employees paid below the **25th percentile of their band** due to market shifts are eligible for an off-cycle market adjustment, separate from the annual merit process. Market adjustments require:
- VP of department + People Ops approval
- Finance approval (budget impact assessment)
- Effective on the next payroll cycle after approval (not retroactive)

### 11.2 Market Adjustment Exclusions
Market adjustments are not available to employees:
- On an active PIP
- Within 6 months of hire (they were hired at market rate)
- Who received a promotion in the current calendar year

## 12. Geographic Differentials

NovaTech's primary compensation market is **San Francisco Bay Area**. Employees in other locations may receive a geographic differential applied to the SF bands.

| Location | Geographic Multiplier |
|----------|----------------------|
| San Francisco Bay Area | 1.00x (baseline) |
| New York City (NYC) | 0.98x |
| Seattle | 0.93x |
| Austin, TX | 0.82x |
| Boston, MA | 0.90x |
| Chicago, IL | 0.82x |
| Denver, CO | 0.80x |
| Other US Major Metro | 0.78x |
| Other US (Remote) | 0.75x |
| London, UK | 0.87x (adjusted for GBP) |
| Other EMEA (Remote) | 0.80x |

**Example:** An IC4 Senior Software Engineer with a San Francisco band midpoint of $210,000 hired to work remotely from Austin would have an adjusted midpoint of $210,000 × 0.82 = $172,200.

Geographic rates are reviewed annually. Employees who relocate must notify People Ops 30 days in advance. Relocation within a tier does not affect salary; relocation to a lower-cost tier may result in a salary adjustment at the next review cycle (with minimum 90 days notice).
