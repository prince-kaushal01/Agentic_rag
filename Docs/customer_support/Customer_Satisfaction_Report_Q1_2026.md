## 1. Executive Summary

Q1 2026 showed solid CSAT performance overall, with an overall score of **4.2 / 5.0** against our benchmark of 4.0. Net Promoter Score (NPS) improved to **38** (from 35 in Q4 2025), continuing a positive trend after the September 2025 SLA breach dipped NPS to 28.

However, Q1 2026 introduced a significant negative trend: the **search performance issue** affecting large-document tenants (discovered January–March 2026) has begun to show measurable impact on satisfaction scores for our largest Enterprise accounts, most notably **ACME Corp** (NPS dropped from 58 in Q4 2025 to 42 in Q1 2026). This is the primary area of concern for Q2 2026.

**Headline Metrics (Q1 2026 vs. Q4 2025):**

| Metric | Q1 2026 | Q4 2025 | Change |
|--------|---------|---------|--------|
| Overall CSAT | 4.2 / 5.0 | 4.0 / 5.0 | +0.2 ↑ |

| Survey Response Rate | 34% | 29% | +5% ↑ |

## 3. Net Promoter Score (NPS) Analysis

### 3.1 Overall NPS

**NPS = % Promoters (9–10) - % Detractors (0–6)**

| Score Range | Classification | Q1 2026 Count | Q1 2026 % |
|------------|---------------|--------------|----------|
| 9–10 | Promoters | 84 | 54% |
| 7–8 | Passives | 52 | 34% |
| 0–6 | Detractors | 18 | 12% |

**NPS = 54% - 12% = +42** (survey-weighted; overall weighted NPS = **38** including non-respondent adjustment)

**NPS Benchmark:** SaaS industry average: 31. Enterprise SaaS average: 42. NovaTech target for 2026: 45.

### 3.2 NPS Trend (Last 6 Quarters)
