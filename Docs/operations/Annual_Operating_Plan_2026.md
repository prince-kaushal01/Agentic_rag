## 1. Company Strategy for 2026

### 1.1 Where We Are
NovaTech Solutions enters 2026 in a position of strength. We closed 2025 with $28.4M ARR, 87 enterprise customers, 387 full-time employees, and $23.1M in cash on hand (31 months of runway at Q4 2025 burn rate). Our Net Revenue Retention (NRR) of 116% demonstrates that the product is delivering value and customers are expanding their use.

Our foundation is solid: we are SOC 2 Type II certified, our platform delivers 99.9% uptime, and our customer NPS of 52 is above industry median for enterprise SaaS. The market opportunity in front of us is real: the enterprise knowledge management market is $8.2B and growing at 18% CAGR. Our technology differentiation (RAG architecture, multi-modal search, enterprise-grade security) is defensible and validated.

In 2026, we must capitalize on this position before the window narrows. Glean has raised $260M and is growing fast. Microsoft is deepening Copilot. The time to establish NovaTech as the definitive enterprise knowledge platform is now.

### 1.2 Three Strategic Themes for 2026

**Theme 1: Enterprise Scale**
Grow from 87 to 130 enterprise customers. Increase average contract value from $74K to $90K ACV. Invest in Sales capacity (2 new AEs), Customer Success capacity (3 new CSMs), and professional services capability.

**Theme 2: AI Differentiation**
Launch Multi-Modal Search (Q2 2026) — making NovaTech the first enterprise knowledge platform to search across text, images, and structured documents in a single interface. Achieve Gartner Magic Quadrant inclusion as a "Visionary" or better.

**Theme 3: International Expansion**
Establish commercial presence in the UK and DACH markets. Hire a London-based Account Executive. Achieve EU data residency option for compliance with GDPR and UK GDPR. Generate $800K in EMEA pipeline.

## 5. Cross-Functional Initiatives (2026)

### Initiative 1: Salesforce CRM Migration and Expansion
**Description:** NovaTech is currently using Salesforce Sales Cloud at a basic level. In 2026, we will implement Salesforce CPQ (for improved quote management), Revenue Intelligence (Clari), and full integration with HubSpot marketing automation. This initiative improves deal visibility, forecast accuracy, and revenue operations efficiency.
**Timeline:** Q1 planning and vendor selection; Q2–Q3 implementation; Q4 full rollout
**Owner:** Sophia Lee + Carlos Ramirez
**Budget:** $180,000 (implementation and first-year licensing)
**Key Deliverable:** Fully integrated CRM + CPQ live by October 1, 2026

### Initiative 2: EU Data Center Region (EMEA Expansion Prerequisite)
**Description:** To enter the EU/UK market, NovaTech must offer EU data residency for customer data. This requires deploying the NovaTech platform in the AWS eu-west-1 (Ireland) region with full isolation from US data.
**Timeline:** Design complete by March 31; deployment complete by June 30, 2026
**Owner:** Marcus Webb (Engineering) + Tom Bradley (Security) + Amanda Hartley (Legal — GDPR compliance)
**Budget:** $220,000 (AWS infrastructure + implementation engineering time)
**Key Deliverable:** EU-hosted NovaTech environment available to customers by June 30, 2026

### Initiative 3: SOC 2 Type II Annual Renewal
**Description:** NovaTech's SOC 2 Type II certification expires August 2026. The 2025–2026 audit observation period runs January–June 2026. Audit firm: Prescient Assurance.
**Timeline:** Audit period January–June; fieldwork July; report issuance August 2026
**Owner:** Tom Bradley (CISO)
**Budget:** $45,000 (audit fee — already contracted)
**Key Deliverable:** Clean SOC 2 Type II report issued by August 31, 2026

### Initiative 4: Series C Fundraise
**Description:** NovaTech will pursue a Series C raise of approximately $40M to fund EMEA expansion, accelerated hiring, and R&D investment in multi-modal AI capabilities. Banker: Qatalyst Partners (engaged).
**Timeline:** Preparation Q1–Q2; investor outreach Q3; close Q4 2026
**Owner:** David Huang (CEO) + Carlos Ramirez (CFO)
**Target:** $40M at ≥$250M post-money valuation

## 6. Risk Scenarios (2026)

### 6.1 Bull Case (30% Probability)
**Assumption:** Multi-Modal Search creates significant competitive differentiation; Gartner MQ inclusion in H2 drives awareness surge; Series C closes at favorable valuation ($300M+); EMEA pipeline accelerates beyond forecast.
**Bull Case ARR:** $46M by December 31, 2026
**Bull Case Headcount:** 435 by year-end

### 6.2 Base Case (50% Probability)
**Assumption:** Plan execution is largely on track; Multi-Modal Search achieves target adoption; Series C closes at $250M valuation; EMEA contributes $800K pipeline but no closed deals in 2026.
**Base Case ARR:** $42M by December 31, 2026 (plan target)
**Base Case Headcount:** 420 by year-end

### 6.3 Bear Case (20% Probability)
**Assumption:** Enterprise sales cycle extends due to macroeconomic headwinds; Multi-Modal Search delayed to Q3; Series C delayed to early 2027; Glean wins 3–5 competitive deals we expected to win.
**Bear Case ARR:** $36M by December 31, 2026
**Bear Case Actions:** Pause 30% of planned Q4 hiring; reduce discretionary marketing spend; accelerate Series C process.
