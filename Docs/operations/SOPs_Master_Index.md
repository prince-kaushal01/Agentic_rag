## SOP Naming and Version Control Guidelines

- **SOP IDs** are assigned by the Operations team and must not be changed once issued. If an SOP is substantially rewritten (>50% new content), a new SOP_ID is issued and the old SOP is deprecated.
- **Version numbering:** Major version (1, 2, 3) for substantial rewrites; minor version (.1, .2) for edits and updates.
- **Storage:** All SOPs are stored in Notion under Operations > SOPs > [Department]. Only Operations and the document owner can modify published SOPs.
- **Effective Date:** Each SOP must include an "Effective Date" in its header. A revised SOP does not take effect until the new version is published in Notion.
- **Acknowledgment:** Employees are notified of SOP revisions via Slack #policy-updates. SOPs requiring explicit acknowledgment (typically HR and Security policies) are tracked in Rippling.
