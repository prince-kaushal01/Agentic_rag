## 2. Building Access Procedures

### 2.1 Employee Access
- All employees receive a **Brivo smart access badge** on their first day (provisioned by IT/Operations during onboarding).
- Badge provides access to: Building lobby (24/7), elevator bank to Floor 14, NovaTech main entrance (24/7), server closet (restricted — Engineering and IT only), and equipment storage.
- Badge is programmed for the employee's standard work location. Remote employees who visit receive a temporary 5-day guest badge.
- Lost badges must be reported immediately to IT (it@novatech.io or Slack #it-help). Badge will be deactivated within 1 hour.
- Do not prop doors open or allow tailgating (piggybacking entry behind another person without scanning).

### 2.2 After-Hours Access
- The office is accessible 24/7 to all employees with a valid Brivo badge.
- After-hours building entry (before 6 AM or after 8 PM) may require additional identity verification at the building lobby front desk.
- Please notify the building security desk when working after 9 PM.

### 2.3 Contractor and Vendor Access
- External contractors (IT vendors, facilities vendors, maintenance) must be registered with building security before arrival.
- Cassandra Baines (cassandra.baines@novatech.io) manages all contractor access registration.
- Contractors receive a visitor badge from building security; must be escorted to work areas by a NovaTech employee.
- Contractor badges do not provide after-hours access unless pre-approved in writing by VP of Operations.

## 3. Visitor Management Policy

### 3.1 Visitor Registration
- All non-employee visitors (customers, prospects, partners, journalists, candidates) must be registered in advance.
- Employees hosting visitors must register them in **Envoy** (the visitor management system) at least 24 hours before the visit. Walk-in registrations are permitted but require host employee to meet visitor in the lobby immediately.
- Registration collects: visitor name, organization, purpose, expected duration, host employee name.

### 3.2 Visitor Check-In
- Visitors check in at the Envoy iPad kiosk in the building lobby (14th floor reception area).
- Visitor receives a printed visitor badge (valid for the day only).
- Host receives a Slack notification and SMS when visitor checks in.
- Visitors must be escorted at all times in areas beyond the main reception and designated meeting rooms A–D.

### 3.3 Visitor Restrictions
- Visitors may NOT access: Engineering work area (Desks E01–E40), IT server room, CEO/executive private offices (without explicit invitation), or any area marked "Restricted."
- Visitors should not be given unattended access to any NovaTech device or system.
- Photography and recording inside the office requires advance approval from Marketing (Priya Mehta).

### 3.4 Customer/Prospect Visits
For customer or prospect visits, please notify Cassandra Baines at least 48 hours in advance so she can:
- Prepare the main conference room and coordinate catering
- Print agenda and company overview materials if needed
- Arrange parking validation (limited validation available; request from Cassandra)

## 4. Mail and Package Handling

- All mail and packages are received at the building mail room (Ground Floor, managed by building management).
- Building management sorts mail daily and delivers to NovaTech Floor 14 reception area by 10:00 AM.

- Outbound mail: FedEx pickup is daily at 4:30 PM from the reception area (FedEx account number is on the shipping station — do not use for personal shipments).

## 6. Desk Booking System (Hybrid Workers)

NovaTech operates a hybrid work model. Full-time in-office employees (Engineering leads, Operations, Executive team) have permanently assigned desks. All other employees use the hot desk booking system.

### 6.1 How to Book a Desk
- System: **Skedda** (app.skedda.com/novatech or Slack /skedda command)
- **Advance booking window:** Up to 14 days in advance; minimum 48 hours in advance
- **Walk-in desks:** 10 desks on the south wing are walk-in (first come, first served; no booking required)
- **Book per day:** Select your desk, date, and start time. Confirmation sent to your calendar.
- Desks are released if not checked in via Skedda by 10:00 AM on the booked day.

### 6.2 Desk Booking Etiquette
- Clean the desk before you leave (wipe down surface; return chair to standard position).
- Do not leave personal items at a hot desk overnight. Unclaimed items are moved to lost and found by 6 PM.
- If you booked a desk and can't come in, cancel via Skedda at least 6 hours before your booking to release it for others.
- Do not "save" desks for colleagues by booking on their behalf.

## 7. Meeting Room Booking

### 7.1 How to Book
- All 12 conference rooms are bookable via **Google Calendar** (room calendars are shared company-wide).
- Search for the room in the "Add rooms" field when creating a calendar event.
- Rooms A (20-person) and B (12-person) require booking at least 2 hours in advance.
- Rooms C–L: can be booked same-day.

### 7.2 Meeting Room Etiquette
- **End on time.** If your meeting goes long, check whether the room is booked next before extending.
- **Clean up after yourself.** Remove trash, erase whiteboards, return chairs to standard positions, and disconnect any external devices from AV systems.
- **No food in Rooms C–L** (small rooms — keep them clean). Food is allowed in Rooms A and B.
- **No shows:** If you have a room booked and don't use it within 15 minutes, cancel the booking so others can use it. Skedda sends a reminder.
- If you need to hold a room for a recurring meeting, use Google Calendar recurring event. Do not use "workaround" bookings.

### 7.3 External Meeting Setup
For meetings with external visitors (customers, candidates), Cassandra Baines can arrange:
- Pre-meeting room setup (name cards, whiteboard supplies, water, coffee)
- Catering for meetings > 4 people or lasting > 2 hours (submit request 48 hours in advance)
- Visitor Wi-Fi credentials (NovaTech-Guest network password changes weekly; current password in Slack #office-info)

## 8. Kitchen Rules

- **Label your food.** All items in shared refrigerators must have your name + date. Unlabeled food is discarded every Friday evening.
- **Refrigerator cleaning:** Building services cleans refrigerators every Friday evening. All food is removed.
- **Shared supplies:** Coffee, tea, sparkling water, and basic snacks are provided by NovaTech. Do not remove in bulk.
- **Dishwasher:** Run it when full; start it at end of day. Dishes must be rinsed before loading.
- **Espresso bar:** Blue Bottle espresso machine is self-serve. Use the app on the machine. It is calibrated weekly by Blue Bottle — please do not adjust settings.
- **Alcohol:** Beer and wine are available in the kitchen refrigerator (work social only; do not take home; do not consume before noon).
- **Allergies:** Snacks may contain nuts, dairy, gluten. If you have a severe allergy, store personal food on the labeled "Allergy" shelf (top shelf, left refrigerator).

## 9. Emergency Procedures

### 9.1 Fire / Evacuation
- **Alarm:** Pull nearest fire alarm pull station OR call 911.
- **Evacuation route:** Primary exit via stairwell A (northeast) to the street level. Secondary exit via stairwell B (southwest). DO NOT USE ELEVATORS.
- **Assembly point:** Northeast corner of Howard St and Beale St (across from building).
- **Floor Wardens:** Cassandra Baines (north wing) and Tom Bradley (south wing). Alternates posted on emergency plan (on each exit door and at IT desk).
- **Visitors and mobility-impaired employees:** Floor wardens ensure all visitors and anyone needing mobility assistance are accounted for. "Areas of Rescue Assistance" are marked at each stairwell door.

### 9.2 Medical Emergency
- Call 911 immediately. Provide address: 535 Mission Street, Floor 14, San Francisco, CA 94105.
- AED (defibrillator) is located at the IT desk (northeast corner) and at the main reception area. Both units are inspected monthly.
- First Aid kit is at the kitchen (north wall) and at the IT desk.
- CPR-certified employees (current list posted in #emergency-contacts Slack channel).

### 9.3 Active Threat / Lockdown
- Call 911. Do NOT pull fire alarm (do not send people into hallways).
- Shelter-in-place procedure: Lock conference room doors (all rooms have interior locks); turn off lights; silence phones; move away from windows/doors; await instruction from law enforcement.
- Building security notifies tenants via PA system and building app.

## 11. Parking and Commuter Information

### Parking
- NovaTech does NOT provide dedicated parking. The building has no tenant-reserved parking.
- Nearest public parking garages: 500 Howard Street Garage (~0.2 miles), SFMTA-operated Fremont Street Garage (~0.3 miles).
- NovaTech does NOT reimburse for parking expenses (commute policy).

### Commuter Benefits
- **Pre-tax transit:** NovaTech provides a pre-tax commuter benefit of up to $315/month via Rippling Benefits for Clipper card loading, BART, Muni, and other qualifying transit.
- **Caltrain:** Monthly passes can be pre-purchased with pre-tax dollars via the commuter benefit program.
- **Biking:** Bike storage available in building B1 (request a permit from Cassandra Baines). Showers available on B2 for cyclists.
- **Lyft Business:** Employees working past 9 PM may expense a Lyft home (up to $40). Log in Ramp with receipt.
