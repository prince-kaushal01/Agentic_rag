## 1. Executive Summary

NovaTech Solutions's SEO program has delivered consistent growth since its formal launch in Q3 2024. As of March 2026, organic search generates 34% of all website traffic and 34% of all MQLs—making it our highest-returning marketing channel on a cost-per-MQL basis ($412/MQL vs. $924/MQL for paid search). The program is built around four keyword clusters, a content pillar strategy, and an aggressive link-building program.

**Q1 2026 Organic Search Performance:**
- Unique organic visits: 48,280 (+41% YoY)
- Organic MQLs: 288 (+52% YoY)
- Average organic cost per MQL: $412
- Keywords in top 10 (primary targets): 12 of 20
- Domain Authority (Moz): 54 (up from 49 Q1 2025)

**2026 Annual Organic Traffic Goal:** 200,000 unique visits by Q4 2026 (run-rate basis)

## 6. Link Building Strategy

### 6.1 Approach
NovaTech's link building strategy focuses on three methods:
1. **Digital PR / Data-driven content** — Original research (e.g., "The Knowledge Gap Report") earns links from industry publications and news outlets.
2. **Thought leadership contributions** — Guest posts and contributor columns on CIO.com, InfoWorld, TechTarget, and similar publications.
3. **Partner and integration links** — Listing on Salesforce AppExchange, ServiceNow Store, and Microsoft Azure Marketplace provides high-authority links.
