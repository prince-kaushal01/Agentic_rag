## 1. Introduction & Brand Foundation

NovaTech Solutions was founded in 2018 with a simple but powerful conviction: enterprise knowledge should not be locked away in silos. Today, as a leading AI-powered knowledge management and enterprise search platform serving hundreds of the world's most complex organizations, our brand must communicate who we are, what we believe, and how we do it—consistently, across every touchpoint.

This document is the authoritative reference for anyone creating content, designing materials, or representing NovaTech Solutions in any public or internal capacity. Adherence to these guidelines is mandatory for all employees, agencies, contractors, and partners. Questions or exceptions must be submitted to the Brand team at brand@novatech.io.

## 2. Mission, Vision & Values

### Company Mission
**"Making enterprise knowledge accessible and actionable."**

This mission statement must appear verbatim when cited in company documents, presentations, and marketing materials. It should not be paraphrased in external communications without Brand team approval.

### Company Vision
We envision a world where every employee in every enterprise organization can find the right information at the right moment—and act on it with confidence. NovaTech Solutions is building the intelligence layer that makes this possible.

### Core Values
1. **Clarity** — We communicate simply and precisely, even when the subject is complex.
2. **Trust** — Our customers trust us with their most sensitive knowledge assets. We earn that trust every day.
3. **Innovation with Purpose** — We build what matters, not what impresses. Every feature serves a real human need.
4. **Inclusivity** — Enterprise knowledge should be accessible to everyone in an organization, not just technical users.
5. **Accountability** — We own our outcomes, our mistakes, and our growth.

## 3. Brand Personality

NovaTech Solutions's brand personality sits at the intersection of four core attributes. Every piece of content we produce should express all four, in balance.

### 3.1 Professional
We are serious about enterprise software. Our communications should reflect the weight of the decisions our customers make—without being stuffy, cold, or unapproachable. We speak with authority because we have earned it.

**In practice:** Use precise, complete sentences. Avoid slang. Cite data when making claims. Do not use superlatives ("best," "fastest," "most powerful") without evidence.

### 3.2 Innovative
We are building at the frontier of AI and enterprise search. Our brand should signal that we are ahead of the curve—without resorting to hype or buzzword soup.

**In practice:** Lead with specific capabilities, not generic "AI-powered" messaging. Reference real technology (RAG, vector search, LLM integration) when appropriate for the audience. Speak to what's possible now, not vague future promises.

### 3.3 Trustworthy
Enterprise buyers are skeptical. They should be. Our brand must project reliability, security-consciousness, and longevity. NovaTech Solutions has never had a data breach. We maintain 99.9% uptime SLAs. We publish our security certifications.

**In practice:** Provide proof wherever possible (case studies, certifications, customer logos). Acknowledge limitations honestly. Never oversell.

### 3.4 Clear
The irony of a knowledge management company producing unclear content is not lost on us. Every sentence, chart, and user interface element should communicate exactly one thing, precisely.

**In practice:** Cut filler phrases ("in order to," "the fact that," "it is important to note"). Use active voice. Favor short paragraphs. Put the most important information first.

## 4. Voice & Tone Guidelines

### 4.1 Brand Voice (Constant)
Our voice does not change. It is:
- **Direct** — We say what we mean. No hedging, no corporate-speak.
- **Knowledgeable** — We know our domain deeply. We share that knowledge generously.
- **Empathetic** — We understand how hard it is to run enterprise operations. We don't minimize problems.
- **Confident, not arrogant** — We believe in what we've built. We don't need to tear down competitors to prove it.

### 4.2 Tone (Contextual)
Tone adapts to context, but voice remains constant.

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Product documentation | Precise, instructional | "To configure SSO, navigate to Settings → Security → Authentication." |
| Marketing copy | Inspiring, benefit-led | "Find what your team needs, in seconds—not hours." |
| Customer support | Warm, solution-focused | "We understand this is frustrating. Here's exactly how we'll fix it." |
| Executive communications | Formal, strategic | "In Q1 2026, NovaTech Solutions achieved 28% year-over-year growth in organic traffic, reflecting sustained brand investment." |
| Social media (LinkedIn) | Professional, conversational | "Knowledge hoarding is costing enterprises millions. Here's what the data says." |
| Crisis/incident comms | Calm, transparent, action-oriented | "We are aware of the issue affecting search indexing. Our engineering team is actively working on resolution. ETA: 2 hours." |

### 4.3 Voice Don'ts
- Do NOT use: "leverage" (say "use"), "synergize," "paradigm shift," "bleeding edge," "move the needle," "circle back," "at the end of the day."
- Do NOT use passive voice except where the actor is genuinely unknown.
- Do NOT use exclamation marks in B2B content (one exception: product launch announcements).
- Do NOT use emojis in formal documents, press releases, or enterprise-facing collateral.
- Do NOT make claims about competitors by name without Legal approval.

## 5. Logo Usage

### 5.1 Primary Logo
The NovaTech Solutions primary logo consists of two elements:
- **The Wordmark:** "NovaTech Solutions" set in Inter SemiBold, with "Nova" in Navy (#1B2B5E) and "Tech Solutions" in Electric Blue (#2563EB).
- **The Symbol:** An abstract "N" formed by two overlapping chevrons, rendered in a gradient from Navy (#1B2B5E) to Electric Blue (#2563EB), representing the flow of knowledge through an organization.

Primary logo files are stored in the Brand Asset Portal (brand.novatech.io/assets). Approved file formats: SVG (preferred), PNG (300 DPI minimum for print), PDF.

### 5.2 Logo Variants
| Variant | Usage |
|---|---|
| Primary (symbol + wordmark, horizontal) | Default for all marketing materials |
| Stacked (symbol above wordmark) | Square formats: app icons, trade show badges, social profile images |
| Symbol only | Favicon, app icon, embossed/embroidered merchandise where wordmark is not legible |
| Wordmark only | Internal documents, email signatures, co-branded placements where symbol creates clutter |
| Reversed (white on Navy) | Dark backgrounds only |
| Monochrome (all Navy) | One-color print applications, engraving |
| Monochrome (all white) | One-color reversed applications |

### 5.3 Correct Logo Usage
- Always use logo files downloaded from the Brand Asset Portal. Do not recreate the logo.
- Maintain clear space of at least 1× the symbol height on all sides of the logo.
- The minimum logo width for digital use is 120px (horizontal variant).
- The minimum logo width for print is 1.25 inches (horizontal variant).
- The logo may be placed on white, light gray (#F8FAFC), Navy (#1B2B5E), or approved photographic backgrounds.

### 5.4 Incorrect Logo Usage (Prohibited)
The following are strictly prohibited:
- **Do not** stretch, compress, or distort the logo in any dimension.
- **Do not** rotate the logo.
- **Do not** change any logo colors, including the gradient in the symbol.
- **Do not** add drop shadows, outlines, glows, or any effects to the logo.
- **Do not** place the primary logo on a busy or photographic background without the reversed variant.
- **Do not** use the symbol alone in external brand contexts where the NovaTech Solutions name has not already been established.
- **Do not** recreate the logo using text characters or emoji.
- **Do not** use old logo versions (pre-2024 rebrand). Check the Asset Portal version number.
- **Do not** modify the tagline or add new taglines below the logo without Brand approval.

### 6.3 Color Usage Rules
- **60-30-10 Rule:** In any designed piece, approximately 60% white/light backgrounds, 30% Navy, 10% Electric Blue.
- Electric Blue should never be used as a background color for large areas.
- Status colors (green, amber, red) must not be used decoratively.
- Do not introduce colors outside this palette without Brand team approval.
- Minimum contrast ratios: 4.5:1 for body text (WCAG AA), 3:1 for large text.

## 7. Typography

### 7.1 Primary Typeface — Inter (Headings)
- **Font Family:** Inter (Google Fonts / licensed via Adobe Fonts)
- **Usage:** All headings (H1–H4), navigation, CTAs, data tables
- **Weights used:** Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600), Bold (700)
- **Fallback:** System UI, -apple-system, sans-serif

| Style | Weight | Size (Desktop) | Size (Mobile) | Line Height |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Bold (700) | 48px | 32px | 1.2 |
| H2 | SemiBold (600) | 36px | 28px | 1.25 |
| H3 | SemiBold (600) | 28px | 22px | 1.3 |
| H4 | Medium (500) | 22px | 18px | 1.35 |

### 7.2 Secondary Typeface — DM Sans (Body)
- **Font Family:** DM Sans (Google Fonts)
- **Usage:** All body copy, captions, form fields, tooltips, email body text
- **Weights used:** Regular (400), Medium (500)
- **Fallback:** Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif

| Style | Weight | Size | Line Height |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body Large | Regular (400) | 18px | 1.6 |
| Body Default | Regular (400) | 16px | 1.6 |
| Body Small | Regular (400) | 14px | 1.5 |
| Caption | Medium (500) | 12px | 1.4 |

### 7.3 Monospace — JetBrains Mono (Code)
- **Usage:** Code snippets, API documentation, technical specifications, terminal output
- **Fallback:** Courier New, monospace

### 7.4 Typography Rules
- Never use more than two typefaces in a single document (Inter + DM Sans is the standard pairing).
- All caps text should use letter-spacing of 0.08em minimum.
- Do not use condensed variants of any approved typeface.
- Do not underline text for decorative purposes—underline is reserved for hyperlinks only.

## 8. Photography Style

NovaTech Solutions photography should feel authentic, modern, and enterprise-appropriate.

### 8.1 Approved Style
- **People:** Diverse teams of professionals in real work settings. Candid or lightly directed—never stiff, never stock-photo cheesy. Ethnicities, ages, and genders should reflect global enterprise diversity.
- **Environments:** Open, modern offices with natural light; collaboration spaces; urban skylines for location photography.
- **Technology:** Close-ups of screens showing the NovaTech platform (real screenshots preferred); tasteful device mockups.
- **Color Treatment:** Images should feel slightly cooler/desaturated to align with the Navy/Blue palette. A subtle Navy color overlay (10–15% opacity) may be applied to align hero photography with brand.

### 8.2 Prohibited Photography
- Generic stock photos of people pointing at laptops, shaking hands, or standing in front of whiteboards.
- Images that do not reflect workplace diversity.
- Photos with visible competitor brand logos or software.
- Low-resolution images (below 72 DPI for digital, below 300 DPI for print).

## 9. Icon Usage

- **Icon Library:** We use the Lucide icon set as our primary icon library (lucide.dev), supplemented by custom NovaTech icons for platform-specific concepts.
- **Icon weight:** 1.5px stroke, consistent across all icons.
- **Icon color:** Icons must be rendered in Navy, Electric Blue, Slate Gray, or White—matching the surrounding content context.
- **Icon sizing:** Minimum 16px (small UI contexts), 24px (standard), 32px (feature/section callouts), 48px (illustration-style).
- Do not mix Lucide icons with icons from other libraries (Heroicons, Font Awesome, etc.) in the same deliverable.
- Custom NovaTech icons are available in the Brand Asset Portal under /icons/custom.

## 10. Co-Branding Guidelines

When NovaTech Solutions appears alongside a partner or customer brand:

### 10.1 Approved Co-Branding
- Partner logos must be displayed at equal or smaller scale than the NovaTech logo.
- A neutral separator (a vertical rule in Slate Gray) must appear between the two logos.
- The NovaTech logo must always appear on the left side in horizontal lockups.
- Co-branded materials must be submitted to brand@novatech.io and the partner's brand team for approval before production.

### 10.2 Prohibited Co-Branding
- Do not blend, overlap, or combine the NovaTech logo with any partner logo.
- Do not use co-branded materials for customer-specific events without a current active contract.
- Technology partner logos (Salesforce, ServiceNow, Microsoft) may appear in the "Integrations" section of materials only—they are not co-brand partners.

### 10.3 Partner-Tier Co-Branding Rights
| Partner Tier | Co-branded Sales Deck | Co-branded Case Study | Joint Press Release |
|---|---|---|---|
| Registered | No | No | No |
| Silver | Yes | Upon approval | No |
| Gold | Yes | Yes | Upon approval |
| Platinum | Yes | Yes | Yes |

All approved brand assets are stored in the NovaTech Brand Asset Portal at **brand.novatech.io**. Access requires a company Google account. Assets are organized by:

For questions about brand guidelines, asset access, or exceptions, contact the Brand team at brand@novatech.io or Slack #brand-studio.
