## 1. Program Overview

The NovaTech Solutions Partner Program is designed to create mutually beneficial, long-term relationships with technology partners, resellers, and referral partners who can help NovaTech Solutions expand market reach, accelerate customer success, and deliver more value to enterprise customers. The program was redesigned in 2025 and Version 2.0 introduces updated tier thresholds, enhanced MDF (Marketing Development Fund) allocations, and a new technology partner certification track.

**Program Goals (2026):**
- Generate $4.2M in partner-influenced pipeline
- Close $2.1M in partner-sourced revenue
- Certify 15 new partner organizations
- Activate 3 new technology integrations (Salesforce Health Cloud, ServiceNow HRSD, Workday)

## 2. Partner Tiers — Reseller and Referral Partners

### Tier 1: Registered Partner
**Description:** Entry-level partnership; primarily referral relationships with minimal investment from either party.

**Requirements:**
- Sign NovaTech Partner Agreement (Reseller Addendum not required)
- Complete NovaTech Foundation Certification (online; 4 hours; free)
- Maintain 1 certified individual at the organization
- Minimum annual referral activity: 2 qualified referrals per year

**Benefits:**
- **Referral margin:** 10% of first-year ACV on referred deals
- **Partner portal access:** Basic (deal registration, partner overview, certification portal)
- **Sales support:** Access to standard sales deck and data sheet library
- **MDF funds:** Not eligible
- **Co-selling:** Reactive only (NovaTech AE responds to referrals)
- **Joint marketing:** Not included
- **Technical resources:** Basic documentation access

---

### Tier 2: Silver Partner
**Description:** Active referral or reseller partner with demonstrated NovaTech expertise and consistent pipeline contribution.

**Requirements:**
- Sign NovaTech Partner Agreement + Reseller Addendum (if reselling)
- Achieve Silver Certification: 2 certified individuals, including at least 1 Technical Certification holder
- Minimum annual referred/resold ARR: $150,000
- Maintain a dedicated NovaTech "Champion" contact at the partner organization
- Participate in quarterly partner business review (QBR)

**Benefits:**
- **Referral/reseller margin:** 15% of first-year ACV
- **Partner portal access:** Silver (deal registration, partner resources, pipeline reporting, co-marketing templates)
- **Sales support:** Dedicated NovaTech SDR support for co-selling (up to 10 hours/month)
- **MDF funds:** $5,000/year (50% reimbursement on approved co-marketing activities)
- **Co-selling:** Proactive — assigned NovaTech AE for joint opportunities
- **Joint marketing:** Co-branded sales deck and one-pager upon request
- **Technical resources:** Partner sandbox environment, technical documentation, Slack partner channel

---

### Tier 3: Gold Partner
**Description:** Strategic reseller or referral partner with significant NovaTech practice and proven customer delivery capability.

**Requirements:**
- Complete all Silver requirements
- Achieve Gold Certification: 4 certified individuals (including 2 Technical Certification holders, 1 Professional Services Certification holder)
- Minimum annual referred/resold ARR: $500,000
- Maintain a dedicated NovaTech business unit or practice area
- Deliver at least 2 customer implementations independently (with NovaTech oversight)
- Participate in monthly partner business review

**Benefits:**
- **Referral/reseller margin:** 20% of first-year ACV; 10% on years 2–3 (renewal bonus)
- **Partner portal access:** Gold (all Silver benefits + executive reporting, customer health dashboards for jointly managed accounts)
- **Sales support:** Named NovaTech Channel Account Manager (CAM); co-selling on all qualified opportunities
- **MDF funds:** $20,000/year (60% reimbursement on approved activities)
- **Co-selling:** Proactive co-selling; joint QBR with NovaTech VP of Sales
- **Joint marketing:** Co-branded campaign templates, case study co-authoring, partner logo on NovaTech website
- **Technical resources:** Priority access to Engineering for integration questions; early access to beta features
- **Events:** 2 complimentary passes to NovaTech Knowledge Summit; partner booth opportunity at EMEA User Conference

---

### Tier 4: Platinum Partner
**Description:** Elite strategic partner. Deep integration with NovaTech's go-to-market, delivery, and product roadmap. Fewer than 5 Platinum partners globally.

**Requirements:**
- Complete all Gold requirements
- Achieve Platinum Certification: 8+ certified individuals across technical, professional services, and sales tracks
- Minimum annual referred/resold ARR: $1,500,000
- Dedicated NovaTech practice with named practice lead
- Executive sponsor relationship (partner C-level / NovaTech VP of Sales)
- Annual joint business plan co-developed with NovaTech
- Deliver customer NPS ≥ 50 on jointly managed accounts

**Benefits:**
- **Referral/reseller margin:** 25% of first-year ACV; 15% on years 2–3
- **Partner portal access:** Platinum (all Gold benefits + roadmap preview, custom analytics)
- **Sales support:** Named Channel Account Manager + Solutions Engineer support
- **MDF funds:** $60,000/year (75% reimbursement); priority approval process
- **Co-selling:** Executive-level joint selling; NovaTech CEO available for strategic deals >$250K
- **Joint marketing:** Joint press release rights; co-authored white papers; speaking slot at NovaTech Knowledge Summit
- **Technical resources:** Dedicated Partner Engineering contact; product roadmap influence through quarterly partner advisory call
- **Events:** 4 passes to NovaTech events; sponsored speaking opportunity at EMEA User Conference
- **Other:** Listed as "Platinum Partner" on novatech.io/partners with logo and case study link

## 5. Referral Partner Program

The Referral Partner Program is the simplest form of partnership—designed for consultants, advisors, and small agencies who interact with NovaTech's target buyers but are not set up as formal resellers.

**How it works:**
1. Referral partner submits a referral lead via partners.novatech.io/referrals
2. NovaTech AE contacts the referred company within 1 business day
3. If the referral converts to a closed-won deal within 180 days, the partner earns a referral fee
4. Referral fee: 10% of first-year ACV (paid within 60 days of deal close)

**Referral Partner Requirements:**
- Sign NovaTech Referral Agreement (1-page; available at partners.novatech.io)
- No certification required
- W-9 or W-8BEN required for payment processing

**Active Referral Partners (Q1 2026):** 12 active referral partners generated 136 MQLs in Q1 2026. Top performers: Strategic Ops Advisory (San Francisco), KM Consulting Group (New York), Brightpath IT Advisory (Chicago).

## 6. Reseller Terms

Resellers purchase NovaTech licenses at the partner margin and resell to end customers at market rates. Key terms:

- **Pricing:** Resellers purchase at a discount from NovaTech list price (per tier: 10/15/20/25%)
- **Payment terms:** Net 30 from NovaTech invoice
- **Minimum order:** $25,000 ACV per deal
- **Customer contracts:** Resellers may use their own order forms, but the end customer must sign NovaTech's standard Terms of Service; NovaTech provides a standard EULA for reseller use
- **Support:** Resellers handle Tier 1 support for jointly sold accounts; NovaTech handles Tier 2/3; support escalation via partners.novatech.io
- **Non-compete:** Resellers may sell competing products but may not actively position NovaTech against itself in multi-vendor proposals
- **Deal registration:** All deals must be registered in the partner portal within 5 business days of first customer meeting to lock margin protection; unregistered deals are not margin-protected

## 7. Marketing Development Fund (MDF) Guidelines

MDF funds are available to Silver, Gold, and Platinum partners for approved co-marketing activities.

**Approved Activities:**
- Joint webinars and events
- Co-branded digital advertising (LinkedIn, Google)
- Trade show sponsorships (NovaTech logo must appear on materials)
- Direct mail campaigns to partner's customer list
- Content creation (case studies, white papers) with joint attribution

**Not Approved:**
- Partner-only marketing without NovaTech branding
- Salesperson compensation or commissions
- General operating expenses
- Entertainment and meals

**MDF Request Process:**
1. Submit MDF request via partners.novatech.io/mdf at least 21 days before the activity
2. NovaTech Partner Ops reviews within 5 business days
3. Upon approval, partner executes activity and submits proof of execution + invoices
4. Reimbursement processed within 45 days of approved proof of execution

## 8. Partner Certification Program

### Certification Tracks

**Track 1: NovaTech Sales Certification**
- Format: Online; 6 hours total; includes video modules + final assessment
- Topics: NovaTech platform overview, buyer personas, competitive positioning, deal qualification, objection handling
- Passing score: 80%
- Renewal: Annual
- Cost: Free for all registered partners

**Track 2: NovaTech Technical Certification**
- Format: Online + hands-on lab; 12 hours total
- Topics: NovaTech architecture, API integration, connector configuration, security and compliance configuration, SSO setup
- Passing score: 80%; hands-on lab must pass all test cases
- Renewal: Annual (with delta exam for major platform updates)
- Cost: Free for Silver+ partners; $500/individual for Registered partners

**Track 3: NovaTech Professional Services Certification**
- Format: Online + proctored implementation lab; 20 hours total
- Topics: Implementation methodology, project management for NovaTech rollouts, change management, customer onboarding, performance optimization
- Passing score: 85%
- Prerequisite: Technical Certification
- Renewal: Every 2 years
- Cost: Free for Gold+ partners; $1,200/individual for Silver partners

**Partner Certification Dashboard:** Available at partners.novatech.io/certifications — shows all certified individuals, expiry dates, and which certifications each individual holds.

## 10. Co-Marketing Guidelines

When producing jointly branded content:
- NovaTech logo usage must follow the NovaTech Brand Guidelines (see Brand_Guidelines.md)
- Partner brand guidelines take precedence on partner-produced materials; NovaTech brand guidelines apply on NovaTech-produced materials
- All co-branded materials must be approved by NovaTech Marketing (brand@novatech.io) before publication
- NovaTech may request removal of co-branded content if the partner violates the Partner Agreement
- Joint press releases require approval from NovaTech VP of Marketing AND VP of Sales
