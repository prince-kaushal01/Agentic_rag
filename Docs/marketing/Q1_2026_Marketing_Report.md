# NovaTech Solutions — Q1 2026 Marketing Performance Report
**Period:** January 1, 2026 – March 31, 2026
**Prepared by:** Rachel Torres, VP of Marketing
**Date Issued:** April 10, 2026
**Distribution:** Executive Leadership Team, Board of Directors (Summary Section Only)

## 1. Executive Summary

Q1 2026 marked a strong start to the year for the NovaTech Solutions marketing organization. We exceeded targets across our primary demand generation metrics, driven by continued investment in content marketing, improved paid search efficiency, and a successful virtual event in March. Total pipeline generated from marketing-sourced opportunities reached **$3.2M**, against a Q1 target of $2.8M—a 14.3% overachievement.

Key headline metrics:
- **Website Traffic:** 142,000 unique visits (+28% YoY, +6% vs. Q4 2025)
- **Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs):** 847 (+35% YoY, +8% vs. Q4 2025)
- **MQL-to-SQL Conversion Rate:** 18% (target: 16%)
- **Pipeline Generated:** $3.2M (target: $2.8M)
- **Deals Influenced:** $1.8M closed-won with marketing touchpoints
- **Marketing Budget Utilization:** 94% ($987K of $1.05M Q1 budget)

## 4. Campaign Performance

### Campaign 1: "The Knowledge Gap" Thought Leadership Campaign (Jan–Feb)
- **Objective:** Top-of-funnel awareness among VP Operations and CTO personas
- **Channels:** LinkedIn Sponsored Content, programmatic display (Demandbase), organic social
- **Budget:** $128,000
- **Impressions:** 2.4M
- **Clicks:** 38,400 (CTR: 1.6%)
- **MQLs Attributed:** 112
- **Cost per MQL:** $1,143
- **Key Asset:** "The Knowledge Gap Report 2026" — 1,847 downloads
- **Notes:** LinkedIn performed 22% better than benchmark on CPC; display underperformed—reduced budget in Feb.

### Campaign 2: Paid Search — Enterprise Search Intent (Ongoing)
- **Objective:** Capture high-intent searchers evaluating enterprise search and knowledge management platforms
- **Budget Utilized Q1:** $219,000
- **Impressions:** 1.1M
- **Clicks:** 44,200 (CTR: 4.0%)
- **Conversions (form fills):** 312
- **MQLs Attributed:** 237
- **Cost per MQL:** $924
- **Top Performing Ad Groups:** "enterprise search platform" ($41 CPC, 6.2% CTR), "knowledge management software" ($38 CPC, 5.8% CTR)
- **Notes:** Negative keyword list expansion in February reduced wasted spend by 18%.

### Campaign 3: "NovaTech Knowledge Summit" Virtual Event (March 18–19, 2026)
- **Objective:** Mid-funnel pipeline generation for enterprise accounts ≥500 employees
- **Format:** 2-day virtual conference; 6 sessions including keynote by CEO David Huang
- **Budget:** $187,000
- **Registrations:** 1,842
- **Live Attendees:** 1,104 (60% attendance rate)
- **MQLs Attributed:** 284 (from registrations + post-event follow-up)
- **Pipeline Generated (30 days post):** $612,000
- **Top Session:** "AI and the Future of Enterprise Knowledge" — 847 live attendees
- **Sponsors:** Salesforce, ServiceNow (Tier 2 sponsors — $40K sponsor revenue, offsetting costs)
- **Net Cost:** $147,000 after sponsor revenue
- **Notes:** CSAT score: 4.6/5.0. 68% of attendees requested a product demo follow-up.
