## Executive Summary

Q1 2026 was a strong quarter for NovaTech Solutions' sales organization. We closed **$4.2M in total revenue** against a target of **$3.8M**, achieving **110% of plan**. New ARR of $1.1M and expansion ARR of $890K drove the overperformance, while churn remained well-controlled at $120K. Net Revenue Retention (NRR) came in at **118%**, our highest quarterly NRR since Q3 2024.

Key highlights:
- Largest single deal in company history: Meridian Enterprises at $387K ARR
- Pipeline entering Q2 is 3.2x quota, the strongest coverage we've had in five quarters
- Win rate improved from 21% in Q4 2025 to 24% in Q1 2026, driven by better discovery discipline
- Average deal size grew to $87K (up from $74K in Q4 2025)

Areas of concern:
- EMEA pipeline continues to lag; only 1 deal closed in EMEA vs. 3 targeted
- Two key reps (Ben Lawson, Chloé Dupont) are below 50% of quota; intervention plans in place
- Q2 large deal forecast is concentrated in two $400K+ opportunities — risk if either slips
