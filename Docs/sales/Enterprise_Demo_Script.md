## Section 1: Opening & Introductions (5 Minutes)

**Goal:** Establish rapport, confirm who is in the room, and align on the agenda.

**Script:**

"[First name], great to finally meet you — and thank you to everyone for joining today. [Brief personal note if applicable: 'I saw your team just launched a new product line — exciting stuff!']

Before I launch into the demo, I'd love to do a quick round of introductions. Could everyone share your name, your role, and — most importantly — what you're hoping to get out of our time together today? That'll help me make sure we're spending the 60 minutes in a way that's actually valuable to you.

[Facilitate introductions, write down names and roles, note any surprise attendees.]

Great. Here's what I'm thinking for our time: I want to start with 10 minutes of conversation about where you are today and what success looks like for you — we've had some great pre-conversations, but there's always more to learn. Then I'll walk through the platform with your specific situation in mind. We'll leave the last 15 minutes for your questions and to talk about what a potential next step might look like.

Sound good? Is there anything you'd like to make sure we cover that we haven't discussed yet?"

## Section 2: Discovery Questions (10 Minutes)

**Goal:** Validate and deepen your understanding of their pain, timeline, and decision process.

**Note to AEs:** This is NOT a repeat of your first discovery call. Use this session to validate assumptions, get to specifics, and surface any new stakeholders or concerns. Tailor questions based on what you know from prior conversations.

### Discovery Question Bank (pick 4–6 based on what you already know):

**On Current State & Pain:**
- "You mentioned on our last call that your team is spending significant time answering repetitive questions. In a given week, how many hours do you think that represents across your support team?"
- "When a new employee joins today, walk me through what their first two weeks look like in terms of finding information and getting up to speed."
- "If I asked your top support agent where they go when they can't find an answer to a customer question, what would they say?"
- "You have Confluence today — what's working about it, and where is it breaking down?"

**On Impact & Urgency:**
- "What's driving the timeline for this evaluation? Is there a specific project, audit, or organizational change that makes this more pressing?"
- "If you could solve this problem tomorrow, what would be different about next quarter?"
- "What's the cost of not solving this in 2026 — either in productivity terms, customer satisfaction, or compliance risk?"

**On Decision Process:**
- "Since we last spoke, have there been any changes to who else will be involved in evaluating this?"
- "Is there a formal scoring process or RFP, or is this more of a qualitative evaluation?"
- "What does success look like for you in the first 90 days of a rollout?"

**Take notes in Salesforce immediately after the call.** All discovery notes must be added to the Salesforce opportunity within 2 hours.

## Section 3: Platform Demo (30 Minutes)

**General Principles:**
- Always show the platform in the context of a scenario relevant to the prospect's industry and use case
- Never do a feature walkthrough. Always do a story-based demo: "Let me show you what happens when [problem the prospect described] happens in NovaTech."
- Pause frequently to ask: "Does this match what you described?" and "Is this the kind of thing your team would find valuable?"

### Demo Module 1: Knowledge Base Creation & Management (8 minutes)

**Narration:**
"Let me set the scene. You've just hired 50 new support agents — congratulations on the growth! It's their first week. When a customer calls with a complex question about your enterprise product, where does that new agent go? Today you said they're searching Confluence and Slack. Let me show you what that looks like in NovaTech.

[Navigate to NovaTech Demo Environment — pre-loaded with [Company Name] demo instance if possible]

Here's the NovaTech homepage for your team. Notice right away: it's not a folder structure. It's a smart search bar — like Google, but for everything your team has created.

[Type a search query relevant to their business: 'returns policy for enterprise customers']

In under 0.5 seconds, NovaTech surfaces the most relevant article, highlights the specific passage that answers the question, and shows me how recently it was updated — in this case, 3 days ago. If the article is older than 90 days, NovaTech automatically flags it for review by the content owner.

[Click through to article]

The article itself supports rich formatting — video embeds, decision trees, step-by-step guides. You can see here we've embedded a 2-minute video walkthrough that any support agent can play directly in the page. No tab-switching to find the video; it's all in one place.

[Demonstrate inline commenting and suggestion workflow]

Now, your content contributors — your senior agents, your trainers — can update this article in real time. See this suggestion icon? If a user notices the article is out of date, they can flag it with a note. The content owner gets an email, approves the fix, and it's live in 60 seconds.

Pause: Does this answer workflow match what you need, or do you have a formal review process you'd want to see?

[Listen and adjust demo accordingly]"

## Section 4: ROI Discussion (10 Minutes)

**Goal:** Connect the demo to quantifiable business value. Move from 'this is cool' to 'this pays for itself.'

**Framing Statement:**
"Before we wrap up, I want to spend a few minutes on the business case — because I find it's much easier to get internal buy-in when you can tie this to hard numbers. Let me share how similar companies think about the ROI.

Let me use your numbers where I can. You mentioned you have about 120 support agents. And you said the average agent spends roughly 2–3 hours per day searching for information. Let's be conservative and say 1.5 hours.

[Write or screen-share a simple calculation:]

120 agents × 1.5 hours/day × $35/hour fully loaded cost = **$6,300/day in search time**
$6,300 × 250 working days = **$1,575,000/year in lost productivity from search alone**

NovaTech customers typically cut search time by 40–60%. At a conservative 40%:
$1,575,000 × 40% = **$630,000/year in productivity recovered**

Your investment in NovaTech at your scale is in the range of $150,000–$200,000/year. That's a 3–4x ROI in year one — payback in under 4 months.

Does this math feel right to you, or should we adjust any of the assumptions?"

**Additional ROI Angles (use if relevant):**
- Ticket deflection value: "If your average ticket costs $15 to handle and you deflect 20% through self-service, for 50,000 monthly tickets, that's $150,000/month in ticket deflection value."
- Onboarding acceleration: "You mentioned new hires take 6 weeks to reach productivity. Our average customer gets that down to 4 weeks — at $50K/hire in fully-loaded cost, that's $25K per hire in productivity gains."
- Compliance risk reduction: "A single failed audit in your industry can cost $500K+ in remediation and fines. Audit-ready documentation eliminates that risk category."

## Section 5: Next Steps (5 Minutes)

**Goal:** End every demo with a clear, mutually agreed-upon next step. Never leave a demo without a next meeting on the calendar.

**Script:**
"[First name], I appreciate you spending an hour with us today. Before I let you all go — quick check-in: what resonated most with what you saw, and was there anything that gave you pause or that you'd want to explore further?

[Listen. Note specific features or concerns mentioned.]

Here's what I'd suggest as a next step: given what you've shared, I think the most valuable thing we could do is a focused technical review with [Technical Evaluator's name] and our Solutions Architect — specifically around the SSO integration and your compliance requirements. Would a 45-minute technical session work for [timeframe]?

[Alternatively, if deal is further along:]
'It sounds like you have enough context to go back and share this with [Economic Buyer name]. Would it help if we put together a one-page business case summary that you could present internally? I can have that to you by [specific date].'

[Or if EB is in the room:]
'[EB name], from what you've heard today — where does NovaTech land for you in terms of fit? What would you need to see to feel confident moving forward?'

Our goal is to get you the information you need to make the right decision, quickly. I don't want to waste your time with an extended sales process if there's something that would disqualify us — so please be direct with us about any concerns."

**Concrete Next Step Options:**
- Technical Review (SE-led, 45 min)
- Security Questionnaire & Architecture Review
- Reference customer call (same industry, similar company size)
- Pilot / POC (30 days, limited users)
- Proposal / Pricing Discussion
- Executive sponsor meeting (your VP/CEO + their EB)
- Contract review kickoff

**Non-optional:** Send a same-day follow-up email (within 2 hours) recapping: what was discussed, the prospect's stated pain and priorities, the next meeting confirmed with time/date, and any action items for both sides.

## Common Questions and Answers for AEs

**Q: "Can you integrate with [obscure internal system]?"**
A: "We have a robust REST API and webhook infrastructure. If there's a specific system you're asking about, I'd love to have our Solutions Engineer connect with your technical team to discuss the integration architecture. What are the top 2–3 systems you'd need us to connect with?"

**Q: "What happens to our data if we cancel?"**
A: "Great question. Upon contract termination, we provide you with a full data export in JSON and CSV format within 14 days. All your content is yours. We purge our systems within 30 days of confirmed export receipt. This is documented in our standard MSA."

**Q: "How long does implementation take?"**
A: "For a company your size, our standard implementation is 6–8 weeks for the core platform, with full adoption and migration complete in 12–16 weeks. We've done it faster for urgent timelines — but I want to set a realistic expectation."

**Q: "We're already evaluating [Competitor]. Why should we choose you?"**
A: "Healthy that you're evaluating options. I'd rather not just say 'we're better' without understanding what they showed you. What stood out to you in [competitor's] demo? That'll help me show you specifically where we differ."
