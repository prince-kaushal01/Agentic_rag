## 1. Pricing Philosophy

NovaTech Solutions prices its platform on a **per-seat, annual subscription** model with tiered packaging. Our pricing is designed to:
- Be immediately accessible to mid-market buyers at the Starter and Professional tiers
- Offer unlimited scalability and customization for Enterprise customers
- Reward long-term commitment with meaningful multi-year discounts
- Enable upsell and expansion within existing accounts through modular add-ons

We do NOT compete on price alone. Our value proposition is ROI-based. AEs should never lead with discounting; always establish the value and ROI before discussing price concessions.

List pricing is the starting point for negotiation on Enterprise deals. Deals over $100K ARR almost always include some degree of negotiation. Discounts must follow the approval matrix in §11.

## 3. Starter Plan — $2,000/month ($24,000/year)

### Who It's For
Small teams and early-stage companies building their first knowledge management system. Typical buyers: SMB IT teams, startups scaling from 50–150 employees.

### What's Included
- **Users:** Up to 50 named users
- **Storage:** 50 GB document storage
- **API Calls:** 10,000 API calls/month
- **AI Search & Answer Suggestions:** Standard AI search (GPT-4o powered)
- **Integrations:** Slack, Google Drive, Dropbox, Notion (read-only import)
- **Authentication:** Email/password + Google SSO
- **Support:** Email support with 48-hour response SLA
- **Uptime SLA:** 99.5% monthly uptime
- **Analytics:** Basic usage reporting (page views, search queries)
- **Content Types:** Articles, FAQs, How-To guides
- **Versioning:** 30-day version history
- **Custom Branding:** Not included (NovaTech branding on portal)

### Starter Limitations
- No custom SSO/SAML
- No advanced permissions or role-based access control
- No dedicated Customer Success Manager
- No custom domain
- No API access beyond basic read endpoints

### Typical Starter Customer Profile
- 50–200 employees
- Single use case (internal IT wiki, HR policy hub)
- Budget owner: IT Manager or Operations Lead
- ARR: $24,000–$36,000 (including basic add-ons)

## 5. Enterprise Plan — Custom Pricing (Starting ~$10,000/month)

### Who It's For
Large enterprises with 250+ users, complex security requirements, multi-department rollouts, and custom integration needs. Typical buyers: Fortune 1000 companies, global enterprises, heavily regulated industries.

### What's Included (Everything in Professional, Plus:)
- **Users:** Unlimited named users
- **Storage:** Unlimited (with fair use policy; burst storage on-demand)
- **API Calls:** Unlimited (rate limits per SLA negotiation)
- **AI Capabilities:** Enterprise AI with custom LLM fine-tuning options, private AI deployment, AI guardrails
- **Integrations:** Custom API integrations; dedicated integration engineer for first 90 days
- **Authentication:** Multi-IdP SSO, SCIM provisioning, MFA enforcement
- **Support:** Dedicated Customer Success Manager + Technical Account Manager; 4-hour P1 SLA; 24/7 emergency support
- **Uptime SLA:** 99.9% monthly uptime (with financial penalties for breach)
- **Analytics:** Enterprise analytics with BI connector (Tableau, Looker, PowerBI)
- **Security:** SOC 2 Type II certified; HIPAA-ready; GDPR Article 28 DPA; ISO 27001 mapped controls
- **Data Residency:** US, EU, or Australia data region selection
- **Audit Log:** Unlimited audit trail with SIEM integration
- **Custom Permissions:** Unlimited custom roles, hierarchical permissions, content segmentation
- **Versioning:** Unlimited version history
- **SLA Guarantees:** Custom SLA with credit-back provisions
- **Professional Services:** 40 hours of implementation support included; additional at $250/hour
- **Training:** Live admin and end-user training (up to 10 sessions included)
- **Executive Sponsorship:** Quarterly business reviews with NovaTech CS leadership

### Enterprise Pricing Drivers
Enterprise contract value is determined by:
1. **Number of users** (primary driver): Baseline starts at $10/user/month for 250–500 users; drops to $7/user/month at 5,000+
2. **Modules included** (Compliance, Multilingual, Advanced Analytics)
3. **Contract term** (1, 2, or 3 years)
4. **Data residency region** (no premium for US; +8% for EU data residency; +12% for Australia)
5. **Professional services scope**

**Typical Enterprise Deal Range:**
- 500 users, 1-year, US data, base platform: $120,000 ARR
- 2,000 users, 2-year, US data, Compliance Module: $280,000 ARR
- 5,000 users, 3-year, EU data, all modules: $580,000+ ARR

## 8. Volume Discounts

Volume discounts are applied to the base platform price for Enterprise deals based on user count. Discounts are applied to the per-user rate only; add-on module prices are not discounted via this table (negotiate separately).

| User Count | Volume Discount |
|-----------|----------------|
| 250–499 | 0% (list price) |
| 500–999 | 8% off list |
| 1,000–1,999 | 15% off list |
| 2,000–4,999 | 22% off list |
| 5,000–9,999 | 28% off list |
| 10,000+ | 35% off list (requires VP approval) |

## 9. Multi-Year Contract Discounts

Multi-year discounts reward long-term commitment and improve NovaTech's revenue predictability.

| Contract Term | Discount | Notes |
|--------------|----------|-------|
| 1 year | 0% | Standard term |
| 2 years | 10% off annual price | Payment: annual or upfront |
| 3 years | 20% off annual price | Payment: annual or upfront; 3yr upfront adds additional 5% |

Multi-year discounts stack on top of volume discounts.

**Example:** 2,000 users, 3-year contract:
- List price: $14,000/month × 12 = $168,000/year
- Volume discount (2,000 users, 22%): $130,440/year
- Multi-year discount (3-year, 20%): $104,352/year
- **Final ARR: $104,352** (vs. $168,000 list — 38% total discount)

## 12. Pricing for International Markets

NovaTech invoices in USD globally. Local currency options (GBP, EUR) are available for Enterprise customers in the UK and EU; billing is at the contracted USD rate converted at the monthly average exchange rate.

**EMEA Pricing Notes:**
- EU data residency adds 8% to the base contract value
- UK VAT (20%) is added to all UK invoices; EU customers provide VAT registration number for reverse-charge treatment
- EMEA deals may include NovaTech Solutions Ltd. (UK) as the contracting entity

**Canada / APAC:**
- Billed in USD; local currency quotations for informational purposes only
- GST/HST added to Canadian invoices
- Australian data residency adds 12% premium

## 13. Common Pricing Scenarios & FAQs

**Q: A customer has 400 users today but is planning to grow to 800 in 12 months. Which tier?**
A: Sell them Enterprise. Starter and Professional have hard user caps. Structure the contract as a 2-year Enterprise deal with a Year 2 expansion provision at the 800-user volume price.

**Q: The customer wants to pay monthly instead of annually. Is that possible?**
A: For Starter only. Monthly billing carries a 20% premium. For Professional and Enterprise, annual invoicing is required. Multi-year contracts may pay annually.

**Q: A customer is asking for a free trial or POC. What are our terms?**
A: Approved POCs are limited to 30 days, up to 25 users, read-only production data. POC approval must come from the Sales Manager. No POC should be granted before Discovery is complete and MEDDIC fields are 70%+ complete.

**Q: Can we offer non-standard SLA guarantees?**
A: Yes, but only through the Enterprise tier and must be approved by both the VP of Sales and our VP of Engineering (for operational feasibility). SLA penalty provisions (credit-back for downtime) are standard in Enterprise agreements.

**Q: A startup is asking for startup pricing. Do we have a program?**
A: Yes. For companies < 3 years old with < $10M ARR and < 100 employees, contact RevOps for Startup Program pricing. Typical: 50% off Professional for Year 1, 25% off for Year 2. Maximum 2-year commitment.
