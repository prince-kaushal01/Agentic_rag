## ENTERPRISE SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Enterprise Software Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between NovaTech Solutions, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("NovaTech"), and ACME Corporation, a Delaware corporation ("Customer"). NovaTech and Customer are each referred to herein individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

---

## Section 1: Definitions

**1.1 "Authorized Users"** means Customer's employees, contractors, and agents who are authorized by Customer to access and use the Platform pursuant to this Agreement, up to the maximum number of Authorized Users specified in the applicable Order Form.

**1.2 "Confidential Information"** means any information disclosed by one Party to the other Party in connection with this Agreement that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

**1.3 "Customer Data"** means all data, information, and content that Customer or its Authorized Users submit, upload, transmit, or otherwise make available through the Platform.

**1.4 "Documentation"** means NovaTech's technical documentation, user manuals, help center articles, and other written materials made available to Customer at novatech.io/docs or through the Platform.

**1.5 "Effective Date"** means January 15, 2025 as specified above.

**1.6 "Order Form"** means the written or electronic order form(s) executed by the Parties that sets forth the specific subscription details, including the subscription tier, number of Authorized Users, fees, and term.

**1.7 "Platform"** means NovaTech's enterprise software platform, including all features, functionality, updates, and enhancements made available to Customer during the Subscription Term, as described in Exhibit A (Platform Description).

**1.8 "SLA"** means the Service Level Agreement set forth in Exhibit B, which describes NovaTech's uptime commitments and remedies for failure to meet such commitments.

**1.9 "Subscription Fees"** means the fees payable by Customer for access to and use of the Platform as set forth in Section 5 of this Agreement.

**1.10 "Subscription Term"** means the period during which Customer is authorized to access and use the Platform, commencing on the Effective Date and ending on January 14, 2027, unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Agreement.

## Section 2: License Grant and Access

**2.1 Grant of License.** Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, and conditioned on Customer's timely payment of all applicable Subscription Fees, NovaTech hereby grants to Customer a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right and license during the Subscription Term to: (a) access and use the Platform solely for Customer's internal business purposes; and (b) permit Authorized Users to access and use the Platform in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.2 Subscription Tier and User Count.** Customer's subscription is for the **Enterprise Tier** of the NovaTech Platform. Customer is authorized to provide access to up to:
- **500 (five hundred) Authorized Users** (standard user seats)
- **5 (five) Admin User seats** (with elevated permissions for configuration and user management)

**2.3 Additional Users.** If Customer desires to add Authorized Users beyond the licensed quantities, Customer shall notify NovaTech, and the Parties shall execute an Order Form amendment. Additional user pricing shall be at the then-current enterprise list rate, subject to any volume discount applicable at the time of expansion.

**2.4 Access Credentials.** NovaTech will provision Customer's account and provide administrator credentials within 5 business days of the Effective Date. Customer is responsible for maintaining the security of all access credentials and for all activities that occur under Customer's account.

**2.5 SAML/SSO Integration.** As an Enterprise tier customer, ACME Corp is entitled to integrate NovaTech's Platform with ACME Corp's SAML 2.0 identity provider (currently Okta). NovaTech's Technical Account Manager will provide integration support within the first 30 days of the Subscription Term.

## Section 4: NovaTech Obligations

**4.1 Platform Availability.** NovaTech will make the Platform available to Customer and its Authorized Users in accordance with the Service Level Agreement set forth in Exhibit B. NovaTech commits to a **monthly uptime of 99.9%** for the production Platform environment, measured on a calendar month basis.

**4.2 Security.** NovaTech will maintain commercially reasonable administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect the security, confidentiality, and integrity of Customer Data. NovaTech maintains SOC 2 Type II certification, which it will make available to Customer upon written request under NDA.

**4.3 Support.** Customer shall receive **Priority Support** as specified in the Support Addendum (Exhibit C), including:
- Priority 1 (P1 — System Down): 4-hour response time, 24 hours/day, 7 days/week, 365 days/year
- Priority 2 (P2 — Critical Feature Impaired): 8-hour response time, business hours
- Priority 3 (P3 — General Issues): 24-hour response time, business hours
- Priority 4 (P4 — Minor Issues/Questions): 48-hour response time, business hours
- Dedicated Support Queue: ACME Corp tickets are flagged for priority handling
- Quarterly Business Reviews: NovaTech will conduct quarterly business reviews (QBRs) with ACME Corp

**4.4 Data Privacy.** NovaTech will process Customer Data in accordance with the Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") attached hereto as Exhibit D. NovaTech is a data processor with respect to Customer Data, and Customer is the data controller.

**4.5 Subcontractors.** NovaTech may use subcontractors and subprocessors in the delivery of the Platform and related services, provided that NovaTech remains responsible for such subcontractors' compliance with this Agreement. A current list of approved subprocessors is maintained at novatech.io/legal/subprocessors.

## Section 8: Refunds and Termination for Cause

**8.1 Refund Eligibility.** Customer may request a refund within **thirty (30) days of the applicable invoice date** if NovaTech fails to meet contracted SLA commitments as set forth in Exhibit B, or if the product materially fails to perform as specified in Exhibit A (Platform Specifications). Enterprise refunds under this Agreement are additionally subject to **Section 7 of NovaTech's Refund and Cancellation Policy** (currently available at novatech.io/legal/refund-policy), which is incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

**8.2 Prorated Refunds.** In the event of a qualifying refund under this Section 8 or under Section 9 (Termination), partial year refunds shall be prorated on a monthly basis, calculated as the number of unused complete months remaining in the applicable annual subscription period multiplied by the monthly subscription fee ($20,000.00/month). For the avoidance of doubt, any implementation fees, custom development fees, or training fees paid by Customer are non-refundable.

**8.3 SLA Credits.** For SLA breaches that do not rise to the level of triggering a refund under Section 8.1, NovaTech shall issue service credits in accordance with the credit schedule set forth in Exhibit B. Service credits are issued as account credits applied against future invoices. Credits are the Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for SLA breaches that do not constitute grounds for a refund under this Section 8.

**8.4 Refund Request Process.** To request a refund under this Agreement, Customer must submit a written refund request to billing@novatech.io and to Customer's assigned Customer Success Manager, including the invoice number, invoice date, amount requested, and a description of the basis for the refund (with supporting documentation). NovaTech will review and respond to refund requests within 5 business days. Refunds approved by NovaTech's VP Finance for amounts exceeding $10,000 will be processed within 10 business days of approval.

**8.5 Payment Current Requirement.** Customer must be current on all payment obligations under this Agreement (no amounts past due beyond Net-60) to be eligible for a refund. Any outstanding past-due amounts may be offset against approved refund amounts at NovaTech's election.

## Section 9: Term and Termination

**9.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for the Subscription Term (January 15, 2025 – January 14, 2027) unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Section 9.

**9.2 Renewal.** This Agreement will not automatically renew without Customer's written confirmation of intent to renew. **Customer must provide written notice of its intent to renew no later than ninety (90) calendar days prior to January 14, 2027.** NovaTech will send a renewal proposal to Customer no later than 120 days prior to the contract expiration date.

**9.3 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement for cause upon 30 days' written notice to the other Party if the other Party: (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within the 30-day notice period; (b) becomes insolvent or makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors; or (c) becomes subject to any proceeding under bankruptcy or insolvency law that is not dismissed within 60 days.

**9.4 Termination for Convenience.** Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon **60 days' written notice** to NovaTech, provided that no refund will be issued for the remaining unused Subscription Term, and any outstanding invoices become immediately due and payable.

**9.5 Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement: (a) all licenses granted to Customer under this Agreement shall immediately terminate; (b) Customer shall cease all use of the Platform; (c) each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information; and (d) NovaTech will make Customer Data available for download for 60 days following the termination date, after which Customer Data will be permanently deleted.

**9.6 Survival.** The following sections shall survive termination or expiration of this Agreement: Section 1 (Definitions), Section 6 (Intellectual Property), Section 7 (Confidentiality), Section 10 (Limitation of Liability), Section 11 (Indemnification), Section 12 (Governing Law and Dispute Resolution), and any provisions that by their nature should survive.

## Exhibit B: Service Level Agreement (SLA)

**Uptime Commitment:** 99.9% monthly uptime for the production Platform environment.

**Uptime Calculation:** Uptime % = ((Total Minutes in Month − Downtime Minutes) / Total Minutes in Month) × 100. Scheduled maintenance windows (published at status.novatech.io 48 hours in advance) are excluded from downtime calculation.

**SLA Credit Schedule:**

| Monthly Uptime | Credit (% of Monthly Fee) | Credit Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 99.0% – 99.8% | 10% | $2,000 |
| 95.0% – 98.9% | 25% | $5,000 |
| 90.0% – 94.9% | 50% | $10,000 |
| Below 90.0% | 100% | $20,000 |

Credits are issued as account credits against the next monthly invoice. Credits requested as cash refunds must comply with Section 8 of this Agreement and Section 4.1(b) of NovaTech's Refund Policy.
