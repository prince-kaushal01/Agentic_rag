## Introduction

NovaTech Solutions, Inc. ("NovaTech," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who use our enterprise software platform and services. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal data in connection with our Platform (available at app.novatech.io), our marketing website (novatech.io), our support portal (support.novatech.io), and any other services we offer (collectively, the "Services").

This Policy applies to:
- **Customers:** Companies and organizations that subscribe to the NovaTech Platform
- **Authorized Users:** Employees and contractors of customers who are given access to the Platform
- **Website Visitors:** Anyone who visits our marketing website or landing pages
- **Prospects:** Individuals who submit contact information for sales or marketing purposes

If you are an employee or contractor of a NovaTech customer ("Authorized User"), your company controls how your data is processed within the Platform. Please refer to your employer's privacy policy for information about how they handle your data in NovaTech.

## Section 1: Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account and Registration Data

When you or your organization creates a NovaTech account, we collect:
- **Account holder information:** Full name, email address, job title, company name, phone number
- **Organization details:** Company name, size, industry, billing address, Federal Tax ID (for invoicing)
- **Authentication credentials:** Username and password (encrypted); SSO identity provider tokens
- **Billing information:** Payment method details (processed by Stripe; NovaTech does not store full credit card numbers), billing address, VAT/tax ID
- **Contract information:** Order form details, subscription tier, number of licensed users

### 1.2 Platform Usage Data

As you use the NovaTech Platform, we automatically collect:
- **Feature usage data:** Which features are used, how frequently, by whom (Authorized User ID level)
- **User activity logs:** Login/logout events, configuration changes, workflow triggers, data exports
- **Performance data:** Page load times, API response times, error rates (per user session)
- **Integration events:** Data exchanged through NovaTech's integrations with third-party tools (Salesforce, Slack, Jira, etc.)

### 1.3 Log Data and Technical Information

When you access the Platform or website, our servers automatically record:
- IP address, browser type and version, operating system
- Pages visited, time spent on pages, referral URLs, exit pages
- Date and time of access, session duration
- Device identifiers (mobile devices), screen resolution
- Crash reports and error logs

### 1.4 Communications Data

We collect communications you send us, including:
- Support tickets submitted via the support portal
- Emails and chat conversations with our support or sales teams
- Survey responses and product feedback
- Comments in community forums or help center

### 1.5 Marketing and Prospect Data

For individuals who engage with NovaTech's marketing:
- Contact information provided through website forms (name, email, company, phone, job role)
- Event registration data (webinars, conferences)
- Content downloaded (whitepapers, reports)
- Website behavior data (pages viewed, CTA clicks, session replays — via Hotjar, with consent)

## Section 6: Your Privacy Rights

### 6.1 Rights for EEA/UK Individuals (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA or UK, you have the following rights under GDPR:

- **Right of Access (Art. 15):** Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Right to Rectification (Art. 16):** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
- **Right to Erasure (Art. 17):** Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to limitations
- **Right to Restriction (Art. 18):** Request that we restrict processing of your personal data
- **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20):** Receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to Object (Art. 21):** Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including for direct marketing
- **Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3)):** Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact our DPO at privacy@novatech.io. We will respond within **30 days** (or 60 days for complex requests). We do not charge a fee for exercising your rights.

### 6.2 California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA):

- **Right to Know:** Request information about what personal data we collect, use, disclose, and sell
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of personal data, subject to certain exceptions
- **Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing:** NovaTech does not sell personal data. You may opt out of sharing for targeted advertising purposes
- **Right to Correct:** Request correction of inaccurate personal data
- **Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information:** Limit use of sensitive personal information to only what is necessary
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** NovaTech will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights

California residents may submit requests at privacy@novatech.io or by calling (415) 555-0100. We will respond within 45 days.

**Categories of personal data collected in the past 12 months:**
- Identifiers (name, email, IP address)
- Commercial information (subscription records)
- Internet or other electronic network activity information (usage data, log data)
- Geolocation data (derived from IP address — city/country level)
- Professional or employment-related information (job title, employer)

**NovaTech does not sell personal data and has not sold personal data in the past 12 months.**

Customer Data is stored primarily in AWS us-east-1 (Northern Virginia). Enterprise customers who require EU data residency may request storage in AWS eu-west-1 (Ireland) — contact your Account Executive or success@novatech.io.

A copy of NovaTech's SCCs is available upon request from privacy@novatech.io.

## Section 9: Data Security

NovaTech implements comprehensive technical and organizational security measures:

- **Encryption:** All data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access control; principle of least privilege; MFA required for all internal access
- **SOC 2 Type II:** Annual audit by Ernst & Young; most recent report available under NDA upon customer request
- **Penetration Testing:** Annual third-party penetration test; most recent conducted October 2025
- **Incident Response:** 72-hour breach notification to affected parties and relevant regulatory authorities
- **Vulnerability Management:** Monthly automated scans; critical patches deployed within 24 hours
- **Employee Training:** All employees complete annual security awareness training
