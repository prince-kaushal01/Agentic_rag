## Template Usage Guidelines

> **When to Use the Mutual NDA (Both Parties Sharing Confidential Information):**
> - Sales prospects and pre-contract conversations where you will share NovaTech product roadmaps, pricing strategy, or architecture details with a prospect who will also share their internal data or technical requirements
> - Partnership discussions (technology partners, resellers, integration partners)
> - Vendor evaluations where both parties will share technical and business information
> - M&A discussions, due diligence processes, or strategic partnership discussions
> - Any situation where the flow of confidential information is genuinely bilateral

> **When to Use the One-Way NDA (Only NovaTech Shares Confidential Information):**
> - Demos or presentations where NovaTech shares product roadmaps, future features, or pricing but the prospect/partner is not sharing confidential information
> - Analyst briefings
> - Conference presentations under NDA

> **Signature Authority:** NDAs may be signed by any NovaTech employee at Director level or above. All executed NDAs must be filed with the Legal team (legal@novatech.io) within 2 business days of execution.

## Section 1: Definition of Confidential Information

**1.1 Confidential Information Defined.** As used in this Agreement, "Confidential Information" means any and all information or data that has or could have commercial value or other utility in the business in which either Party is engaged, that is disclosed by one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party") in connection with the Business Purpose, and that: (a) if disclosed in tangible form, is marked "Confidential," "Proprietary," or with a similar designation; (b) if disclosed orally or visually, is identified as confidential at the time of disclosure and confirmed in writing within fifteen (15) days thereafter; or (c) given the nature of the information or the context of disclosure, a reasonable person would understand it to be confidential.

**1.2 Examples of Confidential Information.** Confidential Information includes, without limitation:
- Business plans, financial information, revenue figures, pricing strategies, and forecasts
- Product roadmaps, technical specifications, source code, algorithms, and architecture details
- Customer lists, customer data, and customer contact information
- Vendor lists and vendor contract terms
- Personnel information, compensation data, and organizational structure
- Trade secrets and proprietary methodologies
- Any information clearly labeled or designated as confidential

**1.3 Confidential Information of Affiliates.** Confidential Information includes confidential information of a Party's affiliates, subsidiaries, and related entities disclosed in connection with the Business Purpose.

---

## Section 2: Exclusions from Confidential Information

The obligations of confidentiality in this Agreement shall not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by competent written evidence:

(a) **Public Domain:** Is or becomes generally available to the public through no fault of the Receiving Party;

(b) **Prior Knowledge:** Was rightfully known to the Receiving Party without restriction prior to the Effective Date of this Agreement;

(c) **Third-Party Disclosure:** Is rightfully obtained by the Receiving Party from a third party without restriction on use or disclosure;

(d) **Independent Development:** Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, as evidenced by contemporaneous written records;

(e) **Written Release:** Is disclosed with the prior written approval of the Disclosing Party.

---

## Section 3: Obligations of the Receiving Party

**3.1 Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party agrees to: (a) keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information strictly confidential; (b) not disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information to any third party without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent; and (c) use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely for the Business Purpose and for no other purpose.

**3.2 Degree of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**3.3 Authorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party may disclose the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information only to its officers, directors, employees, attorneys, accountants, and advisors (collectively, "Representatives") who: (a) have a specific need to know such information in connection with the Business Purpose; and (b) are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than those contained in this Agreement. The Receiving Party shall be liable for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

**3.4 No License.** Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any rights in or to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, other than the limited right to use it for the Business Purpose as set forth herein. No intellectual property license is granted or implied.

**3.5 Security Measures.** The Receiving Party shall implement reasonable physical and electronic security measures to protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or use.

---

## Section 4: Permitted Disclosures (Legal Requirement)

**4.1 Compelled Disclosure.** If the Receiving Party is required by applicable law, regulation, judicial order, or government authority to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall:

(a) Promptly notify the Disclosing Party in writing of such requirement (to the extent permitted by law and practicable given the timeline) before making any disclosure;

(b) Cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party (at the Disclosing Party's expense) in seeking a protective order, confidential treatment request, or other appropriate remedy to limit or delay disclosure;

(c) If disclosure is ultimately required, disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that is legally required, and exercise reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment for the disclosed information.

**4.2 No Liability.** The Receiving Party shall not be in breach of this Agreement for disclosures made in compliance with Section 4.1.

---

## Section 5: Term and Duration

**5.1 Agreement Term.** This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in effect for a period of **three (3) years**, unless earlier terminated by either Party upon 30 days' written notice to the other Party.

**5.2 Survival of Obligations.** The confidentiality obligations set forth in Section 3 shall survive expiration or termination of this Agreement for an additional **two (2) years** following the end of the Agreement Term, for a total confidentiality period of five (5) years from the Effective Date.

**5.3 Trade Secrets.** Notwithstanding Section 5.2, obligations with respect to information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law shall survive indefinitely for so long as such information remains a trade secret.

---

## Section 6: Return and Destruction of Confidential Information

**6.1 Return or Destroy.** Upon: (a) expiration or termination of this Agreement; (b) completion of the Business Purpose; or (c) written request by the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall promptly (and in no event later than ten (10) business days):

- Return to the Disclosing Party all tangible materials containing Confidential Information; OR
- Permanently destroy or delete all copies of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in its possession or control (including electronic copies, backups, and cloud storage), and certify in writing to the Disclosing Party that such destruction has been completed.

**6.2 Retained Copies.** Notwithstanding Section 6.1, the Receiving Party may retain archival copies of Confidential Information to the extent required by applicable law, regulation, or bona fide document retention policies, provided that such retained copies remain subject to the confidentiality obligations of this Agreement.

---

## Section 7: Injunctive Relief

The Receiving Party acknowledges that any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement may cause the Disclosing Party irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy, and that the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction without posting a bond or other security. This right to injunctive relief is in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other remedies available at law or in equity.

---

## Section 8: No Obligation to Proceed

**8.1 No Commitment.** Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to: (a) disclose any particular information; (b) enter into any business transaction or relationship; or (c) proceed with any proposed transaction related to the Business Purpose.

**8.2 No Representations.** Each Party acknowledges that the Disclosing Party makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of its Confidential Information.

---

## Section 9: General Provisions

**9.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of California**, without regard to conflict of law principles. Any action to enforce this Agreement shall be brought in state or federal courts located in **San Francisco County, California**, and the Parties consent to personal jurisdiction therein.

**9.2 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, and understandings regarding confidentiality between the Parties.

**9.3 Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

**9.4 No Waiver.** Failure by either Party to enforce any provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of that Party's right to enforce such provision in the future.

**9.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is found invalid or unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, that provision shall be limited to the minimum extent necessary, and the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**9.6 Counterparts and Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original, and electronic signatures (DocuSign or equivalent) are accepted and shall be deemed original signatures.

**9.7 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign this Agreement in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets.

**9.8 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and sent by email (with confirmation of receipt), certified mail, or overnight courier to the addresses set forth in the signature block below.

# ONE-WAY (UNILATERAL) NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

*Use this variant when only NovaTech is sharing confidential information.*

> **To convert this Agreement to a one-way NDA:**
> 1. Change the title to "Unilateral Non-Disclosure Agreement" or "Confidentiality Agreement"
> 2. Delete the mutual provisions and replace "Disclosing Party" and "Receiving Party" with "NovaTech" and "[COUNTERPARTY]" respectively throughout
> 3. The Business Purpose description should clarify that NovaTech is disclosing information for the Counterparty's evaluation
> 4. The obligations in Section 3 apply only to the Counterparty as the Receiving Party
> 5. Section 6 (Return of Confidential Information) applies only to materials provided by NovaTech
