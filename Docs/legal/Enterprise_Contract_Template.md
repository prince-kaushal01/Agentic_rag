## Section 3: Restrictions

**3.1 Customer shall not:**

(b) Reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Platform;

(d) Access the Platform using automated means without NovaTech's prior written consent;

(e) Remove any proprietary notices or labels from or within the Platform;

(f) Use the Platform in violation of applicable law or NovaTech's Acceptable Use Policy.

**3.2 Acceptable Use.** Customer's use of the Platform is subject to NovaTech's Acceptable Use Policy located at novatech.io/legal/aup.

## Section 6: Intellectual Property

**6.1 NovaTech Ownership.** NovaTech retains all right, title, and interest in the Platform, Documentation, and all associated intellectual property rights.

**6.2 Customer Data Ownership.** Customer retains all right, title, and interest in Customer Data. Customer grants NovaTech a limited license to use Customer Data solely to provide the Platform and related services.

**6.3 Feedback.** Customer grants NovaTech a royalty-free, perpetual license to incorporate any Feedback provided by Customer into the Platform.

**6.4 Aggregated Data.** NovaTech may use anonymized, aggregated usage data for product improvement and benchmarking, provided such data does not identify Customer.

## Section 7: Confidentiality

**7.1 Obligations.** Each Party will: (a) keep the other Party's Confidential Information confidential using at least the same care used for its own confidential information (but not less than reasonable care); (b) use Confidential Information only for purposes of this Agreement; and (c) disclose Confidential Information only to those who need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

**7.2 Exclusions.** Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that is publicly known without breach, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party.

**7.3 Duration.** Confidentiality obligations survive termination for three (3) years (indefinitely for trade secrets).

---

## Section 8: Data Processing and Privacy

**8.1 DPA.** The parties shall execute NovaTech's standard Data Processing Addendum (Exhibit D), which governs the processing of personal data in connection with this Agreement.

**8.2 GDPR/CCPA.** NovaTech acts as a data processor with respect to Customer Data. Customer acts as the data controller.

**8.3 Security Incident Notification.** NovaTech will notify Customer within 72 hours of becoming aware of a confirmed security incident affecting Customer Data.

---

## Section 9: Term and Termination

**9.1 Agreement Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for the Subscription Term specified in the Order Form, unless earlier terminated.

**9.2 Renewal Notice.** Customer must provide written notice of intent to renew no later than **90 days** prior to the Subscription Term end date. NovaTech will send a renewal proposal 120 days prior to expiration.

**9.3 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon 30 days' written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within the notice period, or upon insolvency or bankruptcy.

**9.4 Termination for Convenience.** Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon 60 days' written notice; no refund is provided for unused Subscription Term unless otherwise specified.

**9.5 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination: all licenses terminate; Customer must cease use; Customer Data is available for 60 days then permanently deleted; each Party returns or destroys Confidential Information.

---

## Section 10: Refunds

**10.1 Standard Refund Window.** Customer may request a full refund within **30 days of the invoice date** for any reason.

**10.2 Enterprise Refund Eligibility.** Enterprise customers may additionally request a refund within 30 days of invoice if: (a) a Critical Feature is unavailable for more than 72 continuous hours; (b) NovaTech fails to meet the SLA uptime commitment (99.9% monthly); or (c) the Platform materially fails to meet the contracted specifications in Exhibit A. See NovaTech's Refund and Cancellation Policy (novatech.io/legal/refund-policy) for full details.

**10.3 Prorated Refunds.** Approved refunds after the initial 30-day window are prorated based on unused complete months remaining in the Subscription Term.

**10.4 Non-Refundable Items.** Implementation fees, custom development fees, and training fees are non-refundable once work has commenced.

**10.5 Refund Approval.** Refunds exceeding $10,000 require approval by NovaTech's VP Finance. Refunds exceeding $50,000 require additional CEO approval.

**10.6 Payment Current Requirement.** Customer must be current on all payment obligations to be eligible for a refund. NovaTech may offset approved refunds against outstanding balances.

## Section 12: Limitation of Liability

**12.1 Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, LOSS OF DATA, OR LOSS OF GOODWILL.

**12.2 Liability Cap.** EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY SHALL NOT EXCEED THE **SUBSCRIPTION FEES PAID IN THE 12 MONTHS** PRECEDING THE CLAIM, SPECIFICALLY [CONTRACT_VALUE] OR THE ACTUAL AMOUNT PAID IN SUCH PERIOD, WHICHEVER IS LESS.

**12.3 Exceptions.** The cap and exclusions in 12.1–12.2 do not apply to: (a) indemnification for IP infringement; (b) gross negligence or willful misconduct; (c) breach of confidentiality obligations; or (d) Customer's payment obligations.

## Section 13: Indemnification

**13.1 NovaTech.** NovaTech will defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims that the Platform infringes a U.S. patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret.

**13.2 Customer.** Customer will defend and indemnify NovaTech against third-party claims arising from Customer's breach of this Agreement, unauthorized use of the Platform, or Customer Data.

**13.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party must promptly notify, grant sole defense control, and cooperate with the indemnifying Party.

**14.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of California**, without regard to conflict of laws principles.
