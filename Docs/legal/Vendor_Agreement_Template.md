## Section 1: Services and Scope

**1.1 Services.** Vendor shall provide NovaTech with the professional services described in one or more Statements of Work (each, an "SOW") executed by the Parties pursuant to this Agreement (collectively, the "Services"). Each SOW shall be governed by the terms of this Agreement and shall specify:

(a) A detailed description of the Services to be performed;
(b) The deliverables to be produced by Vendor (the "Deliverables");
(c) The schedule and milestones for delivery;
(d) Acceptance criteria for each Deliverable;
(e) The fees and payment schedule;
(f) The Vendor personnel assigned to perform the Services;
(g) Any special terms applicable to the specific engagement.

**1.2 Changes to Scope.** Either Party may request changes to the scope of Services or any SOW. All changes must be documented in a written Change Order executed by both Parties before Vendor proceeds with the changed work. Verbal change orders or informal email approvals are not binding on NovaTech. NovaTech is not obligated to pay for out-of-scope work performed without a written Change Order.

**1.3 Vendor Personnel.** Vendor shall provide personnel with the appropriate skills, experience, and qualifications to perform the Services. The key Vendor personnel for this engagement are:

- **Vendor Project Lead:** [VENDOR_PROJECT_LEAD_NAME], [TITLE], [EMAIL]
- **NovaTech Project Manager:** [NOVATECH_PM_NAME], [TITLE], [EMAIL]

Vendor shall notify NovaTech at least 15 days in advance of any material change in personnel assigned to Services. NovaTech may, in its reasonable discretion, request replacement of Vendor personnel whose performance is unsatisfactory.

**1.4 Subcontractors.** Vendor shall not subcontract any portion of the Services without NovaTech's prior written consent. If NovaTech consents to subcontracting, Vendor shall ensure that its subcontractors are bound by obligations no less protective than those set forth in this Agreement. Vendor remains fully responsible for the performance of all subcontractors.

## Section 2: Fees and Payment Schedule

**2.1 Fees.** NovaTech shall pay Vendor the fees set forth in the applicable SOW. The total estimated fees for this Agreement are: **[TOTAL_ESTIMATED_VALUE]**.

**2.2 Fee Structures.** Vendor services may be structured as:

- **Fixed-Fee:** A flat amount for a defined set of deliverables and scope, as specified in the SOW. Fixed fees are not subject to increase unless a Change Order is executed.
- **Time and Materials (T&M):** Invoiced based on hours worked at the agreed hourly rates set forth in the SOW. Vendor must obtain NovaTech approval before incurring hours that would exceed the T&M budget cap by more than 10%.
- **Milestone-Based:** Fees tied to the achievement of specific milestones, as defined in the SOW. Payment is released upon NovaTech's written acceptance of the milestone deliverable.

**2.3 Invoicing and Payment Terms.**

- Vendor shall submit invoices to NovaTech's Accounts Payable team at ap@novatech.io
- Invoices must reference the applicable SOW number and PO number issued by NovaTech
- Payment Terms: **Net-30** from the date of a valid, undisputed invoice
- For milestone-based payments, NovaTech's acceptance of the milestone deliverable triggers the Net-30 payment clock

**2.4 Disputed Invoices.** NovaTech may dispute any invoice in good faith by providing written notice to Vendor within 15 days of receipt, specifying the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute. NovaTech shall pay the undisputed portion of the invoice within the normal payment timeline. The Parties shall resolve disputed amounts through good-faith negotiation.

**2.5 Expenses.** Vendor shall not incur reimbursable expenses on NovaTech's behalf without prior written approval. Approved expenses must comply with NovaTech's Expense and Travel Policy (FIN-POL-003). Expenses must be documented with receipts and submitted within 30 days of incurrence.

**2.6 Maximum NovaTech Obligation.** NovaTech's total payment obligation under this Agreement shall not exceed the aggregate fees specified in executed SOWs plus pre-approved Change Orders. NovaTech is not obligated to pay amounts in excess of approved not-to-exceed (NTE) limits.

## Section 3: Term and Termination

**3.1 Agreement Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated in accordance with this Section 3, unless an SOW specifies a different project term.

**3.2 Termination for Convenience.** NovaTech may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience upon **30 days' written notice** to Vendor. Upon such termination, NovaTech's sole obligation is to pay Vendor for Services actually performed and accepted (and non-cancellable costs reasonably incurred) prior to the termination effective date, prorated to the termination date for time-based engagements.

**3.3 Termination for Cause by NovaTech.** NovaTech may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for cause, effective immediately upon written notice, if Vendor:

(a) Materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 15 days of receiving written notice;
(b) Commits fraud, gross negligence, or willful misconduct in connection with the Services;
(c) Becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or is subject to bankruptcy or receivership proceedings;
(d) Violates applicable law in connection with the performance of Services;
(e) Materially misrepresents its qualifications, personnel, or ability to perform the Services.

**3.4 Termination for Cause by Vendor.** Vendor may terminate this Agreement for cause upon 30 days' written notice if NovaTech materially breaches its payment obligations (for undisputed amounts) and fails to cure within the notice period.

**3.5 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination:
- Vendor shall immediately cease performing Services
- Each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information
- Vendor shall deliver to NovaTech all work in progress and partially completed Deliverables, which NovaTech shall own pursuant to Section 4
- NovaTech shall pay for Services performed through the termination date (for convenience termination), reduced by any damages NovaTech has suffered (for cause termination)

## Section 4: Intellectual Property Ownership

**4.1 Work for Hire.** All Deliverables, work product, inventions, discoveries, improvements, software, documentation, reports, analyses, and other materials created by Vendor in the course of performing the Services (collectively, "Work Product") are considered "works made for hire" for NovaTech within the meaning of the U.S. Copyright Act, to the maximum extent permitted by law. All Work Product is the sole and exclusive property of NovaTech upon creation.

**4.2 Assignment.** To the extent any Work Product does not qualify as a "work made for hire" under applicable law, Vendor hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to NovaTech all right, title, and interest in and to such Work Product, including all patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, and other intellectual property rights therein, throughout the world.

**4.3 Assistance.** Vendor shall execute all documents and take all actions reasonably requested by NovaTech to effectuate, record, or perfect the assignment of Work Product, including patent applications, copyright registrations, and similar filings.

**4.4 Vendor's Pre-existing IP.** Vendor may incorporate pre-existing tools, methodologies, frameworks, or IP that Vendor developed independently prior to the Effective Date ("Pre-existing IP") into Deliverables, provided that: (a) such Pre-existing IP is disclosed in writing to NovaTech before use; (b) Vendor has the right to grant NovaTech the license described below; and (c) use of Pre-existing IP does not violate any third-party rights. Vendor grants NovaTech a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use, modify, and distribute Pre-existing IP embedded in Deliverables, solely to the extent necessary to use and enjoy the Deliverables.

**4.5 Open Source.** Vendor shall not incorporate any open source software into Deliverables without NovaTech's prior written consent. If NovaTech consents, Vendor must provide a complete list of all open source components used, including version numbers and license terms.

**4.6 No Additional Compensation.** Vendor acknowledges that no additional compensation is owed in connection with the IP assignment in this Section 4; the Fees set forth in the SOW constitute full consideration for all Work Product and IP rights assigned hereunder.

## Section 5: Acceptance Criteria

**5.1 Acceptance Process.** NovaTech shall have **15 business days** following delivery of each Deliverable to either: (a) accept the Deliverable in writing; or (b) reject the Deliverable in writing, specifying in reasonable detail the deficiencies.

**5.2 Rejection and Remedy.** Upon rejection, Vendor shall have **10 business days** to cure the identified deficiencies and re-deliver the Deliverable. NovaTech shall then have another 10 business days to accept or reject the re-delivered Deliverable.

**5.3 Deemed Acceptance.** If NovaTech does not provide written acceptance or rejection within the applicable acceptance period, the Deliverable shall be deemed accepted.

**5.4 Ongoing Warranty.** Following acceptance, Vendor warrants that each Deliverable will conform to the agreed specifications and be free from material defects for a period of **90 days** (the "Warranty Period"). Vendor shall promptly correct any defects discovered during the Warranty Period at no additional charge.

## Section 6: Confidentiality

**6.1 Obligations.** Each Party shall: (a) keep the other Party's Confidential Information (as defined below) in strict confidence; (b) use Confidential Information only for purposes of this Agreement; and (c) disclose Confidential Information only to those with a need to know who are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations.

**6.2 Definition.** "Confidential Information" includes all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including technical, business, financial, and customer information.

**6.3 Exclusions.** Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no breach of this Agreement, was independently developed, or was rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

**6.4 Duration.** Confidentiality obligations survive for **3 years** following termination of this Agreement, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

**6.5 NDA.** If the Parties have executed a separate Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), the terms of the NDA are incorporated herein by reference and continue in full force and effect, supplementing (not superseding) this Section 6.

## Section 7: Data Security Requirements

**7.1 Security Standards.** If Vendor will have access to NovaTech systems, networks, or any NovaTech or customer data ("NovaTech Data") in the performance of Services, Vendor must:

(a) Maintain an information security program with administrative, technical, and physical safeguards at least equivalent to **SOC 2 Type II** requirements or **ISO 27001** standards (or demonstrate equivalent controls in NovaTech's Vendor Security Questionnaire);

(b) Encrypt all NovaTech Data at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+);

(c) Implement access controls ensuring only authorized Vendor personnel access NovaTech Data on a need-to-know basis;

(d) Not store NovaTech Data on portable devices (laptops, USB drives) unless encrypted;

(e) Complete NovaTech's Vendor Security Questionnaire (VSQ) before commencing Services and annually thereafter.

**7.2 Incident Notification.** If Vendor becomes aware of a confirmed security incident affecting NovaTech Data, Vendor shall notify NovaTech's security team (security@novatech.io) within **24 hours** of discovery and cooperate fully with NovaTech's incident response.

**7.3 DPA.** If Vendor will process personal data of NovaTech's employees or customers, Vendor must execute NovaTech's standard Data Processing Addendum (LEGAL-TMPL-DPA-001) before commencing Services.

**7.4 Return of Data.** Upon termination of this Agreement, Vendor shall return or securely destroy all NovaTech Data within 10 business days and certify in writing that such return or destruction has been completed.

## Section 8: Insurance Requirements

Vendor shall maintain, at its own expense, the following insurance coverage throughout the term of this Agreement, and shall provide NovaTech with certificates of insurance upon request:

| Insurance Type | Minimum Coverage |
|---|---|
| Commercial General Liability | $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate |
| Professional Liability (E&O) | $2,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate |

| Workers' Compensation | Statutory limits as required by applicable law |
| Employer's Liability | $1,000,000 each accident |
| Commercial Automobile | $1,000,000 combined single limit (if use of vehicle required) |

**Notice of Cancellation:** Vendor's insurer shall provide NovaTech with at least 30 days' prior written notice of policy cancellation or material change.

## Section 9: Indemnification

**9.1 Vendor Indemnification.** Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless NovaTech and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from and against any claims, damages, losses, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to:

(a) Vendor's breach of this Agreement or any representation, warranty, or covenant;
(b) Vendor's negligence or willful misconduct;
(c) Any claim that the Services or Deliverables infringe a third party's intellectual property rights;
(d) Vendor's violation of applicable law;
(e) Any claims by Vendor's employees or subcontractors arising from their engagement on the Services (including wage/hour and workers' compensation claims).

**9.2 NovaTech Indemnification.** NovaTech shall defend and indemnify Vendor against third-party claims arising directly from NovaTech's gross negligence or willful misconduct, to the extent not caused by Vendor's acts or omissions.

## Section 10: Warranties

**10.1 Vendor Warranties.** Vendor represents and warrants that:

(a) Vendor has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement;
(b) The Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards;
(c) All Deliverables will conform to the specifications and acceptance criteria set forth in the applicable SOW;
(d) Vendor has, and will maintain, all licenses, permits, and authorizations necessary to perform the Services;
(e) The Services and Deliverables will not infringe or misappropriate any third-party intellectual property rights;
(f) Vendor will comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and NovaTech's policies in performing the Services;
(g) Vendor personnel assigned to this engagement have the qualifications represented to NovaTech.

## Section 11: Limitation of Liability

**11.1** NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS OR LOSS OF DATA, ARISING FROM OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**11.2** EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID BY NOVATECH TO VENDOR IN THE TWELVE MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM, SPECIFICALLY **[CONTRACT_VALUE]** OR THE ACTUAL AMOUNT PAID IN SUCH PERIOD, WHICHEVER IS LESS.

**11.3** The limitations in 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to: (a) Vendor's indemnification obligations under Section 9; (b) either Party's liability for fraud or willful misconduct; (c) Vendor's breach of confidentiality obligations; or (d) Vendor's breach of IP assignment obligations (Section 4).

## Section 12: Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

**12.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of California**, without regard to conflict of law principles.

**12.2 Dispute Resolution.** Disputes shall first be addressed through escalation to senior management of each Party. If not resolved within 30 days, either Party may pursue binding arbitration under AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules, in San Francisco, California.

**12.3 Injunctive Relief.** Either Party may seek injunctive relief in a court of competent jurisdiction for breach of Section 4 (IP) or Section 6 (Confidentiality) without first pursuing arbitration.

## Section 13: General Provisions

**13.1 Independent Contractor.** Vendor is an independent contractor, not an employee, agent, or partner of NovaTech. Vendor is responsible for all taxes, withholdings, and employee benefits for its personnel.

**13.2 No Exclusivity.** This Agreement does not create an exclusive relationship. NovaTech may engage other vendors for similar services; Vendor may perform similar services for other clients (subject to confidentiality obligations).

**13.3 Non-Solicitation.** During the term of this Agreement and for 12 months following termination, Vendor shall not directly solicit or hire any NovaTech employee or contractor, without NovaTech's prior written consent.

**13.4 Entire Agreement.** This Agreement (together with all SOWs and Change Orders) constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter.

**13.5 Amendment.** Amendments require written agreement by both Parties.

**13.6 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts; electronic signatures are accepted.
